**Alex Turner** is the lead writer and editor at DungeonPath.

Alex has been playing and writing about games for years, with a focus on Roblox titles, mobile RPGs, and co-op survival games. Their guides come from hours of actual play: working through each game's progression, testing strategies, and keeping track of how updates change the meta. When a patch lands, Alex plays it before writing about it.

Every guide on this site is maintained — when a game updates, the affected guides get revisited and refreshed. If you spot something outdated or incorrect, [contact us](/contact/) and it goes on the fix list.

**Coverage:** Roblox (Fisch, Pressure, RIVALS, DOORS, Tower Defense Simulator, Blox Fruits, Pet Simulator 99, Sailor Piece, Blade Ball), mobile (Monster Legends, My Singing Monsters), PC (Palworld, Lethal Company).
