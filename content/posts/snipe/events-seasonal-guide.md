## SNIPE Event Types

SNIPE has three categories of limited-time content:

| Event Type | Duration | Rewards | Frequency |
|-----------|----------|---------|-----------|
| **Seasonal** | 2-3 weeks | Exclusive skins, wraps, charms, codes | 4x per year |
| **Update/Milestone** | 1-2 weeks | Credits, XP boosts, exclusive titles | Every 4-6 weeks |
| **Weekend Mini-Event** | 48-72 hours | Double XP, bonus Credits, small codes | ~Monthly |
