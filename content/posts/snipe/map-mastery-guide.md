## The Same Loadout on Every Map Is Costing You Kills

Bridge: your Heavy Sniper dominates. Long sightlines, clear cover positions, enemies visible from 100 meters. You feel unstoppable.

Warehouse: you equip the same Heavy Sniper. An enemy rounds the corner at 8 meters with an SMG. You can't scope before dying. You respawn. Die again. End the match with 3 kills and 16 deaths. You think you played badly. You had the wrong weapon for the map.

SNIPE has 7 maps. Each demands different engagement distances, different weapons, and different positioning. Here's what works on each.

---

## Bridge

**Best weapon:** Heavy Sniper. The longest, straightest sightlines in SNIPE.
**Power positions:** Bridge pillars. Under-bridge pathway. Control rooms at both ends.
**Never:** Stand in the open bridge center — silhouetted against the sky, exposed from both ends.

---

## Warehouse

**Best weapon:** Tactical SMG or Shotgun. Engagement range: 5-20 meters.
**Power positions:** Central catwalk. Container stacks (flank routes). Back corridor.
**Never:** Walk the open warehouse floor — no cover, shot from 3 angles.

---

## Rooftops

**Best weapon:** Heavy Sniper. Vertical angles favor scoped weapons.
**Power positions:** AC unit corner. Central rooftop. Lower balconies.
**Never:** Take the stairs — camped. Stand silhouetted against the sky.

---

## Construction

**Best weapon:** AR or Semi-Auto Sniper. Mixed ranges need versatility.
**Power positions:** Top floor. East scaffolding. Ground-floor garage.
**Never:** Stand near windows (silhouetted). Stay on one floor for more than 20 seconds.

---

## Downtown

**Best weapon:** AR or Tactical SMG. Street fighting with alley routes.
**Power positions:** Alley dumpster. Rooftop access. Street corner.
**Never:** Stand in the open plaza — crossfire from 4 angles.

---

## Arena

**Best weapon:** AR. Open center + cover pillars.
**Power positions:** Center platform. Perimeter pillars. Elevated ramp.
**Never:** Stand on the open arena floor — 360° exposure.

---

## Hangar

**Best weapon:** Heavy Sniper. Long sightlines down the hangar bay.
**Power positions:** Back hangar. Plane wreckage. Side catwalks.
**Never:** Stand in the open hangar bay — no cover, entire map sees you.

## The Map-Specific Weapon Swap

Don't lock one weapon for all 7 maps. You need TWO weapons total: one long-range (Heavy Sniper) for Bridge, Rooftops, Hangar, and one close-range (Tactical SMG) for Warehouse, Downtown. Construction and Arena work with either.

**The 2-weapon minimum:** Master the Heavy Sniper AND the Tactical SMG. Switch based on the map loading screen. You see Bridge → equip Sniper. You see Warehouse → equip SMG. This single habit — swapping weapons based on map — adds 3-5 kills per match for the average player.

**The "but I'm a sniper main" trap:** Being a sniper main means you're great at maps where snipers work and terrible at maps where they don't. You're not a sniper main. You're a player who hasn't spent 5 hours practicing the SMG. Spend the 5 hours. Your Warehouse and Downtown stats will double.
