## Mistake #1: Building Your First Base at Spawn

The starting area looks safe and convenient. It's actually the worst base location on the map.

| Factor | Spawn Area | Fort Ruins Plateau (200, -350) |
|--------|-----------|-------------------------------|
| Ore nodes | 0-1 | 5-8 |
| Flat buildable land | Small patches | Large plateau |
| Natural defenses | None | Cliff walls on 3 sides |
| Fast travel proximity | Far from boss towers | Near multiple fast travel points |
| Raid vulnerability | High — exposed | Low — natural chokepoints |

**Fix:** Use the spawn base temporarily (levels 1-10). As soon as you have a flying mount, scout the Fort Ruins plateau and move your base. The Palbox relocation cost is minimal.

## Mistake #2: Ignoring Work Suitability Traits

Catching Pals with good combat stats but terrible work traits = a base that produces nothing efficiently.

| Trait | Effect | Priority |
|-------|--------|----------|
| Artisan | +50% Work Speed | **#1** — catch these immediately |
| Serious | +20% Work Speed | **#2** |
| Work Slave | +30% Work Speed, -30% Attack | **#3** — negative doesn't matter for base Pals |
| Lucky | +15% Work Speed, +15% Attack | #4 |
| Slacker | -30% Work Speed | **Kill or sell — never assign to base** |

**Fix:** When catching Pals for your base, check traits before assigning. A level 5 Lamball with Artisan outproduces a level 20 Lamball with no traits. Breed Artisan onto your core base Pals as soon as you unlock the Breeding Farm.

## Mistake #3: Using the Wrong Pals for Each Job

Assigning Pals to jobs they don't have suitability for = they do them at 1% efficiency.

| Job | Required Suitability | Best Early Pal | Best Mid-Game Pal |
|-----|---------------------|---------------|-------------------|
| Mining | Mining 3+ | Rushoar, Tombat | Anubis, Astegon |
| Kindling | Kindling 3+ | Foxparks | Jormuntide Ignis, Blazehowl |
| Watering | Watering 3+ | Penking | Jormuntide, Azurobe |
| Planting/Gathering | Planting 3+ | Lifmunk, Tanzee | Lyleen, Frostallion |
| Handiwork | Handiwork 3+ | Lamball (low) | Anubis (best), Lunaris |
| Transporting | Transporting 3+ | Cattiva, Killamari | Helzephyr, Wumpo |

**Fix:** Assign Pals with suitability level 3+ to their matching jobs. Level 1 suitability is barely functional. Use the Monitoring Stand to manually assign Pals rather than letting the game auto-assign.

## Mistake #4: Neglecting the Breeding Farm

Most beginners ignore breeding until level 30+. The Breeding Farm unlocks at level 19 and is the single most powerful progression tool.

| What Breeding Gives You | Without Breeding | With Breeding |
|------------------------|----------------|---------------|
| Anubis (best Handiwork 4, Mining 3) | Level 40+ wild catch | Level 19: breed Bushi + Penking |
| Artisan/Serious base Pals | Random wild catches | Controlled breeding with desired traits |
| Perfect combat Pals | Rare wild catches | Breed specific traits onto any species |

**Fix:** Unlock the Breeding Farm at level 19. First breed: Bushi + Penking = Anubis (sets up your entire base efficiency). Second breed: your two best combat Pals to pass traits to offspring.

## Mistake #5: Carrying All Your Best Items

You die, drop everything, and can't recover your corpse. Hours of resources gone.

**Fix:** Store valuable items (ingots, rare Pal souls, skill fruits, schematics) in a chest before any dangerous activity — boss fights, dungeon runs, exploring new high-level areas. Only carry what you need for the specific task plus emergency food and Pal Spheres.

## Mistake #6: Sleeping on Pal Food Buffs

Raw Berries keep Pals alive, but they work slow and get depressed. Food buffs are multiplicative with work traits.

| Food | Buff | Best For |
|------|------|----------|
| Jam-Filled Bun | 60 nutrition, no buff but efficient | General base feeding |
| Salad | Restores 10 SAN | Preventing depression |
| Fried Chikipi | +15% Work Speed (8 min) | Burst production periods |
| Mammorest Curry | +25% Attack (10 min) | Boss fights |

**Fix:** Replace raw Berries with Jam-Filled Buns as your base food source the moment you unlock a Mill and Wheat Plantation. One Wheat + 2 Berry plots feeds 15 Pals.

## Mistake #7: Fighting Bosses Underleveled

Beginners see a boss tower, walk in at level 15 with 3 random Pals, and die instantly. Bosses don't scale — they have fixed levels.

| Boss | Recommended Level | Recommended Pals |
|------|-----------------|------------------|
| Zoe & Grizzbolt | 15+ | 3 Ground-type Pals at level 15+ |
| Lily & Lyleen | 25+ | 4 Fire-type Pals at level 25+ |
| Orserk | 40+ | Full team of 5 at level 40+ with Ice/Ground types |

**Fix:** Check the boss level before entering. Bring a full team of Pals 2-3 levels above the boss, all with type advantage. Bring 10+ combat food items.

## Mistake #8: Ignoring the Pal Condenser

The Pal Condenser upgrades a Pal's Partner Skill by sacrificing Pals of the same species. A max-condensed Pal has 20-40% stronger Partner Skills.

**Fix:** As you catch duplicate Pals, feed them to the Condenser to power up your main Pal's Partner Skill. Prioritize condensing your main mount (speed boost) and your main combat Pal (damage boost).

---

## Mistake #9: Sleeping Through Nights

Skipping night by sleeping wastes the best catching opportunity. Many rare Pals (Lunaris, Tombat, Felbat) are nocturnal and ONLY spawn at night.

**Fix:** Spend nights hunting nocturnal Pals for the 10-catch XP bonus. Craft a Torch or use a Foxparks as a light source. Nighttime XP from rare Pal catches often exceeds daytime grinding.

---

## Mistake #10: Not Using Fast Travel

Walking everywhere in Palworld wastes enormous time. Beginners don't activate fast travel points as they explore.

**Fix:** Activate EVERY fast travel eagle statue you see — even if you're just passing through. The map is huge and you'll need to revisit every region. A 30-second detour to activate a statue saves 10+ minutes of flying later.
