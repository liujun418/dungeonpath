## Why Food Matters in Palworld

Food isn't just a hunger mechanic in Palworld — it's a buff system, a work efficiency multiplier, and a breeding enabler. Pals with low hunger lose SAN (Sanity), which causes them to work slower, get sick, and eventually refuse to work entirely. Good food management is the difference between a base that runs itself and one that needs constant babysitting.

---

## S-Tier Foods: Must-Craft

| Food | Ingredients | Effect | Best For |
|------|-----------|--------|----------|
| **Mammorest Curry** | Mammorest Meat + Spices + Rice | +25% Attack (10 min) | Boss fights, tower battles |
| **Mozzarina Hamburger** | Mozzarina Meat + Milk + Flour | +20% Defense, +15% Attack (10 min) | All-around combat |
| **Jam-Filled Bun** | 2 Berries + 1 Flour | Large nutrition, cheap | Base worker feeding |
| **Cake** | Flour + Red Berries + Milk + Egg + Honey | Required for breeding | Breeding Farm |

### Jam-Filled Bun — The Base Economy Backbone

One Jam-Filled Bun gives ~60 nutrition (vs ~15 from raw Berries). A worker Pal eats one Bun every 10-15 minutes, meaning 4-6 Buns per Pal per hour. For a 10-Pal base, you need roughly 60 Buns per hour. Two Berry Plantations and one Wheat Plantation with a Mill produce enough for 15-20 Pals with surplus.

## A-Tier: Strong Specialty Foods

| Food | Ingredients | Effect | Best For |
|------|-----------|--------|----------|
| **Salad** | Lettuce + Tomato | Restores 10 SAN | Breeding Pals, depressed Pals |
| **Eikthyrdeer Stew** | Eikthyrdeer Venison + Mushrooms | +10% Attack (8 min) | Early-game combat |
| **Fried Chikipi** | Chikipi Poultry + Oil | +15% Work Speed (8 min) | Base workers (short boost) |
| **Pancake** | Flour + Milk | +10% Movement Speed (5 min) | Exploration, traveling |

### Salad — The SAN Manager

Pals lose SAN when they work, get hungry, or take damage. Low SAN causes depression, fractures, and eventually "Incapacitated" status. Salad restores SAN directly rather than just feeding — it's the only food that actively fixes mental state. Keep 20-30 Salads in reserve for emergencies when multiple Pals get sick simultaneously.

## Pal Feeding Automation

### Feed Box Management

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| Put food in the FIRST slot of the Feed Box | Pals eat from slot 1 first |
| Fill slot 1 with Jam-Filled Buns | Best nutrition-per-ingredient for workers |
| Put 10-20 combat foods in slot 2 | Pals grab these when slot 1 is empty |
| Check Feed Box daily | Hunger drain is constant even when you're offline |
| Remove raw ingredients | If a Pal puts raw Berries in, move them out |

### Pal Diet by Role

| Pal Role | Best Food | Why |
|----------|-----------|-----|
| Base worker (farming, mining, crafting) | Jam-Filled Bun | Cheap, high nutrition, minimal downtime |
| Base worker with low SAN | Salad | Restores SAN while feeding |
| Combat Pal (bossing) | Mammorest Curry | Max Attack buff |
| Combat Pal (tanking) | Mozzarina Hamburger | Balanced Attack + Defense |
| Mount (exploration) | Pancake | Movement Speed for faster travel |
| Breeding Pal | Salad + Jam-Filled Bun | SAN restoration + basic feeding |

## Food Stacking & Buff Mechanics

- **Combat buffs don't stack.** Eating a second Mammorest Curry overwrites the first — the timer resets, the buff doesn't double.
- **Different food types do stack.** +Attack from Curry + +Defense from Hamburger = both active. This is the endgame combat food combo.
- **Work speed buffs are multiplicative** with Pal passives (Artisan, Serious, etc). A Pal with Artisan (+50% Work Speed) eating Fried Chikipi (+15% Work Speed) works at 165% base speed, not 150%.
