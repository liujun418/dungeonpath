## Races Overview

Blox Fruits features 7 playable races, each with four upgrade versions (V1 through V4). Your starting race is randomly assigned among Human, Rabbit, Shark, and Angel. Ghoul, Cyborg, and Draco must be unlocked through quests.

Each race offers unique abilities that dramatically impact your combat style, mobility, and survivability. V4 (Race Awakening) is the endgame upgrade system, unlocking powerful transformations and gear choices.

### V3 — Arowe (Second Sea)

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| Level | 1,000+ |
| Prerequisite | V2 unlocked, defeat Don Swan, unlock his door |
| NPC | Arowe (hidden entrance in Diamond's plateau) |
| Cost | 2,000,000 Beli |

**V3 Quests by Race**:

| Race | V3 Quest |
|------|----------|
| Human | Kill Diamond, Jeremy, and Fajita |
| Rabbit | Collect 30 chests anywhere in Second Sea |
| Shark | Kill a naturally spawned Sea Beast |
| Angel | Kill another player who also has Angel race |
| Ghoul | Kill 5 players in Second Sea |
| Cyborg | Show any physical fruit to Arowe |
| Draco | Defeat a Terrorshark + Horned Man quest (3,000,000 Beli) |

### V4 — Temple of Time (Third Sea)

**Prerequisites**:
1. Race V3 unlocked
2. Defeat rip_indra (Raid Boss)
3. Defeat Dough King (get Mirror Fractal)
4. Find Blue Gear on Mirage Island (nighttime only — use race ability while staring at the moon)

**Awakening Process**:
1. Go to the Temple of Time and pull the lever
2. Gather 2 other players with different races
3. Activate all three race abilities together during a Full Moon
4. Complete the race trial (defeat waves of enemies)
5. Place your gear in the Ancient Clock

**Cost**: ~26,750 Fragments per full race awakening. Total for all 6 base races: 160,500 Fragments.

**Controls**: Press **T** for V3 ability, **Y** for V4 transformation.

## Detailed Race Ability Breakdowns

### Human — The Damage Dealer

- **V1**: No buffs
- **V2**: +Movement speed, +Instinct dodge regen, +Dash distance, +Flash Step range
- **V3 — Last Resort**: Damage increases as HP drops — high-risk, high-reward for clutch PvP
- **V4 Gear: Limit Break**: Rage meter builds during combat, uncapped to 150% at Tier 2, progressively boosting all damage
- **V4 Gear: Psycho**: Flash Step gains 3 charges with temporary invisibility and speed; Tier 2 enhances charge regen and dash distance
- **Best for**: Aggressive players chasing maximum damage output. Pairs well with combo-heavy fruits like Dough and Spirit.

---

### Rabbit (Mink) — The Speed Demon

- **V1**: +1.5x movement speed
- **V2**: +2x speed, +Dash distance, reduced dash energy cost
- **V3 — Agility**: +4x movement speed, massively reduced dash cooldown
- **V4 Gear: Whirlwind (Blue)**: Dashes leave damaging tornadoes that trap enemies
- **V4 Gear: Lightning Cloak (Red)**: Dashes become significantly longer with electricity; Tier 2 adds Super Dash by holding the dash button
- **Best for**: Hit-and-run tactics, kiting, escaping unfavorable fights. Weakest V4 in raw combat — relies on outmaneuvering opponents.

---

### Shark — The Tank

- **V1**: Reduced water damage as a fruit user
- **V2**: Zero water damage, faster swimming
- **V3 — Water Body**: Reduces all incoming damage by 65–70% temporarily
- **V4 Gear: Leviathan's Armour**: Water shield regenerates when dealing damage, absorbs ~85% of incoming damage
- **V4 Gear: Whirlpool**: Hits apply a water debuff that slows enemies and reduces their defense, stacking with consecutive attacks
- **Best for**: PvP tanking. Nearly unkillable in water. S-Tier V4 for both beginners and veterans. Lacks land mobility.

---

### Angel — The Healer

- **V1**: +Extra air jumps
- **V2**: +More air jumps, reduced air jump energy cost
- **V3 — Heavenly Blood**: Heals 14–20% HP, +15% defense, regenerates energy — low cooldown
- **V4 Gear: Prince of the Skies**: Hold dash to glide, hold jump to fly freely; Tier 2 greatly enhances flight
- **V4 Gear: King's Rule**: Light aura that slows enemies, deals damage over time, drains energy, and distorts the enemy's screen
- **Best for**: Sky camping and healing. Excellent with Portal fruit. King's Rule can stun-lock enemies in combos.

---

### Ghoul — The All-Rounder

- **V1**: +30% movement speed at night
- **V2**: Life Leech — heal 25% of all fighting style damage dealt
- **V3 — Heightened Senses**: +Damage, +Speed, +Defense, and 40% cooldown reduction for 8 seconds
- **V4 Gear: Domain Expansion**: Dark field inflicting slowness, health regen negation, and blindness — night passives active during day
- **V4 Gear: Blood Siphon**: Life leech works on all attacks — ~10% on players, ~5% on NPCs; Tier 2 raises player leech to ~15%
- **Best for**: Aggressive sustained combat. Pairs perfectly with Sanguine Art. Arguably the best overall V4 for the cooldown reduction and life leech combination.

---

### Cyborg — The Instinct Breaker

- **V1**: No buffs
- **V2**: +Defense vs melee/sword/gun, converts 15% of damage taken into energy
- **V3 — Energy Core**: AoE field with tick damage, +30% defense, breaks enemy Instinct (Observation Haki)
- **V4 Gear: Energy Control**: Damage chains to nearby enemies via electric orbs; Super Jump and Super Dash unlocked
- **V4 Gear: Aftershock**: Attacks apply an electrifying effect that disables enemy Instinct
- **Best for**: PvP dominance. The electric DoT prevents enemies from charging moves. S-Tier in competitive PvP.

---

### Draco — The Dragon (Newest)

- **V1**: +5% Health, +5% Melee Damage, +5% Damage Reduction — cosmetic: small horns
- **V2**: +Speed, +Dash, Fiery Flight (~5 seconds air time), Flash Step causes burn/explosion damage
- **V3 — Primordial Reign**: Buffs you and allies by 15% damage/speed, debuffs enemies 15% speed, cancels enemy skills in range — 10s duration
- **V4 Gear: Cometic Field**: Hits attach explosive orbs; 3–4 stacks detonate for massive damage (~5K+), breaks Instinct; allies also apply orbs. Damage scales with bounty
- **V4 Gear: Thorn Mail**: Reflects ~45% of incoming damage back at attackers — reflected damage breaks Instinct
- **Best for**: Late-game powerhouse. Team support combined with massive DPS. Can 3-shot high-bounty players. S-Tier alongside Shark.

## Failure Analysis — Common Race Mistakes

**Mistake #1: Chasing S-tier races before reaching Third Sea.** New players who spend thousands of Fragments rerolling for Draco or Cyborg before unlocking V3 handicap their own progression. Starter races (Human, Shark, Rabbit, Angel) have perfectly viable V2 and V3 abilities that carry you through Second Sea content. Switch only after you have access to V4 — the S-tier ranking means nothing without the awakening to back it up.

**Mistake #2: Ignoring race-fruit synergy when picking gear.** Running a close-range fruit like Dough or Buddha with Rabbit's speed-focused Whirlwind gear wastes its combo potential. Likewise, equipping Shark's Whirlpool on a sniper build negates its stacking debuff advantage. Your V4 gear should amplify your fruit's engagement range, not fight against it.

**Mistake #3: Fragment bankruptcy from premature awakening.** Full V4 awakening runs ~26,750 Fragments per race. Players often unlock V4 on two or three races simultaneously, leaving themselves short for stat refills, fruit rerolls, or the next update's race rebalance. Commit to one race's full awakening before diversifying.

**Mistake #4: Solo queueing the Temple of Time trial.** The V4 trial requires three players of different races. Trying to coordinate with randoms without voice chat or a pre-agreed signal is the single biggest cause of repeat failures. Join a Discord group or use the in-game chat to confirm roles before pulling the lever.

## Decision Framework — Choosing Your Race

### By Playstyle

| If you... | Choose this race |
|---|---|
| Want to survive mistakes while learning PvP | Shark — 65–70% damage reduction is the most forgiving mechanic in the game |
| Enjoy fast-paced, aggressive combos | Human or Ghoul — scaling damage or life leech rewards relentless pressure |
| Prefer control and disruption | Cyborg or Angel — Instinct breaking or aerial lockdown changes how opponents play |
| Play with a regular squad | Draco — ally buffs and enemy debuffs swing team fights |
| Optimize for farming efficiency | Ghoul — life leech + cooldown reduction = non-stop grinding |
| Value movement above all else | Rabbit — 4x speed makes you nearly uncatchable |

### By Progression Stage

- **First Sea (no V2 access yet):** Any starter race is fine. Save Fragments for later.
- **Second Sea (V2/V3 available):** If you have Ghoul unlocked, invest here — its life leech carries through all PvE content. Shark V3 makes Sea Beast kills trivial.
- **Third Sea (V4 available):** Pick one race and fully awaken it. Draco and Cyborg dominate competitive PvP. Shark dominates survivability. Do not split your Fragments.

## Counter-Intuitive Blox Fruits Race Facts

**1. Human — the most boring starter race becomes a top-tier DPS endgame.** Most players reroll Human at the first chance, dismissing it as having "no buffs" at V1. But its V3 "Last Resort" scales damage with missing HP, rewarding skilled players who can survive at low health. Combined with the V4 "Limit Break" gear that uncaps the rage meter to 150%, Human outperforms every other race in raw damage output during extended fights. It is the sleeper pick for veteran combo players.

**2. Shark is stronger on land than in water.** Conventional wisdom says Shark is the water race, but its V3 and V4 both provide massive damage reduction (65–70% and ~85% respectively) that work everywhere. The reduced water damage is a minor bonus — the real power is becoming a mobile tank in land-based PvP. Shark is the single best race for learning PvP because it forgives positioning mistakes that would kill any other race.

**3. Rabbit's Whirlwind tornadoes can trap you, not just your enemies.** The blue V4 gear leaves tornadoes on every dash that damage and slow enemies. But poorly aimed dashes scatter enemies away from your combo range, and in tight spaces the tornadoes block your own movement. Rabbit requires the highest mechanical skill to use effectively — its C-tier ranking reflects player skill floor, not ceiling.

**4. Cyborg's Instinct break is a double-edged sword.** Breaking enemy Observation Haki is incredibly powerful, but it also removes the visual dodge animation. Against experienced opponents, you cannot tell whether your attack landed or they dodged it. This can bait you into overextending against players who know how to fight without Instinct, leaving you vulnerable to counter-combos.

**5. Ghoul's night passives become permanent at V4 — but only with the right gear.** Domain Expansion gear activates Ghoul's night passives (including the 30% speed boost) during daytime, making Ghoul the only race with 24/7 passive uptime. Most Ghoul players pick Blood Siphon for the life leech, but the Domain Expansion's permanent night buffs often provide more value in extended engagements.
