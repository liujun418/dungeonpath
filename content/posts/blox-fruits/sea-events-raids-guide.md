## Best Farming Routes

### Beli Farming Route (Second Sea, Level 300-500)

1. Start at Pirate Island, sail east into open water
2. Circle the area between Pirate Island and Snow Island
3. Kill 2-3 Sea Beasts → return to sell drops
4. Repeat
5. Expected: 400,000-800,000 Beli/hour

### Fragment Farming Route (Second Sea, Level 300+)

1. Farm Sea Beasts for Raid Chips (5% drop from Golden/Shark Tooths trade for chips)
2. Activate Factory Raids with a team of 3-4
3. Clear raids in under 8 minutes for 1,000 Fragments each
4. Expected: 3,000-5,000 Fragments/hour with efficient team

### Boss Farming Route (Third Sea, Level 500+)

1. Server-hop for Soul Reaper (every 30 min)
2. Between spawns: kill Sea Beasts
3. Check Darkbeard timer — join server with <30 min remaining
4. Expected: 1-3 rare drops per 2-3 hour session
