## Why Team Composition Matters

Five random Huges won't cut it in PS99's more demanding modes. Each game mode tests different stats — raw damage for Clan Battles, area coverage for farming, movement speed for events. The right 5-pet team with matching enchants can double your output.

---

## Coin Farming Team

Best for: AFK farming, active grinding, maximizing coins per hour.

| Slot | Pet Type | Enchant 1 | Enchant 2 | Role |
|------|----------|-----------|-----------|------|
| 1 | Titanic (or Huge) | Criticals X | Coins V | Main DPS + coin boost |
| 2 | Huge | Criticals X | Coins V | Coin boost |
| 3 | Huge | Criticals X | Coins V | Coin boost |
| 4 | Huge | Criticals X | Magnet III | Wide pickup radius |
| 5 | Huge | Criticals X | Speed V | Fast break speed |

**Why it works:** Criticals X on all 5 pets means 50% crit uptime. Three Coins V enchants stack for +75% coin drops. Magnet III ensures you're not missing distant coin piles. Speed V on one pet speeds up zone clearing.

**Alternative (F2P):** Replace Titanic with another Huge with Criticals X + Coins V.

---

## Clan Battle Team

Best for: Maximum DPS against clan bosses, competing for damage leaderboards.

| Slot | Pet Type | Enchant 1 | Enchant 2 | Role |
|------|----------|-----------|-----------|------|
| 1 | Titanic (highest damage) | Criticals X | Strength X | Primary DPS |
| 2 | Titanic (or Huge Royal) | Criticals X | Strength X | Secondary DPS |
| 3 | Huge (highest base damage) | Criticals X | Strength V | DPS |
| 4 | Huge | Criticals X | Berserker | Burst DPS below 50% boss HP |
| 5 | Huge | Criticals X | Giant's Might | +15% damage to large targets |

**Why it works:** Clan battle bosses have massive HP pools — raw damage is all that matters. Coin enchants are wasted here. Strength X (+30% damage) on every pet, Berserker on one pet for the sub-50% HP damage spike, Giant's Might for the large-target bonus.

## Event Grinding Team

Best for: Limited-time events, seasonal zones, rapid item collection.

| Slot | Pet Type | Enchant 1 | Enchant 2 | Role |
|------|----------|-----------|-----------|------|
| 1 | Huge | Criticals X | Speed V | Fast break + DPS |
| 2 | Huge | Criticals X | Speed V | Fast break |
| 3 | Huge | Criticals X | Magnet III | Item pickup |
| 4 | Huge | Criticals X | Magnet III | Item pickup |
| 5 | Huge | Criticals X | Luck V | Event drop rate |

**Why it works:** Events are time-gated — speed and pickup radius maximize rewards per event window. Speed V on two pets breaks event objects faster. Two Magnet IIIs ensure you collect drops from the entire event zone. Luck V helps with event-specific rare drops.

## Gem Farming Team

Best for: Diamond Mine, Fantasy World gem zones, gem-based progression.

| Slot | Pet Type | Enchant 1 | Enchant 2 | Role |
|------|----------|-----------|-----------|------|
| 1 | Titanic (or Huge) | Criticals X | Diamonds V | Gem DPS |
| 2 | Huge | Criticals X | Diamonds V | Gem boost |
| 3 | Huge | Criticals X | Diamonds V | Gem boost |
| 4 | Huge | Criticals X | Diamonds V | Gem boost |
| 5 | Huge | Criticals X | Treasure Hunter | +10% gem value |

**Why it works:** Same structure as coin farming but with Diamonds V instead of Coins V. Treasure Hunter on pet 5 multiplies the total gem value at the end. Four Diamonds V enchants stacked = +100% gem drops. This team earns 2-3x more gems than a generic coin team in gem zones.
