You stand in the Trading Plaza with 42,000 gems and no idea what to do with them. Last week the answer was obvious: buy a better egg, roll better enchants, flip a Huge. But today is August 13, the Piñata Maze is live, and the chat is full of players flexing Titanic Sombrero Chihuahua eggs while you are still deciding whether gems or coins even matter right now.

The truth is the answer changed the moment Update 88 launched on August 8. The Piñata Maze layered a whole second economy — Fiesta Points, Fiesta Orbs, Fiesta Keys, Fiesta Gifts, and Fiesta Boosters — on top of the game's normal gems, coins, and diamonds. Players who treat the event as "farm gems harder" are leaving value on the table. Players who understand how the two economies plug into each other are pulling Huge and Titanic pets while everyone else grinds the same old breakables.

This guide maps every currency in Pet Simulator 99, ranks the fastest ways to earn each one, and shows you exactly how to route your gems, coins, and diamonds into the Fiesta economy while the maze is active.

## Your Currency Map — What Every Resource Actually Buys

Before you spend another gem, get the full picture. PS99 has more currencies than a mobile game with a gem-pass, a coin-pass, and a season pass. Each one does something different, and confusing them is how you end up with 500,000 coins that can't buy the thing you actually want.

| Currency | What It Buys | Best Use During Piñata Maze |
|---|---|---|
| **Coins** | Early area unlocks, basic eggs | First-hour fuel, then mostly obsolete |
| **Gems** | Upgrades, eggs, enchant rolls, Trading Plaza | Liquidity + enchant rerolls |
| **Diamonds** | Premium eggs (Diamond Egg), VIP, big trades | Sinking into event pets or saving for a specific egg |
| **Fiesta Points** | Fiesta Gifts, event redemptions | Highest-value currency right now |
| **Fiesta Orbs** | Upgrade Machine (20 tiers), Piñata XP boosts | Compounding event upgrades |
| **Fiesta Keys** | Prize Room unlocks | Chase the event's Titanic pets |

Here is the counterintuitive headline up front: **the two currencies that did not exist two weeks ago are now the most valuable things on this table.** Fiesta Points and Fiesta Orbs are only earnable during the event, they unlock pets that sell for tens of millions of gems, and they vanish when the maze closes. Your gems and coins are permanent and always farmable. That single fact rewrites every spending priority below.

## Coins: The First-Hour Fuel

Coins are the currency that matters for about 45 minutes and then almost never again. Do not let anyone tell you coins are a long-term resource. They are the tutorial.

### The Scene: Your First Breakable

You spawn in Area 1, your starter pet taps a wooden crate, and 12 coins fly into your counter. Feels good. You want to hoard them. Wrong instinct.

Coins exist to push you through the early areas. Each new area unlocks faster breakables with bigger coin drops, so the coins you spend on a new area pay themselves back within minutes. The rule is dead simple: **if you can afford the next area, buy the next area.** There is almost no scenario in the first hour where saving coins beats unlocking.

### Coin Spending Priority

1. **Unlock the next area.** Always the best coin investment, no exceptions.
2. **Hatch the highest egg you have access to.** Stronger pets clear breakables faster.
3. **Upgrade your best pets.** Fusing and upgrading beats saving.

### Failure Autopsy: The 40-Minute Coin Hoarder

**Mistake:** You sit in Area 5 tapping crates for an hour, saving 20,000 coins "for later." **Why it fails:** Later never needs coins. By mid game everything costs gems, and your 20,000 coins are worth almost nothing. **Recovery:** Dump every coin into area unlocks the moment you have enough. If you are past the coin economy and still sitting on a pile, spend it on the cheapest eggs and forget it exists.

## Gems: The Trading Backbone

Once areas start costing gems, gems become your real currency. They fund upgrades, eggs, enchant rerolls, and — most importantly — the Trading Plaza where you actually buy and sell pets with other players.

### The Scene: The Diamond Enchant Reroll

You roll a Diamond 7 enchant and it lands with a garbage secondary stat. Rerolling costs gems. Every player hits this moment and asks the same thing: is this reroll worth it?

For your main farming pets, yes. Diamond 7 is the single highest-value farming enchant in the game — it adds roughly 65% more diamonds from every breakable you hit. A bad roll on Diamond 7 is worth rerolling because the gem income it generates over a farming session compounds. A perfect Diamond 7 pays for its own reroll cost within an hour of AFK farming.

### The Gem Farming Loadout

Your gem income is a function of three things: the area you farm, the enchants on your pets, and whether a Diamond Boost potion is running. Stack all three:

- **Diamond 7** — the core gem enchant, ~65% more diamonds per breakable.
- **Fortune** — more loot from every breakable you crack.
- **Chest Mimic** — extra chests spawn, and chests are where the big gem piles live.
- **Treasure Hunter** — bonus drops that convert to gems at the Plaza.

The area matters more than the enchants. Go to the highest area you can clear in under 10 seconds per breakable. If a breakable takes 15 seconds to crack, you are losing money no matter how good your enchants are — drop down a tier.

### Failure Autopsy: Gems on the Wrong Egg

**Mistake:** You grind 80,000 gems, then blow them all on a shiny new area egg the day it drops. **Why it fails:** New eggs have terrible drop rates for the first few days while the price inflates. You are paying peak price for a Huge chance that will be cheaper next week. **Recovery:** Spend gems on things that compound — enchant rerolls, the Gem Spawner upgrade, and Trading Plaza flips. Buy eggs when you have a plan for the pets, not because an egg is new.

## The Piñata Maze Fiesta Economy — The Event Overlay

Everything above was true before Update 88. Here is what changed, and why it matters more than any gem farming tip in this guide.

The Piñata Maze runs alongside the normal game. It does not replace gem farming — it sits on top of it and offers a better return per minute while it is live. Understanding the Fiesta economy is now a core currency skill, not a side quest.

### Fiesta Points: The Highest-Value Currency in the Game Right Now

You earn Fiesta Points in Press Your Luck, a minigame in the event hub. You smash a Piñata, it either pays out points or it is a **dud** — and a dud wipes your entire current stack to zero. That zero-out mechanic is the entire game.

Here is the scene every player hits. You are at six wins, your stack reads 2,400 points, and the reward for one more smash is 1,000 points. One more feels obvious. But the dud chance climbs with every consecutive win — by win 7 it is roughly 30-40%, and a dud zeroes everything you have built.

The counterintuitive rule: **bank early, not often.** Most players chase long streaks because the payout per win scales up. The math says the opposite. Banking after 4-5 wins every time nets you roughly 40% more total points over a session than pushing for 8-10 win streaks, because one dud at win 7 erases more points than three conservative banks combined. The full Press Your Luck math is in the [Piñata Maze maximization guide](/posts/pet-simulator-99/pinata-maze-maximization-guide/), but the one-line version is this: treat your points as worth zero until you bank them.

Why Fiesta Points outrank gems right now: **50,000 Fiesta Points redeems for the Titanic Piñata Cat — the only guaranteed Titanic in the event.** Every gem you could farm in that same time buys a low-odds egg. The points buy a certainty.

### Fiesta Orbs and the Upgrade Machine

Fiesta Orbs drop every time you smash a Piñata, and they feed the Upgrade Machine's 20 tiers. This is where the "gems vs. event" tradeoff gets sharp.

Orbs only buy event upgrades — damage boosts, Piñata XP boosts, and Fiesta Boosters. But those upgrades compound. The Piñata XP trio (tiers 3, 5, and 8) levels your personal Piñata faster, and Piñata level is the gate on the event's Huge drops. Level 12 puts Huge pets in your personal hatching pool. Level 18 opens the Titanic-eligible pool.

**The upgrade trap:** most players buy Fiesta Booster I early because a 15-minute damage buff sounds efficient. But if your Piñata is level 5, a 25% damage boost on a weak baseline does almost nothing. The XP tiers pay for themselves by level 12. Get the XP trio before any booster. A deeper breakdown of the 20-tier order is in the [Piñata Maze event guide](/posts/pet-simulator-99/pinata-maze-guide/).

### Piñata Hatching Replaces Egg Hatching

For the event's duration, your normal egg-hatching is replaced by smashing your personal Piñata. Each smash drops gold, pets, and Fiesta Orbs. Your Piñata has its own level, and every smash contributes XP to it.

The critical number is level 12. Before 12, your hatching pool has no Huge pets at all — you can smash for hours and see nothing above a Legendary. That is the single most common event mistake: players grind the maze for three hours at Piñata level 6 and wonder why no Huge dropped. The answer is not bad luck. It is that the Huge pool literally did not exist for them yet.

### The Cross-Currency Dilemma: Gems on Mainline or the Event?

This is the decision that separates efficient players from everyone else, and there is no one-size answer.

**The dilemma:** You have 60,000 gems and three hours to play today. Do you sink those gems into a mainline egg and farm your normal breakables, or do you convert the session into the event and chase Fiesta Points, Orbs, and Keys?

Here is the framework that settles it. Ask yourself one question: **does the event reward convert back into gems?** If the answer is yes — and it almost always is, because event Huge and Titanic pets sell for tens of millions of gems at the Trading Plaza — then the event is the higher-yield play. A Huge Sugar Skull Raccoon pulled from a level-12 Piñata is worth more gems than you would earn farming normal breakables for a full week.

The exception: **if you are under Piñata level 12, your gem spending should still go to mainline progression first.** The event's best rewards are gated behind Piñata level and maze clears you cannot survive yet. Get your farming baseline stable — decent pets, Diamond 7, a cleared mainline area — then route everything into the event. Front-loading event effort at level 4 just means dying in Hard rooms and seeing zero drops.

**The decision rule in one line:** during the event, gems fund your farming baseline, and the event funds your actual profit. Keep a gem reserve for enchant rerolls and Plaza liquidity; everything else converts into event time.

## Diamonds: The Premium Layer

Diamonds sit above gems as the game's premium currency. They buy the Diamond Egg, VIP access, and the biggest Trading Plaza purchases. They are also the currency most players waste the fastest.

### The Scene: The Diamond Egg Temptation

You crack open a VIP area and see the Diamond Egg. Every egg in your inventory is a roll, but the Diamond Egg is a roll that costs diamonds — the rarest farmable currency in the normal game. The urge to spend 250 diamonds on a spin is strong.

The counterintuitive advice: **save diamonds, don't roll them casually.** Diamonds are the only currency that reliably buys event Huge and Titanic pets from other players at the Plaza. A single event Huge can cost 30 million gems or a few hundred diamonds depending on the day. Blowing diamonds on a low-odds Diamond Egg spin the moment you have enough is the fastest way to stay diamond-poor forever.

### Diamond Spending Priority

1. **Event Huge/Titanic pets at the Plaza** — the highest expected-value diamond spend while the maze is live.
2. **Diamond Egg rolls only when you have a surplus** — never as a reflex.
3. **Gamepasses and permanent boosts** — one-time purchases that compound.
4. **VIP area access** — only if the exclusive eggs inside are worth the diamond tax.

### Failure Autopsy: The Diamond Egg Gambler

**Mistake:** You hit 250 diamonds and immediately spin the Diamond Egg, then do it again, then again. **Why it fails:** Diamond Egg rates for anything above a Huge are tiny, and diamonds are too scarce to spend on impulse. **Recovery:** Set a diamond floor — say 500 — and never roll below it. Treat diamonds as a savings account that buys the pet you actually want, not a slot machine.

## AFK Farming: Stacking Every Currency at Once

Here is where the Fiesta economy plugs into your normal farming loop. The maze drops gems, coins, and Fiesta Orbs simultaneously. One AFK session in a Hard maze room feeds three currencies at once, which makes it the highest-throughput farming in the game right now.

### The Optimal Setup

1. Pick the **highest Hard maze room** you can clear in under 45 seconds.
2. Equip **Diamond 7, Fortune, Chest Mimic, and Treasure Hunter** on your main pets.
3. Activate a **Diamond Boost** potion plus a **Fiesta Booster II (Damage)** if you have it stocked.
4. Position yourself near a cluster of breakables and let your pets attack.
5. Every few minutes, collect the orb drops and bank your Press Your Luck points before you risk a dud.

The key difference from normal AFK farming: **bank Fiesta Points on a timer, not on impulse.** When you AFK, the temptation is to let the streak run while you are not watching. That is exactly how you come back to a dudded zero. Set a timer to bank every 5-10 minutes, and your AFK session actually accumulates instead of gambling itself away.

## Failure Autopsies & Recovery — The Quick Reference

Consolidate the four mistakes that quietly cost players the most during this event:

**1. Coin hoarding in the first hour.** Coins are a tutorial currency. Spend them on area unlocks the moment you have enough, and never look back.

**2. Gems on impulse eggs at peak price.** New eggs are overpriced for days. Spend gems on compounds — enchant rerolls, the Gem Spawner, Plaza flips — not on the new shiny.

**3. Fiesta Points dudded at a high streak.** The dud only hurts because you let the stack grow. Bank after 4-5 wins, and walk away for two maze clears after a dud instead of tilt-requeueing.

**4. Fiesta Orbs on boosters before Piñata XP.** A booster amplifies a baseline you do not have yet at low Piñata level. Max the XP trio first, then buy boosters once there is something worth amplifying.

The common thread: every one of these is a spending-order mistake, not a farming mistake. You have enough currency. You are spending it in the wrong sequence.

## FAQ

### What Is the Fastest Way to Earn Gems During the Piñata Maze Event?

Run the highest Hard-mode maze room you can clear in under 45 seconds with Diamond 7, Fortune, Chest Mimic, and Treasure Hunter active. The maze drops gems, coins, and Fiesta Orbs at the same time, so one session feeds three currencies at once instead of one.

### Should I Spend Gems on Mainline Upgrades or Save Them for the Event?

During the event, divert most gems toward Fiesta Orbs and Fiesta Points sinks, not mainline eggs. A single Fiesta Point stack from Press Your Luck can out-earn hours of gem farming once converted through Fiesta Gifts and the Prize Room. Keep a small gem reserve for enchant rerolls, but let the event drive your spending while it is live.

### Do Fiesta Points Replace Gems, or Do I Still Need Both?

You still need both. Gems fund your farming baseline — upgrades, enchant rerolls, Plaza liquidity. Fiesta Points and Orbs unlock the event's Huge and Titanic pets, which then sell for huge gem profits. Farm both, but prioritize the event currency while the maze is active, because it expires when the event ends.

The Piñata Maze changed the answer to "what should I farm?" The currencies that did not exist two weeks ago are now the most valuable ones in the game, and the whole trick is routing your permanent gems and coins into a temporary economy that pays you back in pets worth tens of millions of gems.

Start with your Piñata level. Bank your Fiesta Points before the streak turns on you. Spend your orbs on compounding XP upgrades, not 15-minute spikes. Keep a gem floor for enchant rerolls and Plaza liquidity, and let the event do the heavy lifting on profit.

The window is finite. The Upgrade Machine, the Press Your Luck leaderboard, and the Fiesta currency all reset when the maze closes. Farm the overlap now, and your gem count will thank you long after the confetti stops.

Good luck in the maze.
