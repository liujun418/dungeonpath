## Events Are Predictable. Master the Pattern.

PS99 events follow the same structure every time: a new event zone with boosted rates, a battle pass with limited pets, event-specific currency, and a countdown timer. The event looks different each time — Void RNG, Fantasy World, Kawaii World — but the underlying mechanics are identical.

Once you understand the pattern, you stop panicking about missing out and start extracting maximum value in minimum time. Here's the framework.

---

## The Event Timeline: What to Do at Each Phase

### Day 1-2: The Rush Phase
The event just dropped. Rates are at their highest (BIG Games often sets boosted rates for the first 48 hours to generate hype). The Trading Plaza is flooded with the new pets at inflated prices.

**What to do:** Grind the event zone as much as your schedule allows during these 48 hours. The boosted rates won't last. Target the event-exclusive Huge and Titanic pets — their value peaks during the event and drops 30-50% after.

### Day 3-7: The Steady Phase
Rates have normalized. The Trading Plaza has settled. The hype players have moved on.

**What to do:** Your daily 30-minute routine: complete event dailies (10 minutes), grind the event zone (15 minutes), check the Plaza for undervalued event pets (5 minutes).

### Day 8-14: The Finish Phase
The event ends in a week. Players are rushing to complete their battle passes. Event pets are being dumped on the Plaza as players panic-sell.

**What to do:** Buy, don't sell. Event pets are at their cheapest in the final week. Buy event-exclusive pets from panic sellers, hold them for 2-4 weeks after the event ends, then resell when supply has dried up and prices have recovered.

---

## The Event Zone Grinding Priority

In every PS99 event, these activities give the best returns, in order:

1. **Event dailies (15 minutes):** Guaranteed event currency + battle pass XP. Always do these first.
2. **Event zone highest-tier egg hatching:** The best egg in the event zone is almost always worth grinding for. It has the highest Huge/Titanic rate of the event.
3. **Event boss/combo farming:** If the event has a combo mechanic (like Void RNG's dice system), learn the optimal combo route and grind it during the boosted-rate phase.
4. **Trading Plaza flipping:** Only if you enjoy trading. Grinding the event zone gives more consistent returns.

## The Huge/Titanic Drop Rate Psychology

Event Huges typically have a 0.5-2% hatch rate during events. That means you need 50-200 eggs for a 63% cumulative chance at one. At 5,000-10,000 event currency per egg and 500-1,000 currency per minute of grinding, you're looking at 4-8 hours of grinding for one Huge.

The players who burn out are the ones who expect a Huge in their first hour. The players who enjoy events are the ones who treat the Huge as a bonus, not the goal. Grind the battle pass. Max the free track. If a Huge drops along the way, great. If not, the battle pass guaranteed rewards are worth more than the Huge you didn't get.

**The post-event value play:** Event-exclusive pets appreciate 30-50% in value 2-4 weeks after the event ends. During the event, everyone has them — supply is high, prices are low. A month later, new players want them and supply has dried up. Buy during the event. Sell a month later.
