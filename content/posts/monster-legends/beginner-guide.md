## Your First 3 Days: A Day-by-Day Plan

### Day 1: Build Your Foundation

You start with a handful of Common monsters, one island, and some starting Gems. Most beginners immediately spend Gems on speed-ups to hatch eggs faster. This is the #1 Gem trap.

**What to do instead:**

1. Breed the 2-element monsters on your starting island. Don't worry about which ones — just fill your habitats with 2-element monsters. They produce 5x the gold of 1-element monsters.
2. Upgrade your habitats to level 2 as soon as you have enough gold. Level-2 habitats hold more gold before capping, which means less frequent collection trips.
3. **Do not spend a single Gem on day 1.** You'll be tempted. The speed-up button will flash at you. Ignore it. You're saving for the Ultra Breeding Tree.

**By end of Day 1:** You should have 4-5 habitats at level 2, filled with 2-element monsters. Gold income: roughly 5,000-8,000 per hour.

### Day 2: The First Big Decision

You have roughly 50,000-80,000 gold. Two things are calling to you: buying a second island (Dark Island, 250,000 gold) or upgrading existing habitats further.

**The island trap:** A second island costs 250,000 gold. If you buy it now, you'll have an empty island and zero gold. An empty island generates nothing. Upgrading your existing habitats to level 3 doubles their gold capacity for 100,000 gold. Better investment.

**What to do:** Upgrade 2-3 habitats to level 3. Buy 1-2 Gold Farms (decorations that boost adjacent habitat gold production by 10%). Start breeding 3-element monsters. Save the remaining gold.

**The 3-element breeding rule:** Breed 2-element + 2-element where both share one element. Example: Greenasaur (Nature + Fire) + Firesaur (Fire). The shared Fire element limits possible offspring and increases your chance of getting the 3-element you want.

### Day 3: Your First Legendary Attempt

By Day 3, you should have enough gold, food, and monster levels to attempt your first Legendary breed. The easiest target: Goldfield (Nature + Earth).

**Required parents:** Pandaken (Fire + Nature, level 15+) + Tarzape (Nature + Earth, level 15+). Both are Epic-tier. If you don't have them yet, breed them first — Epic monsters have a 20-30% success rate with the right combos.

**The breeding attempt:** Place Pandaken and Tarzape in the Breeding Mountain. Wait. The breeding time tells you what you got: anything under 20 hours = not Goldfield. 36 hours = Goldfield (success). If the timer shows less than 20 hours, you got something else — hatch it and try again.

**Don't spend Gems to speed up the breed.** Goldfield's 36-hour breed time is long. The speed-up will cost you 30+ Gems. That's 30% of your Ultra Breeding Tree savings. Wait it out.

## The 3 Gem Traps

| Trap | Cost | Why It's a Trap |
|------|------|----------------|
| **Speed-ups** | 1-30 Gems each | Gems are scarce, time is free. Every Gem spent on speed-ups is a Gem not spent on permanent upgrades. |
| **Random eggs from the shop** | 50-200 Gems | The random egg pool includes Commons. You can spend 200 Gems and get a monster you could breed in 30 seconds. |
| **Gold purchases** | 10-50 Gems for gold | Gold is farmable. Gems are not. This is like trading a permanent asset for a consumable. |

**The one correct Gem purchase at your stage:** Ultra Breeding Tree (100 Gems). -20% breeding time for every breed you'll ever do. Buy it and then stop spending Gems entirely until you have 200+ saved for the next permanent upgrade.
