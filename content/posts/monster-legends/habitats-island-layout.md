## Habitat Types

Monster Legends has 5 habitat tiers. Each has different gold capacity, production rate, and maximum monster slots.

| Habitat Tier | Max Monsters | Base Gold Capacity | Max Gold Capacity (Fully Upgraded) | Upgrade Priority |
|-------------|-------------|-------------------|----------------------------------|-----------------|
| **Legendary** | 4 | 500,000 | 2,000,000 | **#1** |
| **Mythic** | 4 | 750,000 | 2,500,000 | **#1 (if you have Mythic monsters)** |
| **Epic** | 3 | 250,000 | 1,000,000 | **#2** |
| **Rare** | 2 | 100,000 | 500,000 | **#3** |
| **Common** | 2 | 50,000 | 200,000 | #4 (low priority) |

## Habitat Placement: The Adjacency Bonus

Habitats receive production bonuses from adjacent decorations. Understanding adjacency is the key to efficient layout.

### Key Decoration Bonuses

| Decoration | Bonus | Radius | Cost |
|-----------|-------|--------|------|
| Gold Farm | +10% gold from adjacent habitats | 1 tile | 100,000 |
| Element Temple | +15% gold from matching-element habitats | 2 tiles | 200,000 |
| Ultra Breeding Tree | -20% breeding time | Island-wide | 100 Gems |
| Monster Lab | Required for research | Island-wide | 50,000 |

### The 2×3 Grid Layout

```
[Habitat] [Habitat] [Habitat]
[Gold Farm] [Temple] [Gold Farm]
[Habitat] [Habitat] [Habitat]
```

In this layout, all 6 habitats receive the bonus from the Temple (2-tile radius), and 4 habitats receive Gold Farm bonuses (1-tile radius, adjacent). Each habitat gets +25-35% gold production from 2-3 decorating sources.

## Upgrade Priority Order

### Habitat Upgrades

| Upgrade Level | Cost (Gold) | New Capacity | ROI Time |
|--------------|-----------|-------------|----------|
| 1 → 2 | 50,000 | 2× capacity | 5-7 days |
| 2 → 3 | 150,000 | +50% capacity | 10-14 days |
| 3 → 4 | 500,000 | +50% capacity + 1 monster slot | 15-25 days |
| 4 → 5 (max) | 1,500,000 | Doubled capacity + visual upgrade | 30-45 days |

### What to Upgrade First

1. All Legendary habitats to level 3 (cheap, fast ROI)
2. Epic habitats to level 3
3. Legendary habitats to level 4 (adds 4th monster slot — huge value)
4. Gold Farms and Element Temples
5. Legendary habitats to level 5 (endgame, expensive)

## Gold Collection Timing

Habitats fill up and stop producing gold when they hit capacity. Collection timing matters.

| Habitat Level | Time to Fill (4 Legendary Monsters) | Check Frequency Needed |
|--------------|-------------------------------------|----------------------|
| Level 1 (500K cap) | ~2.5 hours | Every 2-3 hours |
| Level 3 (1M cap) | ~5 hours | Every 4-5 hours |
| Level 5 (2M cap) | ~10 hours | Twice daily |

**Optimal routine:** Collect gold every 3-4 hours during active play. At level 3+ habitats, you can go 5-8 hours overnight without capping.
