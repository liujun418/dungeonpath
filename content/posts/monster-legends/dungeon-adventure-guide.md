## Elemental Dungeons

Each element has its own dungeon with 7 nodes. The final node is always a boss.

### Elemental Type Advantage

| Dungeon Element | Bring This Element | Damage Bonus | Avoid This Element |
|----------------|-------------------|-------------|-------------------|
| Fire | Water, Earth | +50% | Nature, Ice |
| Nature | Fire, Ice | +50% | Water, Earth |
| Water | Nature, Thunder | +50% | Fire, Earth |
| Earth | Water, Nature | +50% | Fire, Thunder |
| Thunder | Earth, Water | +50% | Nature, Dark |
| Dark | Light, Thunder | +50% | Nature, Earth |
| Light | Dark, Nature | +50% | Earth, Thunder |

### Dungeon Nodes

| Node Type | Difficulty | Reward | Strategy |
|-----------|-----------|--------|----------|
| 1-2: Mob waves | Easy | Small food/gold | AoE attacks clear quickly |
| 3-4: Mixed waves | Medium | Runes (common) | Conserve healer cooldowns |
| 5-6: Elite guards | Hard | Runes (rare), food | Use controller to lock down elites |
| 7: Boss | Extreme | Runes (epic/legendary), egg chance | Save all abilities for boss phase |

### Best Dungeon Monsters by Element

You do not need Legendaries to clear early dungeons, but having the right role in each element saves revives.

**Fire Dungeon:** Vadamagma is the standout DPS with consistent burn damage. For control, Barbael brings stun access. Fire lacks a true team healer — bring Ignis only if you need self-sustain, otherwise slot an off-element healer like Nereida.

**Nature Dungeon:** Nereida is the best overall healer in the game with party-wide recovery. Rockantium serves as solid AoE DPS, while Goldfield provides Nature-aligned crowd control.

**Water Dungeon:** Lord of Atlantis hits hard with Water AoE, supported by Thalassio's healing and Nebotus's freeze.

**Thunder Dungeon:** Nemestrinus carries Thunder DPS, Ultrabot handles disables, and Raydex offers light support healing.

**Earth Dungeon:** Terracrank is the Earth DPS anchor, Ao Loong controls, and Duchess tanks while healing herself.

**Dark Dungeon:** Darkzgul's life steal and stun make him uniquely versatile. Miserus covers raw damage, and Arch Knight can flex between Light and Dark teams.

## Adventure Map Strategy

The Adventure Map is a campaign-style progression system with 150+ nodes across multiple regions.

### Node Priority

Not all Adventure Map nodes are worth your stamina. Follow this priority order:

1. **Campaign progress nodes** — These unlock new islands, breeding combinations, and extra monster slots. Nothing else matters until these are cleared.
2. **Boss nodes (gold border)** — Best gold and rune drops, but only efficient once your team is level 35+.
3. **Monster egg nodes (special icon)** — Guaranteed monster egg on first clear. High value for expanding your roster.
4. **Resource nodes** — Reliable food and gold for daily farming. Run these when you are out of campaign and boss stamina.
5. **Side path nodes** — Only clear if they lead directly to a boss or egg node. Dead-end side paths are stamina traps.

### Stamina Management

With 144 stamina/day and 30 cap:
- Morning: spend 20-25 stamina on campaign progress (pushing new nodes)
- Midday: spend 10 stamina on boss node farming (gold/rune daily farm)
- Evening: spend remaining stamina on resource nodes
- Never let stamina cap (30) — you lose regeneration time

## Failure Analysis

Here are the five failure patterns I see most often in dungeon runs, ordered by how much stamina they waste.

**1. The "Highest Level" Trap**
Players bring their three strongest monsters regardless of element. Because off-element attacks deal 50% reduced damage, a level-40 Nature monster underperforms a level-25 Water monster in the Fire Dungeon. Always match element first, level second.

**2. Burning Cooldowns on Mob Waves**
Using your healer's AoE recovery on node 2 means it is still on cooldown when the boss drops its ultimate on node 7. Mob waves exist to let you generate extra turn meter. Save every team-wide cooldown for the boss.

**3. Chasing Boss Nodes Too Early**
Boss nodes give the best runes, but they also cost the most stamina and scale to your level. If you are under level 35, the drop rate for epic runes is near zero. Farm resource nodes first, level your team, then push bosses.

**4. Ignoring the Cosmic Shield Tooltip**
The Cosmic Dungeon explicitly states that the Elemental Shield blocks non-Cosmic damage, yet players still attempt it with Fire and Nature teams. The shield reduces damage to literally zero, not just 50%. You need Light or Dark monsters, full stop.

**5. Capping Stamina Overnight**
Stamina regenerates at 1 per 10 minutes. If you log off at 28/30 stamina, you lose 140 minutes of regeneration — 14 stamina, or nearly three dungeon nodes. Always spend down to zero before long breaks.

## Decision Framework

When you open the dungeon or Adventure Map menu, run through this checklist in order.

**Step 1: Element Check**
Look at the dungeon element. If you do not have at least two on-element monsters at level 25+, do not enter. Farm Adventure Map resource nodes until you level a matching pair.

**Step 2: Stamina Budget**
Count your current stamina. Normal nodes cost 3, bosses cost 5. Never start a dungeon unless you can reach the boss node in one sitting — partial clears reset your cooldowns and waste food.

**Step 3: Role Coverage**
Confirm your team has one healer, one AoE damage dealer, and one controller with a disable. If you are missing a role, substitute with the closest off-element utility monster only if its primary value is the disable, not its damage.

**Step 4: Cooldown Discipline**
Before entering, mentally commit to saving your ultimate and healing abilities for node 7. If you are unsure you can clear mobs without them, your team is under-leveled — go back to Step 1.

**Step 5: Post-Run Stamina Dump**
After the dungeon, spend any remaining stamina down to zero on Adventure Map resource nodes. This prevents cap waste during your next offline period.

## Counter-Intuitive Discoveries

These findings contradict the intuitive "bring your best monsters" logic, and they are the biggest sources of free progression.

**Off-Element Healers Ignore the Shield**
Nereida is a Nature monster, which means she deals half damage in Fire Dungeons and zero damage in Cosmic Dungeons. But her healing ability is not flagged as an attack — it ignores element penalties and shields entirely. She is the best healer for almost every dungeon in the game, even when she cannot hurt the boss.

**Controllers Are Element-Agnostic**
A stun from an off-element controller lands at full accuracy. The stun does not deal damage, so it never triggers the 50% penalty. This means you can bring Goldfield (Nature) into a Fire Dungeon purely for his disable, and he functions at 100% utility. Most players bench their best controller because of element mismatch and lose control coverage they did not need to lose.

**Capping Stamina Is the Biggest Daily Loss**
Players treat stamina like a scarce resource to be hoarded. It is actually a depreciating asset. Every minute you sit at 30/30 stamina, you are losing 0.1 future stamina. Over a week, a player who caps stamina twice a day loses nearly 100 stamina — equivalent to 20 dungeon nodes or 6 boss attempts. The optimal play is to spend to zero before every logout, even if that means running low-value resource nodes.

**Resource Nodes Outpace Boss Nodes for New Players**
Boss nodes drop epic runes, but the drop rate below level 40 is under 5%. Resource nodes guarantee food and gold, which let you level monsters faster. A level-30 player who farms resources for three days and then pushes bosses will clear faster than a player who brute-forces bosses from day one. The boss loot only becomes efficient once your team can reliably clear node 7 without using revives.

## Cosmic Dungeon Endgame

The Cosmic Dungeon requires Light and Dark monsters exclusively. It's the hardest PvE content in Monster Legends.

| Floor | Boss | HP | Key Mechanic |
|-------|------|-----|-------------|
| 1-10 | Minor Cosmic entities | 50K-100K | Elemental Shield (blocks non-Cosmic damage) |
| 11-20 | Cosmic Guardians | 150K-250K | Shield + periodic AoE |
| 21-30 | Galactic Genesus | 450K | Shield + debuff cleanse every 3 turns + one-shot charged attack at 20% HP |

### Endgame Team for Cosmic Dungeon

The Cosmic Dungeon requires Light and Dark monsters exclusively. Build around these three roles:

- **Healer:** Nereida — Even though she is Nature, her healing ignores the Cosmic Shield. Equip Speed and HP relics so she cycles turns faster than the boss.
- **DPS:** Arch Knight (Light Legendary) — High single-target damage with Light element synergy. Stack Attack and Crit relics.
- **Controller:** Darkzgul — His stun and life steal keep him alive without healer support on some turns. Run Speed and Control Effect relics to land disables before the boss charges.
