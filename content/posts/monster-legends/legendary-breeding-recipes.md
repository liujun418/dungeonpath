## Legendary Breeding Basics

Legendary monsters are the backbone of competitive Monster Legends. Breeding them requires specific parent combinations, patience, and understanding the probability system.

### Breeding Requirements

| Requirement | Minimum | Optimal |
|-------------|---------|---------|
| Breeding Mountain level | 5 | 7+ |
| Parent levels | 20+ | 30+ |
| Element temples (matching) | None required | 1-2 placed |
| Breeding Boost | Optional | 20 Gems for +10% |

### Success Rate Formula

```
Base Rate (5-12%) + Mountain Level Bonus (2% per level past 5) + Parent Level Bonus (up to 5%) + Breeding Boost (10%) + Happy Hour (2x base rate)
```

During Happy Hour with all bonuses active, effective rate reaches 30-45%.

## Breeding Island Setup

Your Breeding Mountain's placement affects efficiency. Here's the optimal layout:

| Adjacent Structure | Bonus | Priority |
|-------------------|-------|----------|
| Matching Element Temple (1 tile away) | +2% success rate per temple | High |
| Parent Habitat (1 tile away) | -5% breeding time | Medium |
| Ultra Breeding Tree (anywhere on island) | -20% breeding time | High |
| Monster Lab (anywhere on island) | Required for Breeding Boost activation | Mandatory |

Place the Breeding Mountain centrally, surround it with 2 element temples matching your target's elements, and put parent habitats adjacent. The rest of the island fills with Gold Farms to fund attempts.
