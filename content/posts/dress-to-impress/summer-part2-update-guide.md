### The Case-Sensitivity Trap

Here's the thing about DTI codes that catches more players than you'd think: they are strictly case-sensitive. The game treats `lanadolldress` and `LANADOLLDRESS` as two completely different strings. One works. One returns "Invalid code."

I watched a friend type `LanaDollDress` three times last night and get three "Invalid code" errors. She was sure the code had expired. It hadn't. She was just capitalizing the first letter of each word like a normal human being. The code wants all-caps. Don't be my friend.

The fix is boring but effective: **copy from this guide and paste directly into the DTI code box.** Don't retype. Don't trust your eyes. Copy-paste.

Codes with numbers and underscores are even worse for manual entry. `CH00P1E_B4CK_AGA1N` has zeros that look like the letter O and ones that look like lowercase L. You will type this wrong. Copy it.

## New Items That Actually Matter (Sorted by Where to Find Them)

Sixty-four items is a lot. You will not use all of them. Some are filler. Some are recolors. Some are genuinely strong pieces that will show up in winning outfits for months. Here's what to look for in each category.

### Free Items: The Best Bang-for-Buck Pieces

The free section got the lion's share of the update — around 40-45 items. That sounds generous. It mostly is. But here's the counterintuitive part: **the strongest free items in this update are not the flashiest ones.**

The instinct is to grab the biggest, most dramatic piece — the floor-length gown, the oversized sun hat, the feather boa in three summer colors. But on the runway, those pieces dominate your silhouette and leave no room for layering. The voter sees one big item and nothing else. That's a 2-star outfit.

The pieces that will actually boost your scores are the mid-weight layering staples:

- **Embroidered summer corset** — sits under jackets, over dresses, pairs with skirts. Works on Garden, Cottagecore, Fantasy, and Romantic themes. One item, six-plus theme applications. That's efficiency.
- **Linen crop top and wide-leg pants set** — the base layer for Beach, Resort, Vacation, and Casual Summer themes. Neutral tones, clean silhouette, lets your accessories do the work.
- **Sheer beach cover-up** — a transparent overlay piece that adds texture without hiding your outfit. On Beach themes, players who use cover-ups score higher than players who go straight bikini. It reads as intentional styling, not just "I picked the first swimsuit I saw."
- **Woven straw tote bag** — the accessory that makes Beach and Vacation themes look finished. A surprising number of players forget bags entirely. Having one gives you an extra detail point on every summer theme.

**Scene: Beach theme, 90 seconds left.** You've got your swimsuit base on. The instinct is to add more swimwear pieces — a second bikini top layered over the first, a sarong, flip-flops. That's what 80% of the lobby is doing. You'll blend in completely.

Instead, build outward. Swim base. Sheer cover-up over it. Woven tote in one hand. Wide-brim sun hat. Sandals. Now you're not a person in a swimsuit. You're a person who went to the beach and put together an outfit. The voter's brain registers the difference even if they can't explain why. You'll beat every player who stopped at "swimsuit + sarong."

### VIP Items: 12-15 Pieces, 3 Worth Your Attention

If you have VIP, the Part 2 update added about 12-15 VIP-locked items. Three stand out:

- **Butterfly lace evening gown** — a floor-length dress with wing-like sleeve draping. This piece solves the "what do I wear for Elegant Summer" problem that trips up so many players. Elegant themes demand formal silhouettes. Summer themes want light, airy fabrics. This dress does both in one slot.
- **Golden hour headpiece** — a metallic hair accessory that catches the DTI lighting engine at an angle that reads as sunset glow. On Golden Hour, Sunset, and Warm Tone Color themes, this single item replaces three accessories worth of effort. Put it on, pick warm-toned clothes, and the outfit reads as cohesive.
- **VIP-exclusive beach waves (hair)** — a new hairstyle with subtle wind-swept movement. Hair is the most overlooked category in summer themes. Most players pick the same ponytail every round. A dedicated summer hair style separates top-3 from the middle of the pack.

**The VIP trap with seasonal updates:** You pay for VIP and feel pressure to use the VIP items every round to justify the purchase. Don't. A VIP item that doesn't fit the theme is worse than a free item that does. I've seen VIP players force the Golden Hour headpiece onto a Gothic theme "because it's new and shiny." They scored 1.8. The item is good. The decision was not.

### Boutique (Robux) Items: Buy or Skip?

The Boutique got 5-7 new pieces in Part 2. Some are purely cosmetic. One is surprisingly useful:

- **Summer aura effect (85 Robux)** — a subtle golden particle effect that surrounds your model. On themed rounds where you need to communicate "magical summer being" or "sun goddess," this effect does in one purchase what would normally take 3-4 accessory slots to approximate. For players who regularly do Fantasy and Mythical themes, it's efficient. For everyone else, skip.

The rest of the Boutique additions — a sequin party dress, a pair of designer sunglasses, a logo tee — are fine but not essential. Buy them if you like them. Don't buy them expecting them to carry your scores.

### Daily Login Items: The Streak Problem

Part 2 added 3-4 new Daily Login items to the rotation. Daily Login items unlock after hitting consecutive-day milestones — day 7, day 14, day 21, etc.

The problem: if you miss a day, the streak resets. I missed Day 6 of the July cycle because I went camping for a weekend with no signal. The Day 7 item — a sunset ombre hair dye — stayed locked for another full week while I rebuilt the streak. Players who started their streak on August 8, the day Part 2 launched, are now on Day 3. They'll hit Day 7 and unlock whatever the first milestone item is on August 15. If you start today (August 11), you're three days behind. If you start next week, you're even further.

**The decision framework for Daily Login:** If you play DTI at least 4 days a week organically, maintain the streak. The items are free and some are genuinely good. If you only play on weekends, don't stress it. The mental overhead of logging in just to click "claim" is not worth a single digital accessory. Spend that energy getting better at the actual game instead.

## Summer Fit Strategy: Theme-by-Theme Combos for Part 2 Pieces

The update dropped 64 items into a season that already has strong summer staples from Part 1. The move is not to use every new item. The move is to slot the best new pieces into a tight outfit formula that works for each common summer theme.

### Beach / Tropical / Vacation

**Formula:** New linen crop + new wide-leg pants (Free) + sheer cover-up (Free) + woven tote (Free) + beach waves hair (VIP) or default ponytail.

The linen set gives you a clean neutral base. The cover-up adds texture. The tote signals "I packed for this." Three new items, all free. Don't over-accessorize — beach outfits look worse with too much jewelry. One necklace or bracelet max.

**The mistake players make:** Piling on every shell necklace, starfish clip, and ocean-themed ring they own. Six ocean accessories reads as clutter, not theme commitment. The voter's eye can't find a focal point. Pick one ocean accessory and let the outfit breathe.

### Elegant Summer / Garden Party

**Formula:** Embroidered summer corset (Free) + Butterfly lace evening gown (VIP) or any flowing midi dress + Golden Hour headpiece (VIP).

The corset-over-dress layering creates a structured waist that reads as formal. The headpiece catches light and signals "special occasion." If you don't have VIP, swap the evening gown for any pastel-toned dress you own and skip the headpiece for a floral hair clip.

### Fantasy / Fairy / Mythical

**Formula:** Moonlight Butterfly Set (315 Robux, or free if you owned Luxury Dress) + summer aura effect (85 Robux) + Lana Tutu rework from LANADOLLDRESS code.

This is the combo that has been winning Fantasy themes since July 4. The butterfly wings + tutu silhouette + golden particle effect reads as an instantly recognizable magical being. The voter doesn't need to figure out what you are. Their brain says "fairy" in under a second. That speed of recognition is worth more than any amount of detail work.

If you don't own Moonlight Butterfly, the free alternative is to use the sheer cover-up layered over a light-colored dress, plus any wing-like cape or flowing accessory you own. It won't read as clearly as the butterfly wings, but it'll beat a generic "pretty dress" approach.

### Sunset / Golden Hour / Warm Color Theme

**Formula:** Golden Hour headpiece (VIP) + any warm-toned clothing in orange, gold, coral, or rose + minimal accessories.

The headpiece does the heavy lifting. It physically looks like a sunset reflection on your character's head. Pair it with clothes in the same warm color family and the outfit reads as cohesive. The most common failure on Warm Color themes is mixing in cool tones — a blue shoe, a silver necklace. One cool piece breaks the warmth and the voter notices. Stay strict with your palette.

## Moonlight Butterfly Set: The 315 Robux Decision

The Moonlight Butterfly Set costs 315 Robux. It replaced the Luxury Dress on July 4, 2026. If you owned Luxury Dress before the replacement date, you already have the set — check your inventory before buying.

Here's the decision framework:

**Buy it if:**
- You play Fantasy, Fairy, Nature, Ethereal, or Magical themes regularly (at least once per session)
- You want a single purchase that creates an instantly readable silhouette on the runway
- You don't already own strong wing/cape items

**Skip it if:**
- You mainly play modern, casual, streetwear, or era-specific themes
- You already own Luxury Dress (you got it for free)
- 315 Robux is your entire monthly Roblox budget (spread it across multiple smaller purchases instead)

**The argument against buying it:** The wings are distinctive. Distinctive items become recognizable. When three players in the same lobby all wear Moonlight Butterfly wings on a Fantasy theme, no one stands out. You paid 315 Robux to blend into a butterfly swarm. On high-traffic servers where multiple players own the set, the advantage evaporates.

The counterplay: if you see two other players already wearing the set in pre-round lobby, switch to a different fantasy approach. Use the free sheer cover-up + Lana Tutu + custom hair layering. You'll look different from the butterfly crowd. Different wins.

## What Changes and What Doesn't

New items change what's in your wardrobe. They do not change how DTI voting works.

The same rules from our [theme-winning framework](/posts/dress-to-impress/theme-winning-framework/) still apply. Color-first planning still beats item-first browsing. Silhouette reads faster than detail. Voters make their decision in the first two seconds of your runway walk.

The same [runway psychology](/posts/dress-to-impress/runway-and-voting-guide/) still applies. A clean, readable outfit beats a cluttered, over-accessorized one every time. Sixty-four new items is not an invitation to use six per outfit. It's an invitation to be pickier.

The same [hair and makeup rules](/games/dress-to-impress/) still apply. Summer themes need summer hair. A messy bun beats a formal updo on Beach themes. Wind-swept waves beat sleek straight hair on Vacation themes. If you spend all your attention on the new clothes and ignore your hair, your score will tell you what you missed.

The update is a toolbox. Your fundamentals are the craft. Don't confuse more tools with better results.
