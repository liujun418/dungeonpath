## Custom Makeup: The Slot-by-Slot System

Custom Makeup gives you independent control over 6 face features. Understanding each slot's visual impact is step one.

| Slot | Visual Impact | Voter Noticeability | Priority |
|------|-------------|-------------------|----------|
| Eyeshadow | High | High — visible even at runway distance | #1 |
| Eyeliner | Medium | Medium — defines the eye shape | #2 |
| Lipstick | High | High — mouth is focal point on runway | #3 |
| Blush | Medium | Medium — adds facial dimension | #4 |
| Highlighter | Low | Low — only visible in close-up | #5 |
| Face Details | Low-Medium | Low-Medium (freckles, beauty marks, etc.) | #6 |

## Eyeshadow: The Foundation

Eyeshadow is the largest visible makeup element. It frames your entire face. Get this wrong and the rest doesn't matter.

### Eyeshadow Style by Theme

| Theme Category | Style | Opacity | Technique |
|---------------|-------|---------|-----------|
| Formal/Elegant | Soft smoky, neutral tones | 50-70% | Blend upward and outward |
| Gothic/Dark | Heavy smoky, dark colors | 80-100% | Extend beyond crease, smudge edges |
| Fantasy/Magical | Shimmer, pastels, duochrome | 60-80% | Inner corner highlight + outer V |
| Casual/Natural | Matte, warm neutrals | 30-50% | Just enough to define the crease |
| Y2K/Pop | Frosted, bright, metallic | 70-90% | Bold single color, minimal blending |
| Beach/Summer | Coral, gold shimmer | 40-60% | Light wash, sun-kissed look |

### Best Eyeshadow Colors by Outfit Color

| Outfit Main Color | Best Eyeshadow | Safe Alternative |
|------------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Red | Gold, bronze, warm brown | Neutral champagne |
| Blue | Silver, cool grey, ice blue | Neutral taupe |
| Green | Bronze, gold, warm brown | Peach shimmer |
| Purple | Lavender, silver, cool pink | Cool taupe |
| Pink | Rose gold, champagne, soft pink | Warm brown |
| Black | Dark grey, purple, silver | Smoky charcoal |
| White | Any — white is a blank canvas | Silver shimmer |
| Yellow | Warm brown, gold | Peach |

## Eyeliner: Shape the Eye

Eyeliner defines eye shape. Different styles signal different themes instantly.

| Style | Wing Direction | Best Themes | Skill Level |
|-------|---------------|------------|-------------|
| **Cat Eye** | Upward flick, ~45 degrees | Formal, Red Carpet, Elegant | Easy |
| **Puppy Liner** | Downward flick | Kawaii, Cute, Pastel | Easy |
| **Graphic Liner** | Geometric, floating crease | Avant-Garde, Editorial, Futuristic | Hard |
| **Smudged Liner** | No wing, smudged with shadow | Grunge, Gothic, Rockstar | Medium |
| **No Liner** | None | Natural, Beach, Cottagecore | Easy |
| **Double Wing** | Two parallel wings | Festival, Creative, Fantasy | Medium |

## Lipstick: The Statement or the Support

Lipstick is either the star or the supporting actor. It should never compete with eyeshadow for attention.

### The One-Statement Rule

**One bold feature per face.** If you do bold eyeshadow, go neutral on lips. If you do bold lips, keep eyes simple. Two loud features = clown face, which reliably scores 2-3 stars.

| Outfit Situation | Lip Choice | Example |
|-----------------|-----------|---------|
| Bold/dark outfit | Neutral lip (nude, pale pink, gloss) | Gothic outfit + dark red lip = overdone. Gothic outfit + pale nude lip = editorial. |
| Neutral/subtle outfit | Bold lip (red, berry, deep) | Beige dress + red lip = classic. Beige dress + nude lip = boring. |
| Monochrome outfit | Match lip to outfit exactly | All-red outfit + matching red lip = intentional, not accidental |
| Multi-color outfit | Neutral lip, let the outfit be the star | Rainbow theme + pink lip = smart. Rainbow theme + blue lip = too much. |

## Blush: The Dimension Giver

Blush is the most underrated makeup element. It adds facial dimension that photographs well on the runway.

| Placement | Effect | Best Themes |
|-----------|--------|------------|
| Cheek apples (center) | Youthful, sweet, innocent | Fairy, Cute, Kawaii, School |
| High cheekbones | Sculpted, defined, model-like | Formal, Red Carpet, Editorial |
| Across nose (igari) | Sun-kissed, trendy, K-beauty | Beach, Summer, Casual |
| None | Clean, stark, dramatic | Gothic, Vampire, Alien |

## 15 Ready-to-Use Face Recipes

### 1. Classic Red Carpet
Eyes: Soft brown smoky (60% opacity), thin cat-eye liner. Lips: Classic red. Blush: Subtle cheekbone contour. Highlighter: Champagne on cheekbones.

### 2. Gothic Queen
Eyes: Black smoky (90%), thick winged liner, dark purple in outer V. Lips: Deep burgundy or black. Blush: None. Face detail: Dark beauty mark.

### 3. Ethereal Fairy
Eyes: Shimmer lavender (70%), thin puppy liner, white inner corner highlight. Lips: Glossy pale pink. Blush: Rosy on cheek apples. Highlighter: Pearl white on cheekbones + brow bone + nose tip.

### 4. Sun-Kissed Beach
Eyes: Warm gold shimmer (50%), no liner. Lips: Glossy coral. Blush: Bronzer across nose bridge + cheek apples. Highlighter: Gold on cheekbones.

### 5. Y2K Pop Star
Eyes: Frosted blue or silver (85%), thin liner. Lips: Frosted pink. Blush: None. Face detail: Optional rhinestones.

### 6. Editorial Avant-Garde
Eyes: Graphic geometric liner (bold black shapes), neutral shadow. Lips: Nude matte. Blush: Strong cheekbone contour. Face detail: White negative-space line through one eye.

### 7. Cottagecore Cutie
Eyes: Warm brown matte (40%), no liner. Lips: Tinted rose balm. Blush: Soft peach on cheek apples.

### 8. Vampire Dark
Eyes: Deep red + black smoky (95%), thick smudged liner. Lips: Dark dripping red. Blush: Grey contour to hollow cheeks.

### 9. Kawaii Dream
Eyes: Pale pink (60%), puppy liner, white inner corner. Lips: Glossy pink gradient (darker center). Blush: Heavy pink across cheeks + nose (igari style).

### 10. Elegant Bride
Eyes: Champagne shimmer (50%), subtle cat-eye in brown. Lips: Rose pink satin. Blush: Soft rosy. Highlighter: Pearl on cheekbones + cupid's bow.

### 11. 1980s Glam
Eyes: Bright blue or pink (90%), thick all-around liner. Lips: Hot pink or red. Blush: Heavy pink on cheek apples — more is more.

### 12. Streetwear Cool
Eyes: Neutral brown matte (50%), sharp winged liner. Lips: Brown liner + clear gloss. Blush: Bronzer contour.

### 13. Mermaid Fantasy
Eyes: Aqua shimmer (75%), no liner, teal inner corner. Lips: Glossy coral-pink. Blush: Pearl highlighter on cheekbones + temples. Face detail: Optional scale-like dots at temples.

### 14. Minimal French Girl
Eyes: Barely-there taupe (25%), no liner. Lips: Tinted red balm (sheer). Blush: Soft natural flush. Face detail: Freckles.

### 15. Horror / Zombie
Eyes: Dark purple + black smudged under eyes (90%). Lips: Black or dripping dark red. Blush: Grey contour to hollow cheeks + temples. Face detail: Fake scars or stitches.

## Color Theory Cheat Sheet

### The Color Wheel Rule

| Outfit Strategy | Makeup Strategy | Example |
|----------------|----------------|---------|
| Monochrome outfit | Match makeup exactly | All-pink outfit = pink eyeshadow + pink lip |
| Analogous outfit (2-3 adjacent colors) | Pick one color for makeup | Blue + purple outfit = purple eyeshadow |
| Complementary outfit (opposite colors) | Neutral makeup | Red + green outfit = nude makeup |
| Neutral outfit (black/white/beige) | Bold makeup | Beige dress = red lip + bold eye |
