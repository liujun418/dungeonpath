## You Picked a Moon. You Didn't Check the Weather. You Died.

Day 2. Quota is going well. You decide to push Rend for a big scrap haul. You route there. You land. Rain is hammering down. The ground is mud — movement speed -40%. An Eyeless Dog spawns at the forest edge. You can't sprint. You can't escape. Your crew wipes. Day 2 scrap: zero. Day 3: you can't recover. Run over.

All because you didn't check the weather before routing. The terminal shows weather for every moon. Most crews ignore it. The crews that survive 10+ quotas check weather before every landing.

## Weather Effects by Type

| Weather | Effect | Viable Moons | Death-Trap Moons |
|---------|--------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Clear** | None — ideal conditions | All moons | None |
| **Rainy** | Mud patches slow movement 40% in open areas | Assurance, Offense (small maps, less open terrain) | Rend, Titan (large open areas between ship and entrance) |
| **Stormy** | Mud everywhere, lightning strikes random outdoor positions | Only Offense or Assurance if desperate | Everything else — lightning + mud = guaranteed deaths |
| **Foggy** | Visibility reduced to 15 studs. Getting lost outdoors is easy. | Assurance (smallest map, hardest to get lost) | Every large moon — you'll walk in circles trying to find the entrance |
| **Eclipsed** | Outdoor enemies spawn immediately at landing. No safe daytime window. | None. Skip the day entirely if possible. | All moons — this is the hardest weather in the game |

---

## The Pre-Landing Weather Protocol

Before routing to any moon, check the terminal. Type `moons`. Each moon shows its current weather. If your intended moon has Stormy, Foggy, or Eclipsed weather: switch moons. Pick the clearest-weather moon that still meets your profit target. A clear-weather March run earns more scrap than a foggy Rend run where your crew can't find the entrance.
