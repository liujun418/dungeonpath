## Your First 20 Floors: A Floor-by-Floor Walkthrough

### Preparation

Before you start the run, buy the Gummy Flashlight and 2 Medkits (250 Credits total). If you don't have enough Credits, play 2-3 "discovery runs" where you intentionally explore without trying to win — enter rooms, listen for entity sounds, die, repeat. Discovery runs teach you entity audio cues without the pressure of trying to survive.

### Floors 1-5: The Safe Zone

The first 5 floors are Pressure's tutorial zone. Rush can spawn but rarely does before floor 5. Use these floors to practice the room entry protocol: enter, identify the exit, note the nearest closet, move to the exit.

**On floor 3-5, you may encounter your first Screech.** The room goes dark. You hear a whisper: "psst." Spin in a full circle until you see Screech's glowing white eyes, then look directly at it. It takes 3 seconds to attack, so don't panic-spin — one smooth 360-degree rotation finds it every time.

### Floors 6-10: Rush Appears

Rush becomes active. The first time you hear the screech, your heart will race and you'll freeze. This is normal.

**When Rush screeches:** Stop looting. Identify the nearest closet (you already noted it when you entered the room, right?). Walk — don't sprint — to the closet. Enter. Close the door. Wait 1 second after the screech stops. Exit. Continue.

**What not to do:** Sprint in a random direction. Enter a closet you haven't checked (it might be occupied — red light = someone inside). Exit the closet DURING the screech.

### Floors 11-15: Ambush May Appear

Ambush has a roughly 15% chance to replace Rush from floor 10 onward. When this happens, the screech sounds distorted and glitchy instead of clean.

**The Ambush test:** After the first pass, stay in the closet. Count "one-Mississippi, two-Mississippi, three-Mississippi." If you hear another screech within 3 seconds: stay. If 3 seconds pass with silence: exit. Ambush passes 2-6 times. The most common death: exiting after pass 2 when pass 3 is coming.

### Floors 16-20: Eyes and Puzzle Rooms

Eyes rooms appear. When you enter and the screen tints purple, immediately look at the floor. Navigate using floor/wall glances. Looking at Eyes drains ~8% HP per second — 3 seconds of accidental eye contact costs a quarter of your HP bar.

**Puzzle rooms** (breaker panels, code inputs) appear around floor 15-20. These are stationary puzzles — no entities attack during them. Solve the puzzle, collect the reward, keep moving.

## The Sound Recognition Drill

Before your next run, spend 5 minutes on YouTube or in-game memorizing these 4 sounds. They will save your life more than any item:

| Sound | Entity | Action |
|-------|--------|--------|
| Clean, high-pitched screech getting louder | **Rush** | Enter closet, exit after pass |
| Distorted, glitchy screech | **Ambush** | Enter closet, stay for ALL passes |
| "Psst" whisper in dark room | **Screech** | Spin 360° until you see it, look at it |
| Heavy breathing, heavy footsteps | **Figure** (floor 50) | Walk only, crouch, freeze when it listens |
