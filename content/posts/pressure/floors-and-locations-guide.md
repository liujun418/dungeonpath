## The Hotel Teaches You to Be Careful. The Deeper Floors Teach You to Be Fast.

Floors 1-20 reward thoroughness. Check every container. Explore every room. Take your time. This is where you build your item stockpile and learn entity sounds.

Floor 25 onward rewards speed. Entity density increases. Puzzle rooms are more frequent. Time spent in each room increases your exposure to Rush, Ambush, and Eyes. The players who clear floor 50+ aren't more careful — they're faster. They've internalized room recognition, closet locations, and puzzle solutions so they can move through rooms efficiently instead of crawling through them cautiously.

## Floor Ranges: What Changes at Each Tier

| Floors | Entity Density | Ambush Chance | Puzzle Frequency | Strategy |
|--------|---------------|--------------|-----------------|----------|
| 1-10 | Low (tutorial) | Rare (~5%) | Every 15-20 floors | Learn sounds. Build stockpile. Identify nearest closet in every room. |
| 11-20 | Medium | ~15% | Every 12-18 floors | Apply sounds. Start moving faster through recognized room types. |
| 21-30 | Medium-High | ~20% | Every 10-15 floors | Speed matters more than thoroughness. Don't check every container. |
| 31-40 | High | ~25% | Every 8-12 floors | Pre-identify closets BEFORE Rush screeches. Sprint Potions for open-cavern encounters. |
| 41-50 | Maximum | ~30% | Every 5-8 floors | Endurance test. Execute protocols without thinking. |
