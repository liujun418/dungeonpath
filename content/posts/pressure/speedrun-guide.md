## Key Speed Techs

### Instant Room Exit Recognition

The #1 time save. When you enter a new room, scan for the exit door in under 1 second. Exit doors have a faint glow to their frame. Train yourself to spot this instantly.

### Dead-End Skip

Dead-end rooms have no exit door and spawn only loot containers. Recognizing one instantly saves 5-10 seconds of useless exploration. Dead-end rooms are always smaller than normal rooms and have a single entrance.

### Seek Chase Optimization

Sprint Potion during Seek saves approximately 40% of the chase duration. Pop it the moment the chase begins. Guiding Light's blue glow shows the correct path — never hesitate at a junction. Hesitation costs more time than taking the wrong path once.

### Figure Room Efficiency

The Figure encounter on floor 50 is the biggest time variance in speedruns — anywhere from 3 to 15 minutes. Speedrun strats:
- Use Sprint Potion to move between shelves faster (walking speed only matters when Figure is close)
- Don't crouch when Figure is on the opposite side — walking is silent enough at 30+ studs
- Collect 2 books per trip instead of 1 (doubles efficiency, slightly riskier)

### Locker Skip for Rush

If you're near a side room (not just a closet), enter it and close the door — this is faster than entering and exiting a locker by ~1 second. Side rooms with doors block Rush just as effectively as lockers.

## Time Loss Traps

| Trap | Time Lost | Avoid By |
|------|----------|---------|
| Checking every container | 3-5s per container × 50+ rooms | Only check containers in your direct path |
| Backtracking for a missed item | 10-30s per backtrack | The item isn't worth it — keep moving |
| Healing at full HP | 3-5s per unnecessary heal | Only heal below 40 HP in a safe room |
| Waiting for Ambush to finish outside a closet | Death + run reset | Stay in closet, count passes, only exit when safe |
| Freezing when hearing Rush | 2-3s | Pre-identify nearest closet in every room on entry |
