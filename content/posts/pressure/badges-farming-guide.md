## Badge Categories

Pressure has 30+ badges organized into four difficulty tiers:

| Tier | Count | Typical Requirement | Flex Value |
|------|-------|-------------------|------------|
| Common | 8 | Play the game normally | Low |
| Uncommon | 10 | Specific actions or milestones | Medium |
| Rare | 8 | Difficult achievements, floor 50+ | High |
| Legendary | 5 | Extreme challenge, floor 75+ | Extreme |

## Common Badges: Get These First

| Badge | Requirement | Estimated Time | Priority |
|-------|-----------|---------------|----------|
| **Welcome to the Blacksite** | Die once | 2 minutes | #1 — automatic |
| **Light Bringer** | Purchase Gummy Flashlight (150 Credits) | 3-4 runs | #2 |
| **Fully Stocked** | Fill all 5 inventory slots | 3-4 runs | #3 |
| **Survivor** | Reach floor 25 | 5-10 runs | #4 |
| **Hacker** | Use a Code Breacher on a locked door | 5-10 runs | #5 |
| **Team Player** | Revive a teammate in co-op | 1 co-op run | #6 |
| **Not Today** | Survive a Rush encounter without hiding | 10-15 runs | #7 |
| **Collector** | Own 10 different items | 10-15 runs | #8 |

### Best Order

Runs 1-4: Unlock Common badges naturally while learning the game. Don't grind — they come automatically.

## Uncommon Badges: Moderate Challenge

| Badge | Requirement | Estimated Time | Difficulty |
|-------|-----------|---------------|------------|
| **Veteran** | Reach floor 50 | 15-30 runs | ★★★☆☆ |
| **Endless Beginner** | Reach floor 25 in Endless mode | 20-35 runs | ★★★☆☆ |
| **Pandemonium Survivor** | Survive a Pandemonium encounter | 10-25 runs | ★★★☆☆ |
| **Speedrunner** | Clear 30 floors in under 12 minutes | 5-10 dedicated attempts | ★★★★☆ |
| **Hide & Seek Master** | Survive 10 Seek chase sequences | 30-50 runs | ★★★☆☆ |
| **Full Clear** | Collect every item from one floor | 15-25 runs | ★★☆☆☆ |
| **Lockpick Pro** | Open 20 locked containers | 20-40 runs | ★★☆☆☆ |
| **Ghost** | Complete 5 floors without being detected by any entity | 15-25 runs | ★★★☆☆ |
| **Helping Hand** | Revive 5 teammates | 5-10 co-op runs | ★★☆☆☆ |
| **No Batteries** | Reach floor 30 with Gummy or Hand-Cranked Flashlight | 15-25 runs | ★★☆☆☆ |

### Priority Order

1. **Veteran** — Floor 50 is the skill gate. Good players reach it consistently.
2. **No Batteries** — Use Gummy Flashlight, naturally earned while grinding Veteran.
3. **Full Clear** — On an early floor (1-10), methodically check every container.
4. **Pandemonium Survivor** — When you encounter it on floor 40+, have Sprint Potion ready.

## Rare Badges: Flex-Worthy

| Badge | Requirement | Estimated Time | Difficulty |
|-------|-----------|---------------|------------|
| **Death Incarnate Lite** | Reach floor 75 in Endless | 40-80 runs | ★★★★☆ |
| **Lockerless** | Beat Hotel (floor 50) without hiding in a locker | 10-30 dedicated attempts | ★★★★★ |
| **Flawless** | Beat Hotel without taking damage | 20-50 dedicated attempts | ★★★★★ |
| **Speed Demon** | Clear 40 floors in under 15 minutes | 15-30 dedicated attempts | ★★★★☆ |
| **Millionaire** | Accumulate 1,000,000 total Credits earned | 100+ runs | ★★★☆☆ |
| **Blacksite Expert** | Reach floor 50 in The Blacksite | 50-100 runs | ★★★★★ |
| **Entity Hunter** | Encounter every entity type at least once | 50+ runs | ★★★★☆ |
| **Co-op Captain** | Win 20 co-op runs | 30-50 co-op runs | ★★★☆☆ |

### Farming Strategy

**Lockerless** is the badge that separates good players from great ones. Strategy:
1. Use only Gummy Flashlight (no battery management distraction)
2. Rush: hide in side rooms, not lockers. Side rooms with closed doors are safe.
3. Ambush: the real challenge. Know which floors have safe spots (alcoves, side corridors).
4. Bring 2 Sprint Potions for emergency Ambush situations.
5. Practice on floors 1-20 repeatedly until you can do them lockerless consistently.

## Optimal Badge Farming Route

### Phase 1: The Basics (Hours 1-5)
Get all 8 Common badges. Focus on learning the game, not grinding.

### Phase 2: The Grind (Hours 5-20)
- Unlock all Uncommon badges except Speedrunner
- Reach floor 50 consistently
- Build Credit stockpile for item purchases

### Phase 3: The Challenges (Hours 20-50)
- Attempt Lockerless (10-30 attempts)
- Attempt Flawless (if you're feeling ambitious)
- Farm Speedrunner badge
- Reach floor 75 in Endless for Death Incarnate Lite

### Phase 4: The Impossible (Hours 50+)
- Death Incarnate (floor 100 Endless)
- Untouchable (no-damage Blacksite)
- Speedrunner Elite (sub-8 Hotel)

## Badge Display Tips

Pressure shows your rarest badges on your profile card. To maximize flex value:
1. Display **Lockerless** and **Death Incarnate Lite** as your top two
2. These two badges tell experienced players you know entity patterns and have Endless endurance
3. Common badges auto-fill; don't worry about them
