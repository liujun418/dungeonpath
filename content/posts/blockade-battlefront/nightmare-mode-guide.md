## What Nightmare Mode Changes

ST: Blockade Battlefront's Nightmare mode is the game's hardest difficulty. It's not just Hard mode with bigger numbers — several core mechanics change.

### Nightmare vs Hard vs Normal

| Trait | Normal | Hard | Nightmare |
|-------|--------|------|-----------|
| Enemy HP | Base (100%) | +40% | +60% |
| Enemy speed | Base (100%) | Base (100%) | **+100% (2x)** |
| Enemy damage | Base | +25% | +50% |
| Cen multiplier | 1x | 1.5x | **0.5x** |
| Shielded enemies start | Wave 30 | Wave 15 | **Wave 8** |
| Boss enrage (30% HP) | No | No | **Yes — double damage** |
| Wave cap | 90 | 90 | 90 |
| Exclusive drops | None | Hard skins (3-5%) | Nightmare skins (10%) |

## Survival Strategy

### The Speed Problem

Enemies move at 2x speed in Nightmare. This breaks the core strategy from Normal/Hard mode — enemies close the gap between spawn and your Titans in half the time. Your Titans have half the DPS window they normally do.

**Counter:** Position Titans 30% closer to spawn points than you would in Normal mode. Upgraded Speakerman's rapid fire is more valuable than Upgraded Cameraman's single-target DPS because fast enemies need to be tagged quickly, not killed perfectly.

### The Cen Problem

0.5x Cen means a full 90-wave Nightmare clear gives you roughly 13,000 Cen total — less than a Normal mode clear to wave 50. Every single purchase must be justified.

| Titan | Cost | Nightmare Priority | Reasoning |
|-------|------|-------------------|-----------|
| Titan Cameraman | 350 | **Buy** — only affordable carry | Best damage-per-Cen in early game |
| Titan Speakerman | 900 | **Buy** — crowd control essential | Fast enemies need AoE tagging |
| Upgraded Cameraman | 2,500 | **Buy** — but save aggressively | Takes until wave 22-24 to afford |
| Titan Drill Man | 5,000 | **Buy** — shielded at wave 8 | Painful cost but mandatory |
| Titan TV Man | 3,800 | **Buy** — but delayed | Afford around wave 55-58 |
| Everything else | — | **Skip entirely** | Can't afford anything extra |
