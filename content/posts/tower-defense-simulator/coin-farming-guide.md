## How TDS Economy Works

TDS has two currencies: Coins (main) and Gems (premium). Each is earned differently, spent differently, and optimized with different strategies.

| Currency | Primary Source | Used For | Earning Rate (Experienced) |
|----------|---------------|----------|--------------------------|
| Coins | Mode completions, wave bonuses | Towers, skins, crates | 2,000-3,500/hr |
| Gems | Hardcore mode, quests, daily rewards | Exclusive towers, skins | 50-100/hr |

## Coin Farming Methods

### Mode Comparison for Coins/Hour

| Mode | Coins/Win | Time/Win | Coins/Hour | Unlock Level |
|------|-----------|----------|------------|-------------|
| Normal | 400-600 | 15-18 min | ~1,800 | 1 |
| Molten | 500-700 | 18-22 min | ~1,800 | 10 |
| Fallen | 800-1,200 | 20-25 min | **~3,000** | 15 |
| Golden | 800-1,000 | 20-25 min | ~2,200 | 30 |
| Hardcore | 1,500-2,000 | 35-40 min | ~2,600 | 40 |

**Best for pure coin farming:** Fallen Mode. Best coins-per-minute, consistent win rate with a decent loadout.

### Best Maps for Speed Farming

| Map | Clear Time vs Crossroads | Best Mode | Notes |
|-----|------------------------|-----------|-------|
| **Crossroads** | Baseline (fastest) | Fallen | Shortest path, easy chokepoints |
| Harbor | +1-2 min | Fallen | Slightly longer path but easier to defend |
| U-Turn | +2-3 min | Golden | Long path but good for Golden farming |
| Winter Bridge | +3-5 min | Any | Avoid for speed farming — path is too long |

## The Farm Tower Strategy

Farm is the most coin-efficient tower in the game but most beginners skip it.

| Farm Level | Cost | Income/Wave | Payback Time | Total Coins Generated by Wave 35 |
|-----------|------|-----------|-------------|--------------------------------|
| Level 1 | 400 | 150 | 3 waves | ~5,250 |
| Level 2 | 700 | 350 | 3 waves | ~12,250 |
| Level 3 | 1,400 | 750 | 4 waves | ~26,250 |

### When to Upgrade Farm

```
Wave 1-5: Place 2 Farmers, upgrade to level 1
Wave 6-10: Upgrade both to level 2
Wave 11-15: Place Commander + DJ, upgrade Farmers to level 3 one at a time
Wave 16+: Use accumulated coins to max DPS towers
```

## Gem Farming: The Hard Path

Gems are TDS's premium currency. They can't be bought directly with Robux and can't be farmed quickly.

### All Gem Sources

| Source | Gems | Frequency | Consistency |
|--------|------|-----------|------------|
| Daily quest | 15-25 | Daily | High |
| Weekly quest | 100-200 | Weekly | High |
| Hardcore win | 50-80 | Per win | Medium (depends on win rate) |
| Battle pass (free tier) | 300-500 total | Per season | Low (one-time) |
| Event rewards | 100-300 | Per event | Variable |
| Codes | 50-100 | Per code release | Rare |

### Hardcore Gem Optimization

Hardcore gives gems based on completion speed:

| Completion Time | Gems Earned |
|----------------|------------|
| Under 30 minutes | 80 |
| 30-35 minutes | 65 |
| 35-40 minutes | 50 |
| Win (slow) | 40 |

**Best loadout for fast Hardcore clears:** Accelerator, Engineer, DJ, Commander, Farm (solo) or swap Farm for Turret (team).
