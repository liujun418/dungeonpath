Traditional Fisch location maps are dot maps — Moosewood here, Roslit Bay there, Deep Ocean out east. You go to the dot. Sometimes you catch great fish. Sometimes you fish for two hours and catch nothing but Commons. The map never told you why. The difference is conditions — season, time, weather, bait, and minimum rod. A location map without conditions shows you where. It never shows you when. A Phantom Ray location marker says "Haunted Shipwreck." It doesn't say "Autumn only, Night only, Foggy only." You go to the Shipwreck in Summer at noon on a clear day and catch nothing because the Phantom Ray literally isn't in your catch pool. The conditions are hard gates, not suggestions.

Moosewood is your starter region and you should leave it as soon as you can afford the Carbon Rod at 2,000 C$. Moosewood Pier and Pond produce fish up to Rare rarity only. You will never catch a Legendary or Mythic at Moosewood regardless of your rod, bait, or playtime. The area exists to teach you the fishing mechanics and fund your first rod upgrade. The moment you have the Carbon Rod and the Rowboat, leave and never return except to visit NPCs.

Roslit Bay is your home from roughly level 15 to 40. The Roslit Reef produces Clownfish at Rare and Angelfish at Legendary — both during daytime with clear weather and Shrimp bait. Roslit Deep produces Grouper at Rare and Snapper at Legendary. The C$/hour with a Steady Rod at Roslit Bay is roughly 400-800. It's consistent and has no condition gates on its best fish.

Deep Ocean is the best default fishing location in Fisch from level 40 onward. Megalodon is the only Mythic with zero condition gates — it spawns 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, in any weather, in any season. Fish at Deep Ocean whenever you're not specifically hunting a condition-gated fish elsewhere. It's your baseline. The Deep Trench requires a submarine and produces Abyssal Anglerfish (Mythic, night only) with Night Shrimp.

Winter Village requires level 50 and produces Eternal Frostwhale (Mythic) at the Frozen Lake — but only during Blizzards. Outside of Blizzards, the Frozen Lake produces the same quality fish as Moosewood Pier. Do not fish here without an active Blizzard. The Ice Cave produces Glacier Trout at Legendary in any weather — it's the reliable winter option.

Haunted Shipwreck is the most condition-gated location in the game. Phantom Ray requires Autumn plus Night plus Fog — three simultaneous conditions that are active roughly 15% of the time. Before every Shipwreck session, check season, time, and weather. If all three are active, fish immediately with Night Shrimp. If any one is wrong, Phantom Ray is not in your catch pool. Fish elsewhere.
