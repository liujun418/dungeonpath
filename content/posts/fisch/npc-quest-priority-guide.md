## Not All Quests Are Equal

Fisch has 15+ NPCs offering daily quests. Most players accept every quest they see, then spend 2 hours chasing a specific Rare fish for a 500 C$ reward. Meanwhile, Phineas at Moosewood docks is offering 3 types of premium bait for 5 minutes of fishing in the starter zone.

Quest value isn't about the C$ number on the quest card. It's about reward-per-minute. A quest that asks you to catch "any 5 fish at Roslit Bay" pays 300 C$ and takes 8 minutes — 37 C$/minute. A quest that asks you to "catch a Crystal Tetra" pays 800 C$ but takes 45 minutes of server-hopping for night + fog — 18 C$/minute. The bigger number on the card is a trap.

## The Quest Stacking Strategy

Multiple NPCs sometimes ask for the same fish type or the same location. When this happens, accept ALL overlapping quests and complete them in one session. Catching 10 fish at Roslit Bay can complete the Angler's quest, the Fisherman's quest, AND progress toward Phineas's bait reward — tripling your effective C$/minute from the same fishing time.

Before any fishing session, visit all accessible NPCs. Check their quest requirements. If 2+ quests overlap in location or target fish, stack them. If every quest asks for something different, pick the highest C$/minute quest and ignore the rest.
