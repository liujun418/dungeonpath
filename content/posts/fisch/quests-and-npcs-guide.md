## Not All Quests Are Worth Your Time

You see the quest marker. You accept. "Catch 3 Crystal Tetras." You spend 45 minutes server-hopping for night + fog conditions. You finally catch them. Reward: 500 C$. That's 11 C$/minute — less than half of what you'd earn just fishing at Deep Ocean with no quest at all.

Quest value isn't the C$ number on the card. It's reward per minute. A "catch any 5 fish at Roslit Bay" quest pays 300 C$ in 8 minutes (37 C$/minute). A "catch 1 Crystal Tetra" quest pays 500 C$ in 45 minutes (11 C$/minute). The bigger number is a trap.
