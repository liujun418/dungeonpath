## The Bait Trap

You walk into the bait shop. Maggots: 5 C$ each. Shrimp: 50 C$ each. Night Shrimp: 75 C$ each. You have 2,000 C$. The expensive bait must be better, right?

This is the bait trap. Expensive bait is only better if your rod's base stats can leverage it. Using Shrimp on a Flimsy Rod is like putting premium tires on a bicycle — the 10x price increase gives you almost nothing because the base is too weak.

Here's the real bait economy — when each bait is worth it, when it's throwing away money, and how to think about bait as a multiplier of your rod, not a replacement for it.

---

## Bait as a Multiplier, Not a Base

Bait percentages add to your rod's existing stats. A rod with 0% Luck + 15% bait = 15% total. A rod with 25% Luck + 15% bait = 40% total. The same bait is 2.7x more effective on the better rod.

**The rule:** Bait multiplies what you already have. If your rod is weak, save for a better rod before investing in premium bait.

---

## When Each Bait Is Worth It

| Bait | Cost | Bonus | Worth It When | Waste of Money When |
|------|------|-------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Maggots** | ~5 C$ | +5% Luck | Always — cheapest Luck source in the game | Never — it's always worth 5 C$ |
| **Shrimp** | ~50 C$ | +15% Luck | Rod has 15%+ base Luck | Rod has under 10% Luck — save for rod upgrade instead |
| **Night Shrimp** | ~75 C$ | +25% Luck (night) / +5% (day) | Rod has 20%+ Luck AND it's nighttime AND you're hunting Mythics | Daytime use (same as Maggots, 15x price) |
| **Weird Algae** | ~100 C$ | +10% Luck, prefers Rare+ fish | Rod has 25%+ Luck AND you're specifically hunting Legendary/Mythic | General fishing — overpriced for the +10% |
| **Worms** | ~3 C$ | +10% Lure Speed, 0% Luck | Speed-farming C$ in a known good spot | Hunting rare fish — zero Luck means zero help catching better fish |

Here's when to change your bait strategy as you progress:
