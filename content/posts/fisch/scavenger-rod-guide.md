You dock at the oil rig off Rosslit Bay, climb the metal stairs to the top floor, and turn right into the Laboratory. A scientist named Dr. Crookspine is hunched over a bench, and instead of asking for something rare or expensive, he wants junk. Two Silver Rusty Bolts, five Scrap Metal, one Lunar String. Bring him the trash he asked for and he hands over the Scavenger Rod.

It sounds almost too easy, and for once, it is. The Scavenger Rod is the cheapest and least painful of the three new Astral Observatory rods, and its passive makes it one of the better mid-game money rods you can grab in under an hour.

## Why the Scavenger Rod Is the Best Value in the Astral Update

The Astral Observatory update dropped around August 22, 2026, with three new rods. Most of them want hard fights or expensive materials. The Scavenger Rod asks for none of that.

Here is the trade. You fish in three zones you already know, force a couple of mutations, and walk away with a rod that has 100% Luck, 75% Lure Speed, and a 3x chance for Rare Finds. That passive is the whole point. Rare Finds are where mid-game money comes from, and this rod triples how often you roll them.

Compare that to the other two new rods. The [Meteoric Rod](/posts/fisch/meteoric-rod-guide/) and [Rod of Singularity](/posts/fisch/rod-of-singularity-guide/) both want more from you. The Scavenger Rod is the one you can realistically finish the same session you start it.

## Where to Find Dr. Crookspine

Dr. Crookspine sits at the oil rig off the coast of Rosslit Bay. Sail out, dock at the rig, and go to the top of the main building. Enter, then turn right. He is in the Laboratory room.

Talk to him and he gives you the three-part quest. You can turn in each part as you finish it or bring all three at once. The quest marker points at the rig, so you will not get lost on the way there.

## The Three-Part Quest, Step by Step

The three items are not hard to catch. The hard part is remembering the mutation requirements, because two of the three items need a specific mutation on top of the base catch.

### Part 1: 2 Silver Rusty Bolts

Fish in the **Mine Shaft** zone. Use the **Titanium Rod** with **Garbage Bait**. The Rusty Bolt is a low-rarity catch, so it shows up often.

The catch is the word "Silver." The bolt must carry the **Silver** mutation. A plain Rusty Bolt does not count. To force Silver, equip **Mutated Sharky** bait or appraise with a **Mutation Totem**. If you have the **Toxin Burst** rod, its extra catches per cast make this step noticeably faster.

### Part 2: 5 Scrap Metal

Fish in **The Depths during daytime**. Any rod works, and you want **Garbage Bait** again. Scrap Metal is low rarity and appears constantly. There is no mutation requirement here, so this part is just a time sink. Fish until you have five and move on.

### Part 3: 1 Lunar String

Fish in **Desolate Deep's Desolate Pocket**. This one has no bait or weather preference, so don't waste time chasing a specific condition. The requirement is the **Lunar** mutation.

The **Moonlight Rod** applies Lunar to every catch, which turns this part into a single cast. If you don't have it, use **Mutated Sharky** bait or a **Mutation Totem** to force the mutation. Catch one string, confirm the Lunar roll, and you are done.

Return all three sets to Dr. Crookspine and he gives you the Scavenger Rod.

## Scavenger Rod Stats and the Rare-Finds Passive

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Lure Speed | 75% |
| Luck | 100% |
| Resilience | 20% |
| Max Kg | 75,000 |
| Scavenged mutation | 40% chance, 3x multiplier |
| Passive | 3x chance for Rare Finds |

The headline here is the passive. A 3x chance for Rare Finds means this rod pays for itself fast if you use it on the right spots. The 40% Scavenged mutation adds a 3x sell multiplier on top of that, so a good catch can come out worth far more than its base price.

Resilience of 20% is the weak point. You will not hold huge fish as easily as you would with a tank rod, but for mid-game money fishing that is rarely the bottleneck. Lure Speed 75% and Luck 100% mean you catch fast and roll good rarities often.

## What to Prepare Before You Start

The quest is quick once you stop re-rolling, and almost all of that speed comes from what you bring before you sail anywhere.

Pack, in order: **Mutated Sharky** bait (or a spare Mutation Totem) so the Silver and Lunar rolls are forced instead of gambled; the **Titanium Rod** for the Mine Shaft, because its lure profile is what the bolt drop is tuned around; and the **Moonlight Rod** if you own it, which makes the Lunar String part a single cast. That is the whole shopping list. Everything else in your loadout is optional.

Realistic times: Scrap Metal takes about two to five minutes in The Depths, the Lunar String is one cast with the Moonlight Rod or five to ten minutes forcing Lunar without it, and the bolts are the longest part — plan ten to twenty minutes in the Mine Shaft with a totem or Mutated Sharky, longer if you fish them raw. Do the bolts last, keep your two best totems for them, and you are usually done inside an hour.

## What Can Go Wrong (and How to Recover)

**Error: fishing the wrong zone for Rusty Bolts.** The quest text says "bolts," so players fish the rig or Rosslit Bay itself. You catch nothing but common fish for half an hour.
**Cause:** Rusty Bolts spawn in the Mine Shaft, not the rig.
**Recovery:** Go to the Mine Shaft zone, switch to the Titanium Rod with Garbage Bait, and watch them start dropping.

**Error: forgetting the mutation and appraising away the item.** You catch a Rusty Bolt, get excited, and appraise or sell it. You handed away the very thing you needed.
**Cause:** the quest needs Silver on the bolt and Lunar on the string, not just the base item.
**Recovery:** force the mutation before you fish. Mutated Sharky or a Mutation Totem means the catch comes out correct the first time, so there is nothing to accidentally appraise away.

**Error: using high-luck bait where it hurts.** Players stack luck bait to speed up Scrap Metal and Rusty Bolt drops.
**Cause:** these are low-rarity items, so more luck pulls in rarer fish that crowd out what you actually want. Garbage Bait is the correct choice for both.
**Recovery:** swap to Garbage Bait, keep the Titanium Rod for bolts, and the items start showing up in fewer casts.

## The Counter-Intuitive Tip: Stop Re-Fishing, Start Forcing

Most players treat the mutation requirement as a grind. They fish Rusty Bolts over and over, appraising each one, hoping the Silver roll eventually lands. That is the slow, frustrating way to do this quest.

The fast way is to force the mutation up front. A **Mutation Totem** at the appraiser, or **Mutated Sharky** bait, makes the next catch come out with the mutation already applied. For Lunar String, the **Moonlight Rod** makes Lunar automatic on every cast. One totem or one bait swap beats an hour of re-fishing a low-rarity item and hoping.

The same logic applies to bait. Players assume more luck is always better, but for low-rarity quest items, Garbage Bait is the correct call because it keeps the pool clean.
