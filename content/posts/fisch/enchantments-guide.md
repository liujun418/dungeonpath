## How Enchantments Work in Fisch

Enchantments are permanent stat boosts applied to your fishing rod using an **Enchant Relic** at the **Anvil NPC**. The Anvil is located in the blacksmith area at **Moosewood**, the starting island. You can find it next to the weapon rack, identifiable by the anvil icon on the minimap.

### The Enchantment Process

1. **Obtain an Enchant Relic** — these are rare items that can be fished up or purchased
2. **Travel to Moosewood** — the Anvil NPC is at the blacksmith area behind Pierre
3. **Approach the Anvil** and interact with it while holding your rod
4. **Select the Enchant Relic** from your inventory to consume it
5. **Your rod receives a random enchantment** — the result is random from the pool of available enchantments
6. **Check your rod's new stats** in the inventory menu to confirm the enchantment applied

**Critical Rule:** Each rod can hold only **ONE enchantment** at a time. Applying a new enchantment completely replaces the existing one. There is no way to remove an enchantment without overwriting it, so think carefully before re-rolling a rare rod.

### How to Get Enchant Relics

| Method | Success Rate | Time Investment | Notes |
|--------|-------------|----------------|-------|
| Fishing (open water) | ~0.5% base chance | 3-8 hours per relic | Higher luck increases drop rate significantly |
| Fishing with Luck Boost | ~1-2% with 200%+ luck | 1-3 hours per relic | Use best bait + Lucky enchant + luck potions |
| Purchasing from Players | 100% (trade) | Instant | Price varies: 5,000-15,000 C$ in trading hub |
| Event Rewards | Varies | Event-dependent | Some events offer relics as milestone rewards |
| Treasure Chests | ~2% | Variable | Rare drop from ocean chests at random locations |

For detailed rod recommendations, see the [Fisch Best Rods Tier List](/posts/fisch/best-rods-tier-list/) before choosing which rod to enchant.

## Every Enchantment Analyzed

There are **8 enchantments** currently available in Fisch. Each one modifies a specific rod stat or adds a unique proc effect. Below is every enchantment with exact numbers, use cases, and tier ranking.

### S-Tier Enchantments

#### Divine

**The best money-making enchantment in Fisch.**

| Stat | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Proc Chance | 10% |
| Effect | Fish sells at **5x value** |
| Best Rods | Mythic Rod, Ethereal Prism Rod, Masterline Rod |
| Tier | **S+ (Money)** |

Divine triggers on approximately 1 in 10 catches. When it procs on a high-value fish, you effectively earn 5 catches worth of coins from a single fish. On the Mythic Rod with its base 60% mutation rate, a Mythic-mutated fish worth 10,000 C$ becomes a 50,000 C$ payday when Divine procs.

**The Math:** If you average 1,000 C$ per fish and catch one fish every 30 seconds with Divine, you earn:
- Without Divine: 2,000 C$ per minute
- With Divine (10% proc): 2,000 C$ + (1,000 x 5 x 0.1) x 2 fish/min = 3,000 C$ effective per minute
- That is a **50% effective income increase** over no enchantment

For maximum income, pair Divine with the best money-making strategies from the [Fisch Money Farming Guide](/posts/fisch/money-farming-guide/).

#### Lucky

**The best enchantment for catching rare fish and increasing overall catch quality.**

| Stat | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Luck Boost | +45 |
| Effect | Increases chance of rare fish + mutations |
| Best Rods | Destiny Rod, Hades' Soul-Scythe, No-Life Rod |
| Tier | **S (Rare Farming)** |

Lucky gives a flat +45 to your rod's luck stat. This compounds with your rod's base luck to significantly increase your chances of hooking rare fish and rolling mutations. On a rod like the Destiny Rod (base 180% luck), Lucky pushes luck to 225%, which puts you in the top tier for exotic fish spawns.

**Why Lucky Can Be Better Than Mutated:** While Mutated boosts mutation rate specifically, Lucky improves your entire catch table. You get more rare fish AND more mutations. The broad improvement makes Lucky the better all-rounder.

#### Hasty

**Maximizes fish-per-hour by reducing reel time.**

| Stat | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Reel Speed Boost | +55 |
| Effect | Fish reel in much faster |
| Best Rods | Fast Castbound, No-Life Rod |
| Tier | **S (Speed)** |

Hasty reduces the time spent in the reeling minigame by up to 50% depending on the fish. For volume-based farming strategies (catching many fish quickly rather than targeting specific rare fish), Hasty is unmatched. Combined with a rod that already has high lure speed like the Masterline Rod (1000% lure), Hasty creates the fastest catch cycle possible.

### A-Tier Enchantments

#### Mutated

**Specialized enchantment for mutation hunters.**

| Stat | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Mutation Rate Boost | +15% |
| Effect | Fish spawn with mutations more often |
| Best Rods | Trident Rod, Mythic Rod |
| Tier | **A (Mutation Hunting)** |

Mutated adds 15 percentage points to your mutation rate. This is particularly effective on the Trident Rod, which already has high mutation chance as a base stat. The Mutated enchantment stacks multiplicatively with luck boosts from bait, making it a strong choice for dedicated mutation farmers.

**Mutation Rate Formula:**
`Final Mutation Chance = Base Rod Mutation Rate + Mutated Bonus + (Luck Stat x 0.05)`

Example with Trident Rod (base 30% mutation rate):
- Trident Rod alone: 30%
- Trident Rod + Mutated: 45%
- Trident Rod + Mutated + 150% luck: 45% + 7.5% = 52.5%

#### Swift

**Best for players who hate waiting for bites.**

| Stat | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Lure Speed Boost | +55 |
| Effect | Fish bite significantly faster |
| Best Rods | Any rod with low base lure speed |
| Tier | **A (Quality of Life)** |

Swift reduces the time between casts by increasing how quickly fish approach your bait. On rods with low base lure speed (under 50%), Swift is transformative — it cuts wait time roughly in half. However, on rods that already have 99%+ lure speed (like Masterline or Castbound), Swift provides no additional benefit since lure speed is capped at 99%.

### B-Tier Enchantments

#### Magnetic

**Solid early-game enchantment for faster fishing.**

| Stat | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Bite Rate Boost | -55% wait time |
| Effect | Reduces time between fish bites |
| Best Rods | Early-game rods (Carbon, Steady, Plastic) |
| Tier | **B (Early Game)** |

Magnetic increases how frequently fish bite your line. While this sounds similar to Swift, Magnetic affects bite frequency (how often fish spawn near your bait) rather than lure speed (how fast they swim to it). The net effect is similar — more fish per hour — but Magnetic makes more of a difference when fishing in spots with low fish density.

For beginners using basic rods, Magnetic is often the best choice because rod stats are so low that any improvement has outsized impact.

#### Resilient

**For players who lose too many fish.**

| Stat | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Durability Drain Reduction | Slower line degradation |
| Effect | Fish escapes are less frequent |
| Best Rods | Rods with poor resilience |
| Tier | **B (Consistency)** |

Resilient reduces the rate at which your line's durability drains during the reeling minigame. This gives you more time to land difficult fish without them breaking the line. It is strictly a quality-of-life enchantment — it does not increase your catch value or quantity, but it does reduce frustration.

### C-Tier Enchantments

#### Unbreakable

**Niche use for AFK fishing only.**

| Stat | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Durability Loss | 0 (completely negated) |
| Effect | Rod never loses durability |
| Best Rods | Any rod used for AFK farming |
| Tier | **C (AFK Only)** |

Unbreakable makes it so your rod never loses durability from use. This is **completely irrelevant** for active players who repair their rods regularly, but it is the best enchantment for AFK fishing setups where you cannot repair. If you run AFK farms overnight, Unbreakable is the only choice.

**Worth noting:** The recent Fisch updates have reduced durability loss across all rods, making Unbreakable less valuable than it was in 2025. Unless you specifically AFK fish for 8+ hours at a time, choose a different enchantment.

## Fish Mutations: Complete Reference

Mutations are special visual effects and value multipliers that can appear on any fish you catch. They are one of the most important mechanics in Fisch because a single good mutation can multiply a fish's value dramatically.

### How Mutations Work

When you catch a fish, the game rolls for mutations sequentially. Each mutation has a base chance that is modified by your rod's luck stat, active bait bonuses, and any enchantments (like Mutated). Multiple mutations **can** stack on a single fish, though the chances of rolling 2+ rare mutations simultaneously are very low.

**Mutation Roll Order:**
1. Game determines if fish catches (based on luck + rod stats)
2. Game rolls for common mutations first (Albino, Silver, etc.)
3. Game rolls for rare mutations (Mythic, Abyssal, etc.)
4. Game rolls for conditional mutations (Sunken, Frozen, Electric — location-based)
5. All multipliers are applied multiplicatively to calculate final value

| **Sparkling** | Uncommon | Particle effects | 1.85x | Anywhere (rare roll) |
| **Shiny** | Uncommon | Reflective surface | 1.1x | Anywhere |

When multiple mutations apply to a single fish, the multipliers are **multiplied together**, not added.

| Mutations Applied | Multiplier Calculation | Final Multiplier |

| Mythic + Abyssal (if compatible) | 3.0 x 2.5 | **7.5x** |

A fish with a base value of 20,000 C$ and the Mythic + Sparkling + Albino combo would sell for **166,500 C$** — enough to buy several high-end rods with a single catch.

## 4 Counter-Intuitive Truths About Enchantments

### 1. Divine Is Not the Best Money Enchantment (For You, Yet)

**What you'd expect:** A 5x-value proc is obviously the best money enchantment — the Quick Reference table at the top crowns it for maximum coins per hour.

**Why it's wrong:** Divine multiplies the fish you already catch; it doesn't make you catch more. If your average fish sells for 200 C$, Divine's 10% proc adds about 80 C$ per catch — roughly 2,400 C$ per hour at 30 fish. Magnetic's bite boost, in contrast, nearly doubles your catch count in low-density spots, adding 5,000+ C$ per hour at the same location.

**The principle:** Value enchantments scale with fish value; quantity enchantments scale with catches. Until your average fish clears roughly 1,000 C$, the quantity side wins.

**When it's actually useful:** Once you're farming Grand Reef or Volcanic Vents with an average fish of 1,500 C$+, Divine pulls ahead — and stays ahead.

### 2. Selling Relics Is Often Better Than Using Them

**What you'd expect:** A relic is the thing you enchant with. Of course you use it.

**Why it's wrong:** Early game, one relic trades for 5,000-15,000 C$ — the same value as 1-3 hours of Carbon Rod fishing. Three relics nearly buy a Steady Rod (25,000 C$). A Steady Rod upgrades every cast you ever make; a random enchantment on a Plastic Rod upgrades nothing.

**The principle:** A relic is worth what it converts into. Used on a rod you'll replace this week, it converts into a memory. Sold, it converts into the rod that replaces it.

**When it's actually useful:** The moment you own a rod you'll keep for 20+ hours — Mythic, Trident, Destiny — stop selling and start enchanting.

### 3. Lucky Beats Mutated — Even for Mutation Hunting

**What you'd expect:** The enchantment literally named "Mutated" (+15% mutation rate) is the mutation-farming pick. Case closed.

**Why it's wrong:** Mutated raises mutation rate; Lucky raises mutation income. The part nobody spells out: mutations only pay off on fish worth something. A Mutated Common sells for 2x of almost nothing — 40 C$ instead of 20 C$. Lucky's +45 luck pulls more Rare and Legendary fish into your catch table, and those are the fish whose 2-3x mutations actually move your bank balance. On most rods, Lucky's rare-fish volume produces more mutated coins than Mutated's flat percentage.

**The principle:** Mutation farming is income, not a rate. A rate only matters when it multiplies something worth multiplying.

**When it's actually useful:** The Trident Rod is the exception — its high base mutation rate makes Mutated's +15% genuinely additive, which is why it's the one rod where Mutated can beat Lucky.

### 4. Keeping a "Bad" Roll Is Often the Winning Move

**What you'd expect:** Rolled Resilient when you wanted Divine? Re-roll. The Anvil exists for exactly that.

**Why it's wrong:** Every re-roll is a 1-in-8 gamble at a full relic's cost. Chasing one specific enchantment usually costs 6-8 relics — 30,000 to 120,000 C$ of tradable value — and the rods that "need" the perfect enchantment often don't have the stats to care. An S-tier enchantment on a rod you barely use underperforms an A-tier enchantment on the rod you fish with every day.

**The principle:** The enchantment is a multiplier; the rod is the base. Two usable rolls you actually keep beat one perfect roll you spend 40,000 C$ chasing.

**When it's actually useful:** Re-roll only when the current roll actively fights the rod's job — Unbreakable on your main farmer, Swift on a rod already at 99% lure speed — and when you hold spare relics, so the roll doesn't reset you to zero.

## Best Enchantment + Rod Combos

### For Money Farming: Mythic Rod + Divine

The undisputed king of coin generation. The Mythic Rod's base 60% mutation rate ensures almost every fish has some mutation bonus. When Divine procs, it multiplies an already mutated fish by 5x.

**Expected output:** 150,000-300,000 C$ per hour at Grand Reef or Volcanic Vents.

**Setup:**
- Rod: Mythic Rod
- Enchantment: Divine
- Bait: Seaweed (for luck) or Worm (for universal attraction)
- Location: Grand Reef (best mix of rare + common fish)
- Technique: Focus on quantity — Divine is a numbers game

### For Mutation Hunting: Trident Rod + Mutated

The Trident Rod's base 30% mutation rate plus Mutated's +15% pushes you to 45% minimum. With good luck boosts, you can reach 55%+ mutation rate.

**Expected output:** 40-60% of all catches will have at least one mutation.

**Setup:**
- Rod: Trident Rod
- Enchantment: Mutated
- Bait: Coral (luck boost) or Shrimp (universal)
- Location: Roslit Bay or Depths (high rare fish concentration)
- Technique: Keep every fish that has 2+ mutations; sell the rest

### For Rare Fish: Destiny Rod + Lucky

The Destiny Rod's built-in luck boost combined with Lucky enchantment gives the highest total luck stat of any non-endgame rod combination.

**Expected output:** Significantly more exotic and legendary-grade fish per hour compared to any other mid-game setup.

**Setup:**
- Rod: Destiny Rod
- Enchantment: Lucky
- Bait: Fish Head (rare fish bait) or Truffle Worm (high luck)
- Location: Grand Reef or Depths
- Technique: Target specific rare fish locations; use radar accessories

### For Endgame: Masterline Rod + Hasty

Once you have the Masterline Rod, its infinite weight capacity and 1000% lure speed already give you perfect utility. Hasty maximizes the catch-and-release cycle speed for the fastest farming possible.

**Expected output:** 200,000-400,000 C$ per hour at any location.

**Setup:**
- Rod: Masterline Rod
- Enchantment: Hasty
- Bait: Quality Bait (any high-tier bait for bonus luck)
- Location: Any high-density fishing spot
- Technique: Rapid cast, reel, appraise, repeat cycle

### For AFK Farming: Any Rod + Unbreakable

For overnight AFK sessions, Unbreakable is a requirement. Without it, your rod will break within 2-3 hours of continuous use.

**Setup:**
- Rod: Any rod with good base stats (No-Life Rod recommended for free option)
- Enchantment: Unbreakable
- Bait: Any cheap bait unlimited supply
- Location: Moosewood dock (safe, no aggressive fish)
- Duration: Up to 12 hours without rod failure

## Failure Analysis: How Players Wreck Their Enchantments

### Failure 1: The First-Relic Panic Burn

**The scene:** Hour three of your Fisch life. An Enchant Relic drops from an ocean chest — your very first one. You sprint to the Anvil and feed it to the Plastic Rod you've had since spawn. Two days later, the rod is a memory — and so is your only relic.

**What went wrong:** You treated a rare, tradable resource as an instant power-up. A relic is worth 5,000-15,000 C$ at the trading hub. On a Plastic Rod, every enchantment multiplies almost nothing.

**The fix:** Adopt a relic rule before you ever fish one up: relics go on rods you'll still be using in a week. The Steady Rod is the first worthwhile target; Plastic and Carbon rods are not.

### Failure 2: The Re-Roll Rage Spiral

**The scene:** You rolled Resilient on your Mythic Rod. It's not what you wanted, so you feed the Anvil another relic. Swift. Another. Magnetic. Three relics later, the rod still doesn't have Divine — and you've burned 30,000+ C$ of tradable value to get there.

**What went wrong:** Chasing one specific outcome when every roll is a 1-in-8 gamble. Landing Divine usually costs around 8 relics on average — more than most players farm in a week. The spiral usually ends with the player accepting something worse than the roll they were angry about.

**The fix:** Before the first roll, decide how many relics this rod deserves — two is a healthy budget. If both miss, keep the better of the two results and move on. Hasty, Lucky, and Swift are excellent consolation prizes, not failures.

### Failure 3: The Unenchanted New Rod

**The scene:** You finally buy the Masterline Rod, equip it, and head straight to Grand Reef. Three hours of fishing later you open your inventory and realize: no enchantment. The relic budget got spent on the rod itself.

**What went wrong:** Rod purchases consume your entire savings, and enchantments run on a separate resource that's easy to forget. An unenchanted rod performs at roughly 70-80% of its potential.

**The fix:** Every time you set a savings goal for a rod, add one Enchant Relic to the budget line. With the relic already secured, you enchant the new rod the same day you buy it.

### Failure 4: Unbreakable on the Wrong Rod

**The scene:** You tried one overnight AFK session, rolled Unbreakable, and now it sits on the rod you use for everything — including active money farming, where durability rarely matters and an income enchantment would.

**What went wrong:** Enchantments are per-rod, not per-activity. The enchantment that enables AFK farming actively weakens active play, because it occupies the slot of an income enchantment.

**The fix:** Keep one dedicated AFK rod with Unbreakable and put income enchantments on your main rod. If Unbreakable lands on your only good rod, it's not an emergency — keep fishing until your next relic drops, then re-roll once. Panic re-rolling is how Failure 2 starts.

## Advanced Enchantment Strategy

### The Relic Economy

Enchant Relics have a stable player-to-player trading value of approximately 5,000-15,000 C$ depending on supply. During events, relic prices can spike to 20,000+ C$ as demand increases. If you need coins quickly, consider selling excess relics rather than using them.

### When to Re-Roll

Re-rolling (applying a new enchantment over an existing one) is expensive but sometimes necessary. Here is when it makes sense:

| Scenario | Should You Re-Roll? | Reasoning |
|----------|--------------------|-----------|
| You got Unbreakable on an active rod | Yes | The weakest combat enchantment |
| You got Resilient on a high-resilience rod | Yes | Better options exist |
| You got Magnetic on a max lure rod | Maybe | Useless stat overlap |
| You got Lucky when you wanted Divine | Probably not | Lucky is S-tier anyway |
| You got Hasty on a volume-farming rod | No | Hasty is excellent |
| You got Mutated on a Trident Rod | No | Perfect synergy |

### Enchantment Progression Path

As you progress through Fisch, your enchantment needs change:

1. **Early Game (Plastic Rod to Steady Rod):** Magnetic or Swift — speed up the grind to afford better rods. Do not use rare relics here; only common-tier relics if you have spares.

2. **Mid Game (Mythic Rod to Destiny Rod):** Divine (for money) or Lucky (for rare hunting). This is where enchantments matter most because you are spending significant time with these rods.

3. **Late Game (Trident Rod, Hades' Soul-Scythe):** Divine or Mutated — maximize your per-fish value as you target specific mutations.

4. **End Game (Masterline Rod, Castbound):** Hasty or Lucky — squeeze every drop of efficiency from the best rods in the game.

## The 3-Relics Decision: Enchant, Sell, or Save?

**The situation:** You're mid-game with 60,000 C$ in the bank, a Steady Rod in your hands, and three Enchant Relics in storage. The Mythic Rod costs 110,000 C$. The trading hub will pay roughly 30,000 C$ for your relics. You can't do all three things at once — so which is it?

**Path A — Enchant the Steady Rod now.** Spend one relic (Magnetic or Swift is ideal for a low-stat rod) and fish harder. The faster catch cycle lifts you to roughly 15,000-18,000 C$ per hour, so the Mythic Rod comes in about 3-4 hours. The catch: when the Mythic arrives, the Steady Rod retires — and its enchantment retires with it. You still hold two relics for the new rod.

**Path B — Sell the relics and buy the Mythic Rod sooner.** Three relics at ~10,000 C$ each add ~30,000 C$ to your 60,000 C$ — the Mythic Rod is within a couple of hours of fishing. The catch: it arrives unenchanted. You're now running the most expensive rod you own at 70-80% of its potential while farming relics to fix that.

**Path C — Save everything and enchant nothing.** Keep grinding at your current rate. Slowest to the rod, and nothing gets wasted.

**Verdict:** Path A for most players. The relic you spend on the Steady Rod is the price of fishing faster, and fishing faster funds everything else. Path B only wins if event pricing pushes relics above 20,000 C$ each, or if you need the rod's raw stats immediately for a specific quest. Path C is the hoarder's trap: relics sitting in storage generate exactly zero C$ per hour. Whichever path you take, the re-roll table above sets the budget for the next step.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I have two enchantments on one rod?**
A: No. Each rod can hold exactly one enchantment at a time. Applying a new enchantment overwrites the existing one.

**Q: Does the enchantment stay if I dye my rod?**
A: Yes. Enchantments are tied to the rod, not its appearance. Dyeing, renaming, or adding accessories does not affect enchantments.

**Q: Can I transfer an enchantment to a new rod?**
A: No. Enchantments are consumed when applied. You must use a new Enchant Relic on each rod you want to enchant.

**Q: Do enchantments work on all rods?**
A: Yes, every rod can receive enchantments. However, some rods have stat caps that make certain enchantments wasted (e.g., Swift on a rod already at 99% lure speed).

**Q: Is there a way to guarantee a specific enchantment?**
A: No. Enchantment results are completely random. There are no methods, tricks, or glitches to influence which enchantment you receive.

**Q: Does the Anvil cost coins to use?**
A: Using the Anvil itself is free. The only cost is the Enchant Relic you consume in the process.
