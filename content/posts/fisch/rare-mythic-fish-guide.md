## How Rarity Works in Fisch

Fisch uses a layered rarity system. Each fish has a base catch rate modified by your rod, bait, enchantments, and environmental conditions.

| Rarity Tier | Base Catch Rate (with basic rod) | Typical Value |
|-------------|-------------------------------|---------------|
| Common | ~50-60% | 5-50 C$ |
| Uncommon | ~20-25% | 50-150 C$ |
| Rare | ~8-12% | 150-500 C$ |
| Legendary | ~2-4% | 500-2,000 C$ |
| Mythic | ~0.3-1% | 2,000-15,000+ C$ |

A 0.3% base rate becomes ~2-3% with a fully optimized Mythic hunting loadout — 10x better odds.

## Luck Stacking: The Full Formula

Total catch quality is determined by:

```
Effective Luck = (Rod Luck + Enchant Luck) × (1 + Bait Luck) × (1 + Server/Potion Boost)
```

Example with max setup:
```
(45% Destiny + 15% Divine) × (1 + 25% Night Shrimp) × (1 + 20% Luck Potion)
= 60% × 1.25 × 1.20
= 90% effective luck modifier
```

This turns a 0.5% base Mythic rate into a ~1% rate — double your odds. Every piece of the setup matters.

## Most Common Mythic Hunting Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It Wastes Your Time | Fix |
