You push open Door 23 and the hallway stretches into darkness, a single flickering bulb casting long shadows across the carpet. Your flashlight died three doors ago. You hear the distant screech — clean, not distorted — and your hand finds the closet handle before your brain registers the sound. Three seconds later, Rush blasts past. You exhale. The closet smells like old wood and static. You count to two, step out, and immediately spot Dupe's fake door across the hall — the number sits wrong, the font slightly thicker, no faint glow underneath. You walk past it. Door 24. Still alive.

This is the Hotel. Not a reflex test. A pattern-matching marathon where death comes from panic, not speed. Most players don't die because they were too slow. They die because they looked at Eyes while running from Rush, or hid from Ambush and came out too early, or sprinted past Figure because they thought "blind" meant "deaf." The Hotel punishes assumptions, not reaction time. The next 77 doors will test whether you can override your instincts when every neuron is screaming at you to panic.

## The Hotel Is a Memory Test, Not a Reaction Test

New players think DOORS requires fast reflexes. It doesn't. Rush gives you 3.5 seconds of warning. Ambush gives you 3.5 seconds before each pass. Screech gives you 3 seconds. Every entity telegraphs its arrival with a generous window.

The real skill is knowing what's coming before it arrives. At Door 9, Rush can spawn. At Door 13, Ambush can replace Rush. At Door 50, Figure. The players who clear the Hotel consistently aren't reacting faster. They're anticipating correctly.

## Doors 1-8: The Tutorial Zone

Rush CAN spawn but rarely does before Door 9. These first 8 doors are your practice zone. Use them to build the room entry protocol: enter, identify exit, note nearest closet, proceed. Do this for all 8 doors and it'll be automatic by the time Rush starts appearing.

**Door 2-3:** Your first dark room. Screech may appear — listen for "psst," spin 360°, look at it. This is also where you'll find your first items. Prioritize light sources and gold.

**Door 5-8:** Puzzle rooms may appear. Breaker panels, code locks, memory games. Solve the puzzle, collect the reward. No entities attack during puzzles.

**The most important thing to learn in Doors 1-8:** Closet discipline. Every time you enter a room, note the nearest closet. Not "somewhere in the room." The exact closet. When Rush screeches at Door 10, you don't want to be scanning the room for hiding spots with 3 seconds on the clock.

---

## Doors 9-49: The Gauntlet

This is the longest stretch of the Hotel — 40 doors of escalating entity encounters.

**Doors 9-20:** Rush is now active. Every screech could be real. Whenever you hear it, closet immediately. No hesitation. No "one more item." Screech appears in dark rooms. Ambush has a ~15% chance to replace Rush starting at Door 10 — if the screech sounds distorted or glitchy, stay in the closet until all passes are done.

**Doors 21-35:** Dupe appears — fake doors in hallways. The real door has a slightly different number font and a faint glow. Rush and Ambush continue. Eyes may appear — purple tint, look at the floor immediately.

**Doors 36-49:** Halt can appear in long corridors. Screen flashes blue (walk toward Halt), then red (turn 180° and run). The cycle repeats 3-5 times. Rush, Ambush, and Eyes continue. Seek's eyes appear on the walls as a warning that the first Seek chase is approaching.

**The mid-gate checkpoint:** If you can consistently reach Door 40, you have the fundamentals. The back half of the Hotel tests endurance more than skill.

## Door 50: The Library (Figure)

The full Figure protocol is covered in our dedicated guide — but here's the essential framework:

- Figure is blind. It tracks sound. Walk, don't run. Crouch when it listens.
- Collect 8 books from shelves around the Library. Place each on the central pedestal.
- Each book placement draws Figure to investigate — use this as a decoy.
- After the 8th book, the exit opens. Sprint immediately. Don't look back.

---

## Doors 51-100: The Endurance Test

You beat Figure. Now do everything again, but harder.

**Doors 51-70:** Rush, Ambush, Screech, Eyes, Dupe — all the entities you've faced before. Nothing new. Jeff's Shop appears around Door 52 — buy Medkits and light sources if you're low.

**Doors 70-90:** Second Seek chase. Same as the first but longer and with more hazards. Halt appears more frequently. The entity density increases — it's possible (though rare) to encounter two entities simultaneously.

**Doors 91-99:** Maximum difficulty. Rush and Ambush can appear in rapid succession. Dark rooms are more common. Guiding Light's sparkles are dimmer. This is the final push.

**Door 100:** The Electrical Room. Second Figure encounter. Circuit breaker puzzle — memorize the pattern, replicate it. Figure patrols the room. Same protocol as Door 50: walk, crouch, freeze. Solve the puzzle. The exit opens. You've cleared the Hotel.

## Failure Analysis: Why You Actually Died

**"I had plenty of time" — Door 9-20 Rush deaths**
You heard the screech, you saw the closet, you kept looting for one more second. Rush travels at 60 studs per second. In a 20-stud room, that 3.5-second warning shrinks to under 2 seconds if you are not near cover. The death isn't mechanical. It's greed.

**"I came out after the first pass" — Ambush deaths**
Ambush's audio cue is distorted, but the distortion is subtle on laptop speakers. Players treat it like Rush, exit after one pass, and die on the return. The fix isn't better headphones. It's counting to three between passes regardless of audio confidence.

**"I looked up for one second" — Eyes deaths in high-stress rooms**
Eyes spawns most often when another entity just passed. Players exit closets after Rush, adrenaline high, scan the room for loot, and lock eyes with purple. Eyes deals damage per frame of eye contact. At 60 FPS, "one second" is 60 damage ticks. You're dead before you process what happened.

**"I beat Door 50, I know Figure" — Door 100 deaths**
Door 100's Figure has the same AI but a different layout. The circuit breaker puzzle forces you to stand still at panels. Standing still near Figure is safe only if you are crouched AND he is in patrol phase. If he enters search phase while you are mid-puzzle, walking away preserves life. Sprinting confirms death.

**"I bought everything at Jeff's" — Door 70+ resource deaths**
Jeff's Shop at Door 52 tempts players into hoarding. Three medkits, two lighters, full inventory. Then Door 70 hits with double-Rush and no closet nearby because you were slow from full inventory. Over-preparation slows movement. Movement speed is a hidden survivability stat.

## Decision Framework: The 2-Second Rule

Every entity gives you a window. The window is never less than 2 seconds if you are paying attention. Here is the exact protocol for each stimulus:

**Auditory Stimulus: Screech**
- Clean tone, no reverb distortion → Rush. Enter nearest closet. Wait for pass. Count "one-one-thousand, two-one-thousand." Exit.
- Distorted, glitchy, or layered tone → Ambush. Enter nearest closet. Count passes. Minimum 2 passes, usually 3-5. Exit only after 3 full seconds of silence.

**Visual Stimulus: Purple Tint**
- Immediate action: Look at floor. Do not look up for loot. Do not look at doors. Navigate using peripheral vision and wall contact. Wait for tint to fully fade before normal vision resumes.

**Environmental Stimulus: Darkness**
- Listen for "psst." If heard, spin 360° at moderate speed. Locate glowing eyes. Look directly at them.
- If no "psst," equip light source. Move to nearest wall. Follow wall to exit. Do not cross room center.

**Movement Stimulus: Flashing Corridor**
- Blue flash → Walk toward the figure. Do not sprint. Sprinting overshoots the turn window.
- Red flash → Turn 180° immediately. Sprint away. Do not look back. The next flash determines next action.

**Proximity Stimulus: Heavy Footsteps (Figure)**
- Footsteps audible but not close → Walk only. Never run. Running's audio radius is 40 studs. Figure detects at 35 studs.
- Footsteps stop → Crouch immediately. Hold crouch until footsteps resume or 5 seconds pass.
- Already detected → Sprint to break line of sight. Do not crouch. Crouching while detected reduces escape speed below Figure's search speed.

## Counter-Intuitive: What Looks Wrong But Saves Your Life

**1. Empty inventory is safer than full inventory**
Jeff's Shop sells survival, but every item you carry reduces sprint duration and turning responsiveness. A player with one lighter and no medkit moves faster through Dupe hallways than a player with three medkits and two lockpicks. If you are at Door 80 with full inventory, consider deliberately leaving non-essential items behind. Speed is survivability.

**2. Looking at Screech heals you**
Screech deals damage over time until you make eye contact. Most players panic-spin and miss the eyes. The counter-intuitive play is to stand still, listen for the directional audio cue, then turn once deliberately. A controlled 180° is faster than a panicked 360° that overshoots.

**3. Walking toward Halt is the correct move**
When Halt's corridor flashes blue and the figure appears ahead, your survival instinct screams "run away." Running away on blue guarantees the red flash catches you facing the wrong direction. The game wants you to walk into the blue. It feels like walking into death. It is the only way to survive.

**4. Making noise near Figure can save you**
Figure is blind but tracks sound. However, when Figure enters its "search" phase after hearing a noise, it moves to the noise origin and stays there for 8 seconds. A deliberate noise — dropping a book or walking briefly — can pull Figure to the opposite side of the Library, creating a safe corridor for you to reach a distant bookshelf. Quiet players get pinned. Noisy players control the map.
