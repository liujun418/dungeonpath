You take a step forward onto a winding stone path, surrounded by hedges trimmed to perfection. Flowers bloom in organized beds. For a moment, you think the game has glitched — this can't be DOORS.

Then the Groundskeeper materializes between two monuments.

It does not rush at you. It does not scream. It just stands there, slowly raising one arm to point directly at your position. You freeze. Your left foot is resting on a patch of grass. The Groundskeeper's gaze locks onto that foot. You scramble backward onto the stone just as the screen flashes red. You survived by a hair.

This is The Outdoors: DOORS Floor 3. It lures you into a false sense of security with sunshine and open skies, then punishes the slightest misstep with instant death. Every path, every monument, every open field hides a trap. But with the right strategy, you can navigate this beautiful nightmare and reach the exit in one piece.

## What Is The Outdoors?

**The Outdoors** is the third floor of DOORS, representing a dramatic departure from the enclosed hotel corridors and mine tunnels that define the earlier floors. Instead of claustrophobic indoor spaces, The Outdoors places you in a sprawling, garden-themed environment with open pathways, hedge mazes, monuments, and natural hazards.

Despite its serene visual design, The Outdoors is arguably the most mechanically complex floor in DOORS. The entities here are more numerous, the environmental hazards are unforgiving, and the final boss fight demands mastery of every skill you've learned in Floors 1 and 2.

### How to Access The Outdoors

There are two ways to access The Outdoors. **From the Lobby**, spend 10 Stardust to unlock it permanently. **Through story progression**, complete the storyline by finding all Lotus Petals and crafting a Lotus Flower.

**Stardust** is earned through completing runs, achievements, and special events. The 10 Stardust cost is a one-time unlock -- once purchased, The Outdoors is permanently available.

## Door-by-Door Walkthrough

### Early Outdoors (First Doors): The Garden Entrance

The opening section of The Outdoors establishes the garden theme and introduces the floor's core mechanic: **environmental awareness**.

**What to expect:**
1. You emerge from an underground exit into a lush garden area
2. The first doors lead through manicured paths and flower beds
3. **Groundskeeper** appears early and establishes the "don't step on grass" rule
4. Standard entities (Rush, Screech) appear to ease you in

| Door Range | Key Encounters | Strategy |
|------------|---------------|----------|

| **11-20** | Mandrake encounters, Caws | Learn Mandrake retrieval; feed bread to Caws |
| **21-30** | Monument puzzles begin | Study monument patterns; interact carefully |

### The Groundskeeper: Core Mechanic Introduction

**The Groundskeeper** is the defining entity of The Outdoors and introduces the floor's most important rule:

**DO NOT STEP ON THE GRASS when the Groundskeeper can see you.**

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Warning** | Groundskeeper points toward monuments or your position |
| **Location** | Throughout the garden areas |
| **Attack** | Kills you instantly if it sees you walking on grass |
| **Survival** | **Stay on designated paths** when Groundskeeper is active |
| **Danger Level** | ★★★★★ |

The Groundskeeper changes how you navigate the entire floor. Every movement decision must account for:
- Where the Groundskeeper is looking
- Whether your current position is on grass or a path
- The safest route to the next door

**Strategy:** When you see the Groundskeeper, **freeze immediately** and check your footing. If you're on a path, stay there. If you're on grass, wait for the Groundskeeper to look away, then carefully step back onto the path.

#### Mandrake

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Warning** | Screaming plant appears on paths |
| **Attack** | Continuous scream drains HP and can kill |
| **Survival** | **Pick up the Mandrake and return it to its hole** before the scream kills you |
| **Danger Level** | ★★★★ |

Mandrake is a time-pressured puzzle entity. When it appears, it begins screaming immediately. You must:
1. **Approach the Mandrake quickly**
2. **Pick it up** (interact prompt)
3. **Find its hole** (usually nearby, glowing)
4. **Place it back** before your HP reaches zero

**Pro tip:** The hole is always within a short radius of where the Mandrake spawns. Don't run too far -- check the immediate area first.

#### Monument

Monuments are puzzle entities. They present a pattern or sequence that you must replicate. Interacting with the monument out of sequence triggers a possession effect that damages you and disorients your controls.

|---------------------|--------------|

#### Surge

| Attribute | Detail |

Surge is an area-denial entity. When it activates, lightning strikes open areas. You need to find cover behind monuments, structures, or other objects. The key is to **recognize the warning signs early** and reach cover before the strike.

### The Hedge Maze Section (Late Outdoors)

One of the most distinctive features of The Outdoors is the **Hedge Maze**, which appears in the late section of the floor.

| Hedge Maze Feature | Detail |
|-------------------|--------|
| **Entrance** | Marked by a stone archway |
| **Layout** | Multiple dead ends with one correct path |
| **Hazards** | World Lotus vines block the exit |
| **Boss Location** | The center contains the **World Lotus** final boss |

**Navigation strategy:**
1. **Use the right-hand rule** -- keep your right hand on the hedge wall at all times and follow it
2. **Mark dead ends mentally** -- so you don't repeat mistakes on future attempts
3. **Watch for visual cues** -- the correct path sometimes has subtle markers (different colored leaves, light patterns)
4. **Stay on paths** -- the Groundskeeper may still be active in the maze

### Final Doors and World Lotus (Boss Fight)

The final confrontation with the **World Lotus** takes place at the center of the Hedge Maze.

**How the boss fight works:**
1. You reach the center of the Hedge Maze
2. The World Lotus activates, covering the exit with vines
3. You must **collect Lotus Petals** (8 total) scattered around the area
4. Each petal collection triggers a hazard phase
5. After collecting all petals, you can **craft the Lotus Flower** to complete the floor

| Boss Phase | Challenge | Strategy |
|------------|-----------|----------|
| **Phase 1: Petal Search** | Find Lotus Petals around the maze center | Scope the area quickly; petals glow faintly |
| **Phase 2: Hazard Activation** | Entities spawn when you pick up petals | Use Crucifix for protection; stay mobile |
| **Phase 3: Vine Barrier** | World Lotus blocks the exit with vines | Craft Lotus Flower from collected petals to clear |
| **Phase 4: Escape** | Run to the exit while entities pursue | Use Vitamins for speed; follow the path |

**Detailed boss strategy:**
1. **Before picking up petals**, walk the perimeter and note all petal locations
2. **Plan your collection order** -- start with the closest petal, work outward
3. **Use a Crucifix** if entities swarm you during petal collection
4. **Save Vitamins for Phase 4** -- the escape chase
5. **Don't step on grass** -- even during the boss fight, the Groundskeeper may be watching

## Tough Choices: Decision Framework for The Outdoors

The Outdoors constantly forces you to make hard calls with limited resources. Here are the three most common dilemmas and how to resolve them.

### Dilemma 1: Crucifix vs. Vitamins — Only 500 Gold, Which Do You Buy?

You arrive at Jeff's Shop with exactly 500 gold. The Crucifix costs 500. Vitamins cost 100. You cannot afford both. What do you do?

**Buy the Crucifix if** you are confident in your path navigation but worried about entity swarms during the boss fight. A single Crucifix blocks entities during the petal collection phase — without it, one well-timed Rush can wipe your progress and force a full restart.

**Buy Vitamins and Bandages (400 gold total) if** you die more often to Mandrake's scream or the final escape chase than to entity swarms. Vitamins make Phase 4 of the boss fight almost trivial, and Bandages keep you alive through Mandrake encounters.

**The right call depends on your weakness.** If you have never died to the final chase, buy the Crucifix. If the boss escape regularly kills you, buy the Vitamins.

### Dilemma 2: Take the Known Safe Path or Risk the Shortcut?

You spot a gap in the hedge that looks like it cuts straight to the next door. It crosses a patch of grass. The Groundskeeper is visible nearby but looking away. The shortcut saves 15 seconds. Do you take it?

**Never take the shortcut.** The Groundskeeper's line of sight is unpredictable — it can snap its head around mid-step with no warning. The 15 seconds you save are not worth the instant death. Always stay on designated stone or dirt paths, even when they add time. One mistake and the run is over.

### Dilemma 3: Collect a Lotus Petal Now or Clear Entities First?

A petal is within arm's reach, but you hear Rush approaching. Grabbing it now saves 10 seconds of backtracking. Hiding and coming back costs time. What do you do?

**Hide first, always.** Petals do not despawn. Rush can kill you while you are animation-locked picking up a petal — the interaction animation leaves you unable to move or hide. Wait for the entity to pass, then collect in safety. The 10 seconds you lose are nothing compared to a full restart.

## Counter-Intuitive Tips That Will Save Your Run

Some of the most effective strategies in The Outdoors go against everything you learned in The Hotel and The Mines.

### 1. Do Not Hide from the Groundskeeper — Stand Your Ground

In Floors 1 and 2, the correct response to any entity is "find a closet or bed and hide." The Outdoors breaks this pattern completely. When the Groundskeeper appears, **the safest position is out in the open on a path** — as long as you stay completely still. Running for a closet draws attention to your movement. Freeze in place, wait for the Groundskeeper to look away, and only then continue moving.

### 2. Bread Is Not Just for Birds — It Is a Distraction Tool

Most players treat Bread as a one-purpose item for the Caws achievement. But Bread has a hidden use: dropping it on the ground can distract certain entities for a few seconds, giving you time to cross dangerous open areas. This is not documented anywhere in-game — it was discovered by the speedrunning community through trial and error. Carry an extra loaf even after you have fed the birds.

### 3. The Right-Hand Rule Can Fail in the Hedge Maze

Conventional maze wisdom says "always follow the right wall." In The Outdoors Hedge Maze, this works about 80% of the time. But the maze has a section where following the right wall loops you back to the entrance. The fix: if you recognize a dead end you passed 30 seconds ago, switch to the **left-hand rule** for the next three turns. The maze designer deliberately broke the single-hand rule to catch veterans who stopped paying attention to their surroundings.

### 4. Save Your Crucifix for Petal Collection, Not the Boss

It feels natural to hoard your Crucifix for the World Lotus encounter — the final boss should be the hardest part, right? Wrong. The hardest phase of the boss fight is the petal collection (Phase 2), not the escape. Pop your Crucifix when you are picking up petals 4 or 5, when entity spawns peak. This creates a safe zone for collecting the remaining petals. You do not need the Crucifix for the final escape — Vitamins handle that.

## All Entities in The Outdoors

### Outdoors-Exclusive Entities

#### Groundskeeper

The Groundskeeper is the most lethal entity in The Outdoors because its kill condition is instant and applies across the entire floor. There is no dodging, no hiding from the attack itself -- the only defense is **prevention through path awareness**.

- **Warning signs:** Points at monuments or players; slow movement
- **Location:** Throughout garden and path areas
- **Attack:** Instantly kills players caught walking on grass
- **Survival:** Stay on paths; freeze when Groundskeeper is looking at you
- **Danger Level:** ★★★★★

#### Mandrake

- **Warning:** Appears on paths with immediate screaming
- **Location:** Path intersections and open areas
- **Attack:** Continuous scream drains HP rapidly
- **Survival:** Pick up and return to hole before HP reaches zero
- **Danger Level:** ★★★★

#### Monument

- **Warning:** Glowing pillar activates
- **Location:** Plaza areas and open spaces
- **Attack:** Possession effect from incorrect interaction
- **Survival:** Solve puzzle patterns correctly
- **Danger Level:** ★★★

#### Surge

- **Warning:** Electrical crackling; sky darkens
- **Location:** Open areas without cover
- **Attack:** Lightning strikes in open spaces
- **Survival:** Hide behind structures when lightning approaches
- **Danger Level:** ★★★★

#### Caws

- **Warning:** Bird sounds (cawing) in garden areas
- **Location:** Trees and garden perches
- **Attack:** None -- passive entity
- **Survival:** **Feed Bread** to get the "Feed The Birds" achievement
- **Danger Level:** ☆

Caws is the only non-hostile exclusive entity in The Outdoors. It exists primarily for the **achievement** -- carry Bread in your inventory and interact with a Caw to feed it.

#### World Lotus

The World Lotus is the floor's final boss. It does not directly attack you but creates increasingly dangerous conditions as you collect Lotus Petals. The fight combines elements from every entity you have faced in The Outdoors.

- **Warning:** Final boss at Hedge Maze center
- **Location:** Center of the Hedge Maze
- **Attack:** Spawns vines and entities during boss phases
- **Survival:** Collect 8 Lotus Petals, craft Lotus Flower, escape
- **Danger Level:** ★★★★★

### Returning Entities in The Outdoors

Most returning entities behave identically to their Hotel and Mines counterparts, with a few environmental adjustments: **Rush** and **Ambush** work the same way — hide in the nearest cover. **Screech** appears in darker garden areas; spin your camera rapidly to shake it off. **Seek** triggers chases through garden paths — follow the glowing markers. **Figure** may appear in certain sections; crouch-walk and use sound awareness. **Dupe** creates fake doors in garden structures, so count and verify door numbers before entering.

|--------|--------|-----------|
| **Grass on paths** | Instant death if Groundskeeper sees you walking on it | Stay on designated stone/dirt paths |

### Hedge Maze Dead Ends

| Hazard | Effect | Avoidance |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| **Wrong turns** | Waste time, expose you to entities | Use right-hand rule; mark dead ends |

| Hazard | Effect | Avoidance |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| **Open sky areas** | Surge lightning strikes | Stay near monuments or structures |
| **Exposed paths** | Vulnerable during Surge events | Wait out Surge behind cover |

### Environmental Hazard Priority

Not all hazards are equal. Ranked by threat level:

- **Grass (Groundskeeper):** Lethality instant death, frequency constant, mitigation easy (stay on paths). This is your number one priority at all times.
- **Lightning (Surge):** High damage, periodic frequency, medium mitigation (find cover). Dangerous but predictable.
- **Hedge Maze dead ends:** Time waste, constant frequency, easy mitigation (right-hand rule). Annoying but rarely lethal.
- **Mandrake scream:** HP drain, periodic frequency, medium mitigation (quick retrieval). Deadly if ignored, manageable if handled promptly.

## Tips for Surviving The Outdoors

### Core Survival Strategies

Master these seven rules and you will survive most of what The Outdoors throws at you:

- **Stay on paths** at all times. The Groundskeeper kills on grass sight, period. This matters every moment you are in The Outdoors.
- **Carry Bread** in your inventory. It enables the Caws achievement and has a hidden distraction use. Check your inventory at Jeff's Shop.
- **Learn monument patterns** before touching them. Incorrect interaction causes possession. Practice the three monument puzzle types in the early section.
- **Save Vitamins for the escape.** The speed boost is crucial for the final chase. Do not waste Vitamins on early sections.
- **Collect all 8 Lotus Petals** systematically. They are required to craft the Lotus Flower and complete the floor. Use a clockwise search pattern.
- **Watch for Surge warnings** — the electrical crackling sound and sky darkening. Lightning strikes are fast, so stay near cover in open areas.
- **Handle Mandrake immediately** when it appears. The scream drains HP rapidly, so prioritize it over all other tasks.

### Multiplayer-Specific Tips

Playing with a team makes The Outdoors significantly easier. Coordinate these roles:

- **Split petal collection** during the boss fight — each player grabs petals from different areas to cut collection time by up to 50%.
- **Assign one person to watch the Groundskeeper** and call out when it is safe to move. This lets the rest of the team focus on puzzles and navigation.
- **Share Bread** so multiple players can feed Caws for the achievement. Only one loaf is needed per player.
- **Coordinate monument puzzles** — one player solves the pattern while others watch for approaching entities.
- **Revive during the boss fight.** Downed players can be recovered mid-fight, so do not abandon teammates who fall during petal collection.

## Common Outdoors Mistakes and How to Recover

Even experienced players wipe in The Outdoors. Here are the most common failure scenarios, what causes them, and how to recover when things go wrong.

### Mistake 1: Stepping on Grass While Running from an Entity

**What happens:** You are fleeing from Rush or Surge and instinctively cut across a patch of grass. The Groundskeeper catches you mid-stride. Instant death.

**Why it happens:** Panic overrides awareness. When an entity chases you, your brain prioritizes "get away" over "watch your footing." The grass blends into the path visually, especially in dim sections.

**How to prevent it:** Train yourself to check the ground before every movement, even during chases. The stone paths have a distinct texture — lighter gray with visible cracks. Grass is darker and has a green tint. If you are unsure, stop moving and look down first.

**How to recover if it happens:** If you step on grass and the Groundskeeper is not looking directly at you, step back onto the path immediately. You have roughly 1 second before the Groundskeeper registers the violation. Do not run — a single controlled step backward is enough.

### Mistake 2: Ignoring Mandrake to Deal with Other Threats First

**What happens:** You see Mandrake appear but decide to handle Rush first. By the time you get back to the Mandrake, your HP is already drained to zero from the continuous scream.

**Why it happens:** Mandrake's threat is subtle — it does not chase you or deal burst damage. Players underestimate the scream's DPS and prioritize flashier threats like Rush or Ambush.

**How to prevent it:** Treat Mandrake as the highest-priority threat in any encounter. The scream deals damage every second, and the total HP pool is only 100. You have roughly 10 seconds from spawn to death. Handle Mandrake before anything else.

**How to recover:** If you are at 50% HP or below and the Mandrake is still screaming, pop a Green Herb or Bandage immediately to buy time. Then grab the Mandrake and find its hole. Do not search for the hole while at critical HP — heal first, then solve the puzzle.

### Mistake 3: Touching the Monument Before Understanding the Pattern

**What happens:** You see a glowing Monument and press the interact button immediately. The Monument activates a possession effect that disorients your controls and deals damage over time.

**Why it happens:** The game trains you to interact with everything. In The Hotel, touching objects is safe. The Monument punishes this conditioned behavior.

**How to prevent it:** Every Monument displays its pattern before requiring input. Watch the full sequence — it takes 3-5 seconds. Only interact once you understand the order. If you are unsure, wait for the Monument to repeat the pattern.

**How to recover:** If you trigger the possession effect, immediately stop moving and let the disorientation pass. The effect lasts 3-4 seconds. Do not try to fight the controls — moving randomly during possession often leads to stepping on grass.

### Mistake 4: Collecting Lotus Petals in Random Order During the Boss Fight

**What happens:** You grab the closest petal, then the next closest, then realize you have to cross the entire arena to reach the remaining ones. Entities spawn and swarm you mid-crossing.

**Why it happens:** Excitement and pressure. The World Lotus encounter feels urgent, so you grab whatever is nearest without planning.

**How to prevent it:** Before touching any petal, walk the full perimeter of the boss arena and memorize all 8 spawn locations. They are always in the same positions — learn them. Plan a clockwise or counter-clockwise route that minimizes backtracking.

**How to recover:** If you are mid-fight and realize you missed a petal on the far side, do not panic-run across the arena. Wait for a safe window (no entities active, Groundskeeper looking away), then move methodically to the missed location.

### Mistake 5: Wasting Vitamins Before the Final Escape

**What happens:** You pop Vitamins to dodge a Surge strike or outrun a Rush in the mid-section. Later, during the World Lotus escape chase, you have no speed boost and get caught.

**Why it happens:** Vitamins are common and cheap, so players use them casually. The game does not warn you about the final chase.

**How to prevent it:** Reserve at least one set of Vitamins specifically for the boss fight escape (Phase 4). The final chase is the only section where Vitamins are truly essential. Before that, use positioning and cover to handle threats.

**How to recover:** If you used all your Vitamins and reach the boss fight, focus on perfect pathing. Memorize the escape route during Phase 1-3 so you can run it without hesitation. Every second of hesitation during the chase costs you ground.

## FAQ

**Q: How do I unlock The Outdoors?**
A: Spend **10 Stardust** in the Lobby to unlock The Outdoors. Stardust is earned through completing runs, achievements, and special events.

**Q: What is the Groundskeeper and how do I survive it?**
A: The Groundskeeper kills you instantly if it sees you walking on grass. **Stay on designated stone/dirt paths at all times** and freeze when the Groundskeeper is looking in your direction.

**Q: How do I complete The Outdoors?**
A: Collect **8 Lotus Petals** scattered throughout the floor, craft a **Lotus Flower**, and use it to clear the World Lotus vines blocking the exit. Then escape to the final door.

**Q: What do I do with Mandrake?**
A: Pick up the Mandrake and return it to its glowing hole before the scream drains all your HP. The hole is always nearby.

**Q: How do I solve Monument puzzles?**
A: Monuments display a pattern or sequence that you must replicate. Watch the displayed pattern carefully, then interact with the monument in the correct sequence.

**Q: What is the World Lotus?**
A: The World Lotus is the final boss of The Outdoors, located at the center of the Hedge Maze. It spawns vines and entities during its boss phases and must be defeated by collecting Lotus Petals and crafting a Lotus Flower.

**Q: Can I skip The Outdoors?**
A: No, if you want to complete the full DOORS storyline, you need to complete The Outdoors. However, you can access other floors (The Hotel, The Mines) independently from the Lobby.

**Q: How many achievements are in The Outdoors?**
A: There are **8+ Outdoors-exclusive achievements**, including Touched Grass, Down The Water Spout, Do'h!, A Bone To Pick, Feed The Birds, Picking Flowers, Detour, and Rebirth.

**Q: Is The Outdoors harder than The Mines?**
A: It depends on playstyle. The Outdoors has more **environmental awareness** requirements (grass, monuments, surge) while The Mines has more intense **pressure-based encounters** (Figure hunting by sound). Many players find The Outdoors more punishing due to the instant-death grass mechanic.

**Q: What's the best route through The Outdoors?**
A: The optimal route minimizes time on open paths, uses the right-hand rule in the Hedge Maze, collects Lotus Petals in a systematic order, and saves Vitamins for the final escape. Practice the route to learn entity spawn patterns and optimal movement paths.
