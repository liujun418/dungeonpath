You are at Door 96. The hallway stretches ahead of you, dim and silent except for the low hum of the building itself. Your inventory is down to half a bandage, one lockpick, and a lighter flickering on its last fuel. Behind you, somewhere in the dark, the Figure is still hunting. You have made it this far four times before, and four times you have died — once to a Dupe door you swore was correct, once to an Ambush you left hiding from too early, once to Screech because your lighter died in a dark hallway, and once to the Figure because you panicked and sprinted across the library.

You told yourself you had memorized the rooms. You told yourself a walkthrough was all you needed. But the Hotel does not test memory. It tests composure, resource discipline, and the ability to adapt when the plan breaks. This guide is written for that fifth run. It will not just list rooms. It will teach you how to survive them.

## The Hotel: Floor 1 Overview

The Hotel is the first floor of DOORS and the experience most players encounter first. It consists of 100 numbered rooms (Door 1 through Door 100), each presenting unique challenges, entities, puzzles, and opportunities. Behind Door 100 awaits the Figure -- the floor's final boss entity.

This walkthrough covers every room, every entity encounter, every secret hiding spot, and the Jeff Shop layout. Whether you are running for your first completion badge or speedrunning, this guide has you covered. If you are looking for a quicker overview, our [Beginner Guide](/posts/doors/beginner-guide/) covers the absolute essentials.

## The Hotel Survival Framework

Use this framework to make the right decision at every stage of the run. Memorizing rooms helps, but knowing what *kind* of challenge you are facing is what keeps you alive.

**Door 1-10: Audio Boot Camp.** These rooms teach you the core audio-visual language of the Hotel. Rush introduces flickering lights. Hiding teaches closet and bed mechanics. Do not waste lockpicks here — keys are easy to find. Your only priority is grabbing every coin and learning to identify safe closets by the light seeping through the cracks.

**Door 10-30: Speed vs. Safety.** Seek can spawn anywhere in this range, usually between Doors 11 and 16. Keep vitamins in your hotbar before Door 10. If Halt appears in a long hallway, trust the mechanic: walk backward without turning your camera. Panic-turning is the leading cause of Halt deaths.

**Door 30-50: Resource Checkpoint.** You must enter the Jeff Shop at Door 50 with at least 200 coins. If you are below 150 coins by Door 40, backtrack and check every drawer. Never skip the shop. A run without Bandages or a Lockpick at Door 50 is a run that ends at Door 100.

**Door 50 (Library): The Exam, Not a Room.** From the moment you enter, crouch. Do not stand up until you have the key. Listen to your heartbeat — if it gets loud, freeze. Search clockwise or counter-clockwise systematically, but never double back randomly. Sprinting in Phase 1 is an automatic reset.

**Door 50-99: Endurance Management.** Entity frequency peaks here, but your goal is not perfection. It is to arrive at Door 100 with at least two Bandages, one Vitamin, and a clear head. If you take 20 HP of damage in this stretch but keep your healing items, you are in a better position than a flawless runner who spent all their coins on a Crucifix.

**Total entities encountered so far:** Rush (multiple times), Ambush (possibly)

### Doors 20-39: The Mid-Hotel Grind

This section introduces more complex room layouts and more frequent entity encounters.

| Room Range | Key Encounters | Strategy |
|------------|---------------|----------|
| **20-25** | **Jack** (jumpscare from dark closets), more Rush/Ambush | Only hide in closets with light seeping through. Dark closets may contain Jack. |
| **26-30** | Possible **Dupe** encounter (fake doors with numbers) | **Check the door number carefully.** Dupe creates a door with the wrong number. Entering it damages you significantly. |
| **31-35** | **Screech** encounters (attacks when you are in dark rooms without a light source) | Always carry a Lighter or have a light-giving item equipped. Screech deals damage quickly if ignored. |
| **36-39** | **Jeff Shop** preparation rooms. Start saving coins (at least 500). | Do not spend all your coins on consumables. Save for the Jeff Shop. |

### Dupe Door Mechanics

Dupe is one of the trickiest entities because it creates visual deception:
- **Real Door:** Door number matches the current room, normal door creak sound, normal door appearance. Proceeds to next room.
- **Fake Door (Dupe):** Different number (usually off by 1-3), slightly distorted sound, slightly darker or more faded. Deals 40 damage.

**Pro tip:** Always verify the door number before entering. This habit alone saves you from Dupe every time.

### Eyes Entity Details
- **Appearance:** Floating pair of eyes in the darkness
- **Behavior:** Deals damage over time if you look directly at it
- **Counter:** Turn your camera away from the eyes until they disappear
- **Damage:** Approximately 5-10 HP per second of exposure
- **Duration:** 10-15 seconds if you keep looking away

### Door 100: The Figure Boss Fight

Door 100 contains **The Figure**, the final boss of the Hotel floor. This is a stealth encounter that tests everything you have learned.

#### Figure Fight Mechanics

| Phase | Description | What to Do |
|-------|-------------|------------|
| **Phase 1: The Library** | A large library room. The Figure patrols the area. You must find the library key while avoiding the Figure. | **Crouch-walk.** The Figure detects sound. Move slowly and pick up the key when you see it. The key spawns in a random location on the bookshelves. |
| **Phase 2: The Escape** | After getting the key, run to the next door. The Figure chases you through a hallway with obstacles. | **Run.** Do not stop. Follow the path and open the door at the end. |
| **Phase 3: The Final Door** | Use the key to open Door 100. The Figure makes one final attempt to catch you. | Open the door quickly and enter. You have completed Floor 1. |

#### Figure Detection Mechanics
- **Standing/walking normally:** High detection from far away — Figure moves toward your location immediately
- **Crouching/walking slowly:** Low detection, short range — Figure may not notice you if you stay far enough
- **Running/sprinting:** Maximum detection, entire map — Figure will find you very quickly
- **Hiding under tables/desks:** No detection — Figure cannot see you while hidden
- **Heartbeat loud:** Figure is nearby — listen for your heartbeat. If it is loud, the Figure is close, stay still

#### Figure Fight Tips

1. **Crouch the entire time in Phase 1.** Do not sprint unless you have the key and are in Phase 2.
2. **Listen for the Figure's heartbeat.** The louder it gets, the closer the Figure is. If it is very loud, freeze in place.
3. **Use your items wisely.** Vitamins give you a speed boost for Phase 2. Save bandages for after the fight if you take damage.
4. **The key location is randomized.** Search the library systematically. Check bookshelves, tables, and the floor.
5. **In Phase 2, do not look back.** Just run toward the next door. Looking back slows you down and the Figure catches up.

## Secret Rooms and Hidden Areas

### Library Secret Room

Between Doors 60-80, there is a chance of encountering a hidden room variant:
- **Hidden Closet** — Behind a bookshelf in certain library rooms. Reward: Rare item spawn (Crucifix, Spotlight).
- **Secret Passage** — Appears as a slightly transparent wall in hallway rooms. Reward: Shortcut to next room, skips entity encounter.
- **Jeff's Back Room** — Accessible from the Jeff Shop (rare). Reward: Extra items at discounted prices.

### How to Find Secret Rooms

1. **Look for visual inconsistencies.** Slightly misaligned walls, different textures, or unusual shadows often mark secret areas.
2. **Listen for audio cues.** Secret rooms often have subtle ambient sounds that differ from regular rooms.
3. **Use the Hint item from Jeff Shop.** If you buy a Hint, Guiding Light will point toward nearby secrets.
4. **Check behind large furniture.** Bookshelves, cabinets, and paintings sometimes hide passages.

## Badge Farming in the Hotel

Several badges can be earned during the Hotel floor:
- **The Hotel** (Medium) — Complete Floor 1, reach Door 100 and escape
- **Speedrunner** (Hard) — Complete the Hotel in under 15 minutes
- **Pacifist** (Medium) — Complete the Hotel without killing any entities (using Crucifix)
- **Full Clear** (Very Hard) — Find all secret rooms and items in a single run
- **No Damage** (Extreme) — Complete the Hotel without taking any damage

### Speedrun Route

For the Speedrunner badge, follow this optimized route:
- **Doors 1-10 (2 min):** Sprint through everything. Grab only essential items.
- **Doors 11-30 (4 min):** Run Seek chases efficiently. Skip non-essential rooms.
- **Doors 31-50 (3 min):** Quick Jeff Shop purchases (lockpick + vitamins only).
- **Doors 51-80 (4 min):** Avoid unnecessary hiding. Only hide for guaranteed Rush/Ambush.
- **Doors 81-100 (2 min):** Figure fight — optimize key search pattern.
- **Total: ~15 minutes**

## Counter-Intuitive Hotel Advice

The Hotel punishes instincts that work in other games. These five principles go against common sense, but they will save your run.

**1. The fastest route is not the safest.** Sprinting through Doors 1-10 saves seconds, but it trains your muscle memory to sprint everywhere. That habit will kill you in the Figure library, where one accidental sprint means detection. Walk the early rooms deliberately. Build discipline, not speed.

**2. Sometimes backtracking is the right play.** If you enter Door 35 with 10 HP and no bandages, turn around and search the previous room's drawers. The Hotel does not punish backtracking; it punishes desperation. A two-minute detour for healing is cheaper than a twenty-minute restart.

**3. Do not open every door you see.** Dupe doors punish curiosity. If a door number feels even slightly off — if the texture looks darker, if the sound is distorted — trust the feeling and verify. The "safe" play is hesitation, not exploration.

**4. Hiding is not cowardice; it is a resource.** Every time you hide from Rush successfully, you are banking HP and items for the Figure fight. Aggressive players who never hide arrive at Door 100 exhausted and out of supplies. The best runners hide often and move calmly.

**5. The Crucifix is often a trap purchase.** At 100 coins, it feels like security. But if buying it means skipping Bandages and Vitamins, you are trading a safety net for guaranteed survival items. Buy the Crucifix only when it does not compromise your healing budget.

## Why Players Die in the Hotel (Even With a Walkthrough)

Having a map of every room does not guarantee survival. Most deaths in the Hotel happen because players treat a walkthrough as a script instead of a decision tool. Here are the five mistakes that kill experienced players.

**1. Memorizing rooms instead of understanding patterns.** The Hotel uses procedural spawns. Rush does not always appear at Door 4, and Seek can trigger anywhere between Doors 11 and 16. If you memorize "Door 14 = Seek," you get complacent at Door 12 when it shows up early. Learn the *pattern* — flickering lights mean Rush, hallway darkness means Seek — and you will react correctly no matter which door it is.

**2. Not adapting to entity combinations.** Walkthroughs list entities in isolation, but the Hotel stacks them. You might get a Dupe door in a dark room while Screech is active. Players who panic and try to solve one threat at a time usually die. The correct priority is always: immediate damage first. If Screech is biting you, equip your lighter before checking the door number.

**3. Wrong item priority at the Jeff Shop.** New players see the Crucifix and the Revive and think, "Now I cannot die." They skip Bandages to afford the Crucifix, then take chip damage in Doors 51-99 and enter the Figure fight with no way to heal. Bandages and Vitamins are non-negotiable. Luxury items are luxuries.

**4. Leaving hiding too early after Ambush.** The walkthrough says Ambush bounces back, but it does not say exactly how many times. Players count to two, get impatient, and step out into a third pass. Treat every Ambush as a four-pass encounter. Wait for the audio to stop completely, then count two more seconds. Boredom is cheaper than a restart.

**5. Sprinting in the Figure library.** Every walkthrough says "crouch." Every player nods. Then they spend ninety-nine doors running full speed, walk into the library, and their muscle memory takes over. The Figure detects sprinting across the entire map. If you do not practice walking through the entire Hotel, your body will betray you at Door 100.

## Common Death Causes and How to Avoid Them

- **Rush (failed to hide in time)** — All rooms. Listen for flickering lights. Always know where the nearest closet is.
- **Ambush (left hiding too early)** — Door 6+, especially 50+. Count the passes. Ambush can bounce back 2-4 times. Wait 3 seconds after the last pass.
- **Dupe (entered wrong door)** — Door 26+. Always verify the door number. This is the most preventable death.
- **Screech (no light source)** — Door 31+. Always carry a lighter or shakelight. Equip it in dark rooms.
- **Figure (detected in library)** — Door 100. Crouch-walk. Listen for heartbeat. Do not sprint in Phase 1.
- **Seek (failed obstacle during chase)** — Door 14, and occasionally 50+. Follow the glowing door outlines. Practice the obstacle patterns.
- **Halt (turned around)** — Long hallways. Walk backward. Do not turn your camera around.
- **Eyes (looked at the entity)** — Door 66-70. If you see floating eyes, look away immediately.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How many rooms are in the Hotel floor?**
A: 100 rooms, numbered Door 1 through Door 100. Door 100 contains the Figure boss fight.

**Q: Can I skip the Jeff Shop?**
A: Yes, but it is not recommended. The items sold there (especially lockpicks and bandages) are essential for later sections and the Figure fight.

**Q: What happens after I complete the Hotel?**
A: You unlock access to The Mines (Floor 2). See our [The Mines Walkthrough](/posts/doors/the-mines-walkthrough/) for the next floor.

**Q: Does the Figure always appear at Door 100?**
A: Yes, in the standard Hotel floor. However, modifiers or special game modes can change this behavior.

**Q: How much damage does each entity deal?**
A: Rush and Ambush are instant-kill if they catch you outside of hiding. Screech deals approximately 5-10 HP per second. Dupe deals 40 damage. The Figure is instant-kill if caught.

**Q: Can I use the Crucifix on the Figure?**
A: No. The Crucifix works on Rush, Ambush, and several other entities, but not on the Figure. You must complete the stealth encounter.
