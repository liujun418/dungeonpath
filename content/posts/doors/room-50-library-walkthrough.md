## You're Stuck on Room 50. Here's Why.

If you've died to Figure in the Library more than five times, you're probably making the same mistake most players do: treating it like a stealth puzzle when it's actually a **rhythm puzzle**.

Figure's patrol is predictable. Its listening pauses are on a timer. The book placements create noise that Figure investigates on a consistent pattern. Once you feel the rhythm, the Library goes from "impossible" to "tense but manageable."

You don't need faster reflexes. You need to understand the pattern.

---

## Before You Enter: What to Bring

Room 50 starts the moment you open Door 49. These items should be in your inventory:

**Must have:**
- A light source (Gummy Flashlight preferred — no battery anxiety during the puzzle)
- At least 1 Vitamins (for emergency sprints if Figure detects you)

**Nice to have:**
- Lockpick (the Library has lockers you can open for extra items, but they're not essential)
- Bandages (chip damage happens — tripping over furniture, Screech before you entered Room 49)

**Don't bother with:**
- Standard Flashlight + spare batteries — the battery swap animation has gotten players killed while Figure was investigating nearby
- Code Breacher — there are no locked doors in the Library itself

## Book 1-2: The Safe Phase (Figure Is Far)

When you enter the Library, Figure is in the center of the room doing its initial patrol loop. This loop takes about 25 seconds and covers the middle shelves. You have about 15 seconds of safety before Figure's patrol brings it near any bookshelf.

### What to Do Immediately

**Step 1: Crouch and freeze.** Don't move for 3 seconds. Watch Figure. See which direction it's walking. It always starts moving clockwise around the center.

**Step 2: When Figure turns its back, walk to the NEAREST bookshelf.** Not the farthest one, not the one with the glowing book. The nearest one. You're establishing a safe movement rhythm, not optimizing book collection yet.

**Step 3: Grab one book from that shelf.** Don't look for the "right" book — all 8 books are identical in function. Just grab whichever one you can reach fastest.

**Step 4: Walk it to the central pedestal.** Figure's patrol is still in the center. Wait for it to pass the pedestal (heading away from you), then walk (don't run) to the pedestal, place the book, and walk back to a shelf.

### The Key Rhythm

Figure's patrol cycle is roughly: walk 8 steps → stop and listen for 3 seconds → walk 8 steps → listen. Each cycle takes about 11-12 seconds. Your movement window is the 8-step walking phase — Figure's hearing is reduced while it's moving because its own footsteps mask quieter sounds.

**The rule for books 1-2:** Only move during Figure's walking phase. When it stops to listen, you stop. Even if you're 2 steps from the pedestal. Patience here sets up the rest of the encounter.

## Book 3-5: The Mid-Phase (Figure Knows Something Is Happening)

After 2 books are placed, Figure's behavior changes. The patrol speeds up slightly (now ~9-second cycles instead of 11) and Figure pauses to listen more frequently — every 5-6 steps instead of 8.

This is where most deaths happen. Players got comfortable during books 1-2 and try the same rhythm. It doesn't work anymore.

### The Adaptation

**You now have two tools you didn't have before:**

**Tool 1: Book placement noise as a decoy.** When you place a book on the pedestal, it makes a distinct thud sound. Figure WILL investigate that sound — it takes Figure about 4 seconds to walk to the pedestal from anywhere in the room. This means placing a book creates a 4-second window where Figure is walking toward the pedestal and away from the shelves. Use this.

**Tool 2: Item throwing.** Every Library spawns loose items on the floor — books, cans, bottles. Pick one up and throw it (press G or the throw key) to the opposite corner of the room. Figure will investigate the impact sound. This buys you 5-7 seconds of distracted Figure time. Each item is a get-out-of-jail-free card.

### The Books 3-5 Pattern

Here's the rhythm that works:

1. Wait at a shelf near a book. Watch Figure.
2. When Figure walks toward the far side of the room, walk to grab the book.
3. If Figure turns toward you before you reach the pedestal, stop. If you're in the open, crouch and freeze. Figure's detection range while walking is ~30 studs. While listening, it's ~50 studs.
4. Place the book. **Immediately after placing, walk AWAY from the pedestal** — don't wait to see Figure's reaction. You already know it's coming.
5. By the time Figure reaches the pedestal, you should be at a shelf 20+ studs away, crouched.

## Book 6-8: The Pressure Phase (Figure Is Hunting)

After 5 books, the exit door code is partially revealed (you only need 5 numbers for the code). Figure's patrol is now aggressive — 6-7 second cycles, pause every 4-5 steps, and Figure occasionally sprints between listening positions.

### The Real Danger: Complacency

The biggest risk in books 6-8 is that you've been doing this for 4 minutes and your focus is slipping. You think you know the rhythm. Then Figure pauses one second earlier than you expected and you're caught in the open.

**Counter:** For books 6-8, shorten your movement bursts. Instead of walking 10 steps per cycle, walk 5 steps, then pause and listen. Yes, it's slower. It should be slower. The last 3 books should take as long as the first 5 combined.

### The Figure Detected You. Now What?

You were impatient and Figure heard you. Its head snaps toward you. It roars. It's charging.

Do NOT:
- Try to crouch and hide — it already knows where you are
- Run toward the exit door — it's locked until all 8 books are placed
- Jump — jumping makes you easier to track

**The recovery play:**

1. Sprint to the OPPOSITE end of the Library. Figure chases in a straight line toward your last known position. If you break line of sight behind 2+ rows of shelves, Figure loses you in 6-8 seconds.
2. While sprinting, throw any item in your inventory toward a far corner. The noise gives Figure a false target.
3. Once line of sight is broken, crouch behind a shelf and DON'T MOVE for 10 seconds. Figure will investigate your last known area, then return to patrol.
4. Wait an additional 5 seconds after Figure resumes patrol before moving again. It's extra-cautious after a detection.

**One detection doesn't end the run.** Two detections in quick succession usually does. After surviving a detection, play ultra-safe for the next book — you've used your recovery window.

---

## After the Books: The Exit Sprint

All 8 books placed. The exit door opens with a loud grinding sound. Figure immediately charges toward the noise.

**Do not hesitate.** The moment you place the 8th book, you should already be facing the exit door. Sprint directly to it. Don't look back at Figure. Don't grab any remaining items. Don't check if you left anything behind.

The exit is a straight line from the pedestal. If you placed the 8th book and immediately sprinted, you'll reach the door before Figure reaches you. If you paused for even 2 seconds — pop a Vitamin if you have one. The speed boost is the difference between clearing the door and dying 3 steps from it.

## Practice Plan: From 20 Deaths to Consistent Clear

| Session | Goal | Expected Attempts |
|---------|------|-------------------|
| Session 1 | Place 3 books without being detected | 5-8 attempts |
| Session 2 | Place 5 books, learn the mid-phase rhythm shift | 5-8 attempts |
| Session 3 | Full clear attempt | 3-5 attempts |

Most players clear the Library consistently after 15-25 total attempts. The learning isn't mechanical — it's internalizing Figure's patrol rhythm. Once you feel it, you'll wonder why it ever seemed hard.
