## Stop Using Code Aggregator Sites

Google "DOORS codes 2026" and you'll find 20 websites listing "15 WORKING CODES." All 20 sites copy from each other. None verify whether codes actually work. You try 15 codes. 13 are expired. One was never real. One gives you 5 Knobs — roughly 30 seconds of gameplay value.

The real codes — the ones that drop during updates and expire in 48 hours — never make it to those websites. The players who get them are in the official Discord with notifications on.
