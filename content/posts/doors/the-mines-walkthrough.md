## Room Numbers Don't Kill You. Threats Do.

Most walkthroughs go room by room: "Room 1: safe. Room 2: safe. Room 3: Grumble." This is useless because rooms are procedurally generated. Your Room 3 might not be the same as this guide's Room 3.

This walkthrough is organized by threat — the entities, hazards, and situations that kill you, in the order you'll actually encounter them. When you die to something, flip to that section. The room number doesn't matter. The threat does.

---

## Grumble: The Ceiling Killer

**First appearance:** Room 3-8 (procedural)
**What kills you:** You enter a room. You scan horizontally for threats — the Hotel taught you threats come from doors and corridors. Grumble is above you. It spots you. It charges. You die. You never looked up.

**The protocol:**
1. Enter every Mines room. First glance: UP. Check the ceiling for a green glow.
2. If you see Grumble: freeze. It tracks movement, not sound.
3. Wait for Grumble to move to another ceiling position. This takes 8-15 seconds.
4. When Grumble moves, walk (don't run) to the exit. Grumble has a 2-second reaction delay when it repositions.

**The tell you missed:** A faint green particle effect drips from Grumble's ceiling position. If you see green particles falling, Grumble is above you. Freeze.

## Open Caverns: The Closetless Death Trap

**First appearance:** Room 5-8 (procedural)
**What kills you:** Rush screeches. You look for a closet. There isn't one. You freeze, looking. Rush arrives. You die with your neck craned toward a wall that had nothing on it.

**The protocol:**
1. Enter an open cavern. Immediately identify: this room has no closets.
2. Note which direction leads back to the last safe room with closets.
3. If Rush screeches: SPRINT back to that room. Not walk. Sprint. You have 3.5 seconds.
4. If you have a Sprint Potion: pop it the moment you hear the screech. The 2x speed guarantees you reach the closet.

## Giggle Swarms: The Chip Damage Killer

**First appearance:** Room 8-12 (procedural)
**What kills you:** Individual Giggles deal 5-8 damage. A swarm of 15-20 can deal 100+ damage if you try to fight them. You stand your ground, trying to kill them one by one, and die to accumulated chip damage.

**The protocol:**
1. When you hear wet gurgling/splashing, Giggle swarm is incoming.
2. SPRINT through the room. Don't fight. Don't stop. Don't look back.
3. Giggles are slow — they can't catch a sprinting player.
4. If you're trapped (both exits blocked by swarms): use a Lantern burst if you have one (AoE light damage disperses them), or accept the chip damage and sprint through the smaller swarm.

**The Hotel habit that kills you here:** The Hotel taught you to face threats. Screech? Look at it. Rush? Hide from it. Figure? Outsmart it. Giggles are the first entity where the correct response is "run away." Fighting is death.
