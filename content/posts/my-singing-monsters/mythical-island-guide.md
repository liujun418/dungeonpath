You hit Level 20. The XP bar fills, the level-up animation plays, and a new island icon lights up on the map. You tap it, pick the boat teleport, and land on a quiet patch of land with five pedestals around a central structure. Welcome to Mythical Island.

If you have played MSM for a while, you know the pattern: new island, new monsters, new ways to spend coins. Mythical Island follows that pattern — except it also introduces the Wondermine, a system genuinely different from anything else in the game. And it is one of the easiest islands to waste resources on if you walk in blind.

This guide covers unlocking Mythical Island, breeding every Mythical, how the Wondermine actually works, and whether it is worth your coins.

## Why Mythical Island Matters

Mythical monsters are among the highest coin producers in the game on a per-monster basis. A single well-placed Mythical can out-earn multiple three-element monsters on your Natural Islands. And because Mythical Island is its own separate space, every Mythical you place there adds to your total income without competing for beds on your crowded Natural Islands.

Mythicals also show up as requirements in seasonal events and limited-time breeding windows. If you ignored Mythical Island and suddenly need a Blarret for an event, you are looking at a 24-hour breeding timer and no way to speed it up except gems. Having a few Mythicals ready means you do not miss out.

The island's song is also one of the best in the game. A fully populated Mythical Island with all 12 monsters singing together is genuinely impressive — and it is a flex when visitors see your island.

But the real reason Mythical Island matters: it teaches you resource management. The Wondermine forces you to choose between gambling and grinding. Make the wrong call and you burn coins. Make the right call and you build a steady collection.

## How to Unlock Mythical Island

The unlock condition is simple: reach account Level 20. No quest chain, no gold cost, no special currency. Once you hit Level 20, the island appears on your map. You access it through the boat teleport system — the same interface you use to reach [Ethereal Island](/posts/my-singing-monsters/ethereal-island-guide/) and [Shugabush Island](/posts/my-singing-monsters/shugabush-island-guide/).

If you are not Level 20 yet, here is what moves the needle fastest:

- **Place four-element monsters on every Natural Island.** Entbrat, Deedge, Riff, Shellbeat, and Quarrister each give a solid chunk of XP when placed. Breed duplicates and place them. You can sell them later.
- **Bake treats constantly.** Feeding monsters is the most reliable XP source. Keep your bakeries running and upgrade them when you can afford it.
- **Do your daily goals.** XP, coins, and sometimes gems — it adds up over weeks.
- **Place Wublins and Ethereals.** These give large one-time XP bonuses when you wake them or teleport them. The [Wublin Island guide](/posts/my-singing-monsters/wublin-island-guide/) covers the optimal wake-up order.

Most active players hit Level 20 within three to four weeks. Casual players: six to eight weeks. Mythical Island is an early late-game goal, not an endgame one.

Once you land, you will see five pedestals around a central structure. The structure is the Wondermine. The pedestals are where you place your first Mythicals. The island comes with zero free monsters. You earn every single one.

## The 12 Mythical Monsters and Their Home Islands

Each Mythical is tied to one of the five Natural Islands. You can breed each Mythical on two islands: its home Natural Island and Mythical Island itself. The breeding combo is the same either way. Breeding on a Natural Island means competing for nursery space with your regular projects. Breeding on Mythical Island means dedicated space.

| Mythical | Home Island | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| G'joob | Plant Island | First Mythical most players target. Component monsters are ready earliest. |
| Strombonin | Cold Island | Common second target. Cold Island is your second Natural Island. |
| Bona-Petite | Air Island | Uses mid-tier Air monsters in its combo. |
| Galvana | Water Island | Relatively accessible combo on Water Island. |
| Banjaw | Earth Island | Earth Island is your last Natural Island, so Banjaw tends to come later. |
| Cherubble | Plant Island | Second Plant Island Mythical. Plant Island players get a head start. |
| Auglur | Cold Island | Second Cold Island Mythical. |
| Blarret | Air Island | Second Air Island Mythical. |
| Whajje | Water Island | Second Water Island Mythical. |
| Bizzeebix | Earth Island | Second Earth Island Mythical. |
| Flum-Ox | Plant/Cold | Cross-island Mythical. Combo spans elements. |
| Blipsqueak | Air/Water/Earth | Cross-island Mythical. Requires multi-element breeding. |

The distribution is roughly two Mythicals per Natural Island, with a couple of cross-island entries. You cannot camp on one Natural Island and collect all 12 — you need to progress through the full game.

## How Breeding Works on Mythical Island

Breeding Mythicals is deterministic. Each one has a specific two-monster combo. Put the right two monsters into the breeding structure and you have a chance at the Mythical egg. The chance is not 100% — you will get failures that produce the regular offspring of that combo. But the combo is fixed. There is no mystery.

You can breed Mythicals in two places:

1. **On the Mythical's home Natural Island.** Easier early on because you already have the component monsters leveled there. The downside: you tie up your breeding structure and nursery on an island you also use for other projects.

2. **On Mythical Island itself.** Once you have at least one Mythical placed on Mythical Island, you can use the breeding structure there. More efficient long-term because it does not compete with your Natural Island breeding queue.

Key insight: **you do not need to own every Mythical to start breeding on Mythical Island.** You only need one. Get your first Mythical — say, G'joob from Plant Island — place it on Mythical Island, and now you have a breeding structure there. From that point, you can breed the remaining 11 Mythicals entirely on Mythical Island.

Breeding times for Mythicals are long. Expect 18 to 36 hours per attempt. Enhanced structures and friend torches help. Budget several weeks of real time to collect all 12.

For breeding odds, torch mechanics, and structure upgrades, read the [full breeding guide](/posts/my-singing-monsters/breeding-guide/).

## The Wondermine: How It Works and What It Costs

The Wondermine is the signature structure on Mythical Island. It does something no other structure in MSM does: it consumes monsters permanently in exchange for a random reward.

Here is the flow: you select the Wondermine, choose monsters from your island to feed into it, and pay a coin fee. The monsters are gone — removed from your island, not returned. The Wondermine then produces a reward. The pool includes Mythical eggs, partial progress toward an egg, and consolation prizes like coins or treats.

The coin fee per use is substantial. For a mid-game player, one activation can cost several hours of coin income. And you also lose the sacrificed monsters, so there is an ongoing replacement cost.

The Wondermine is best understood as a slot machine. You put coins and monsters in, pull the lever, and maybe get a Mythical egg. The odds are not published, but the community consensus is that the Mythical egg drop rate is low — low enough that deterministic breeding is almost always the better play.

The Wondermine only makes sense when you have a maxed-out coin economy and a steady supply of duplicate monsters you do not need. If you are still building bakeries, upgrading castles, or clearing obstacles, the Wondermine is a trap.

## Breeding vs. Wondermine: A Decision Framework

| Your Situation | Recommended Approach | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Zero Mythicals. Want your first. | Breed on the Natural Island. | Deterministic. You control the outcome. |
| One Mythical. Want more. | Breed on Mythical Island. | Dedicated breeding structure. No competition. |
| Hunting the last one or two. | Breed on Mythical Island with torches. | Still faster and cheaper than gambling. |
| All 12 owned. Want duplicates. | Either approach works. | Your economy can support either at this point. |
| Excess coins and duplicates. | Wondermine. | Converts waste into a chance at something useful. |
| Below Level 30. Still building economy. | Breed only. Never touch the Wondermine. | Coins are better spent on bakeries, castles, and upgrades. |
| Need a specific Mythical for a deadline. | Breed and use gems to speed up. | Wondermine gives a random Mythical, not the one you need. |

The pattern is clear: breeding is the default. The Wondermine is the exception. If you are ever unsure, breed.

## A Counter-Intuitive Tip: The Wondermine Is a Worse Early Investment Than It Looks

New players often unlock Mythical Island, see the Wondermine, and think: "This is the fast way. I will feed it monsters and get eggs." This is exactly wrong.

The Wondermine looks like a shortcut. It is not. It is a resource sink for players who have already outgrown the point where coins and common monsters matter. If you are still saving for your next castle upgrade, every coin you drop into the Wondermine is a coin not going toward a permanent economic upgrade.

A bakery upgrade pays you back every day for the rest of your playtime. A Wondermine activation gives you one roll of the dice. Guaranteed returns beat gambles every time.

The smarter play: unlock Mythical Island, breed your first Mythical on its home Natural Island, place it on Mythical Island, then use the Mythical Island breeding structure for the rest. Ignore the Wondermine until you have at least six Mythicals placed and a coin income that makes the fee feel trivial.

## Failure Retrospective: How I Burned Millions on the Wondermine

When I first unlocked Mythical Island, I had about two million coins saved. I looked at the Wondermine, read the in-game description, and thought I had found the fast track. I fed it duplicate T-Roxes and Pummels. Paid the fee. Got a small coin reward. Tried again. Treats. Tried again. A few coins. By the time I stopped, I had burned through 1.5 million coins and had zero Mythical eggs.

**The error:** I treated the Wondermine like a production building. It is a casino. The house wins over small sample sizes.

**The cause:** I did not understand the odds. The game does not publish the drop rate, and the early rewards make it feel like you are making progress. You are not. You are converting coins into less valuable coins.

**The recovery:** I stopped using the Wondermine entirely. I bred G'joob on Plant Island using the standard combo. It took about a dozen attempts over three days. Once G'joob was on Mythical Island, I bred Strombonin next, then Bona-Petite. Within two weeks of switching to breeding, I had five Mythicals placed and was earning more coins from them than I had wasted.

The lesson: the Wondermine is not your friend. It tests whether you understand opportunity cost. Pass the test and breed. Fail and donate coins to the game's sink.

## What Is Still Unconfirmed

Community data on Mythical Island varies in a few areas:

- **Wondermine success rates.** The game does not publish drop rates. Some players report getting an egg in under 10 attempts. Others report 50-plus with no egg. The true rate is probably single-digit percentages, but nobody has a verified number.
- **Wondermine coin costs.** The fee may scale with your level or the number of Mythicals you own. Different players report different amounts. Assume the cost is "a lot" and plan accordingly.
- **Breeding odds per Mythical.** Combos are confirmed, but exact percentage chances are not published. Community estimates range from roughly 5% to 15%, depending on torches and the specific Mythical. Torches help. That is the consistent finding.
- **Mythical Wubbox gem cost.** The Mythical Wubbox exists, costs gems, and accepts Mythicals in its grid. Whether the gem cost matches other Wubboxes is disputed.

None of this changes the core advice: breed your Mythicals. The Wondermine is a late-game luxury. The combos are known, the breeding structures are reliable, and torches improve your odds. You do not need exact percentages to know that deterministic breeding beats gambling.

## FAQ

### How do I unlock Mythical Island?

Reach account Level 20. The island appears on your map automatically. Access it through the boat teleport system. No quest, no purchase, no special currency. Speed up leveling by placing four-element monsters on your Natural Islands, baking treats, and completing daily goals. Most players hit Level 20 within three to six weeks.

### How do I breed G'joob?

G'joob is the Plant Island Mythical. Breed it on Plant Island using T-Rox and Pummel — the confirmed pair. You can also breed G'joob on Mythical Island once you have at least one Mythical placed there. G'joob is a common first target because Plant Island is your first Natural Island, so the component monsters are easy to have ready by Level 20. Expect a long breeding timer and multiple attempts.

### Is the Wondermine worth it?

For most players, no. The Wondermine costs a substantial amount of coins per use and permanently consumes the monsters you place in it. The reward is a random chance at a Mythical egg with low odds. Breeding is deterministic — you use the right combo and you will eventually get the Mythical you want. The Wondermine only becomes worthwhile when you have a maxed-out economy, excess coins, and duplicate monsters you do not need.

### How many Mythical monsters are there?

There are 12 Mythical monsters on Mythical Island: G'joob, Strombonin, Bona-Petite, Galvana, Banjaw, Cherubble, Auglur, Blarret, Whajje, Bizzeebix, Flum-Ox, and Blipsqueak. Each is associated with one of the five Natural Islands. Most Natural Islands have two Mythicals each, with a couple of cross-island Mythicals. A Mythical Wubbox also exists on the island.

### What level do I need to be for Mythical Island?

Level 20. If you are below that, focus on your Natural Islands — place four-element monsters, bake treats, and collect coins regularly. Wublins and Ethereals also give good XP bonuses. The grind from Level 15 to Level 20 is where most players feel the slowdown. Use that time to stockpile coins for the breeding projects you will start once Mythical Island unlocks.
