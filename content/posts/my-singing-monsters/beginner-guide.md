## Day 1: Don't Rush the Second Island

The tutorial nudges you toward buying Cold Island (50,000 coins). If you buy it immediately, you'll have a Cold Island with 2 monsters, zero coins, and Plant Island still half-empty.

**The better Day 1 plan:** Upgrade Plant Island's Castle to level 4 first. This increases your monster capacity. Breed double-element monsters until Plant Island has 8-10 monsters singing. NOW buy Cold Island — you'll have enough Plant Island income to immediately breed 4-5 monsters for Cold Island from existing stock.

**The rhythm:** Fill → save → buy next island → immediately populate → repeat. An empty island costs coins and produces almost nothing. A populated island funds the next one.

## The Breeding Order

| Step | Breed | With | Result | Time |
|------|-------|------|--------|------|
| 1 | Potbelly (Plant) | Toe Jammer (Water) | Oaktopus | 8h |
| 2 | Mammott (Cold) | Potbelly | Drumpler | 30m |
| 3 | Tweedle (Air) | Potbelly | Dandidoo | 8h |
| 4 | 3-element monster | 1-element (missing) | 4-element target | 24h |

**For 4-element monsters:** always use 3-element + 1-element where the 1-element is the MISSING type. This limits offspring to 3 outcomes, giving ~33% success rate per attempt. Random pairings can drop your chance to under 10%.

## The Diamond Trap

Diamonds are MSM's scarcest resource. The game constantly offers speed-ups for 1-20 Diamonds each. Resisting this is the single biggest factor in progression speed.

**Never spend Diamonds on:** speed-ups, food, coins, random eggs.

**Only spend Diamonds on:** Castle upgrades (increase monster capacity → more income), and eventually Wubbox (the strongest monster in the game).
