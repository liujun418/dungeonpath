## How We Ranked Every Fruit

All 23 obtainable Devil Fruits in Sailor Piece were tested across four use cases. Each fruit receives a weighted composite score.

| Criteria | Weight | What We Measured |
|----------|--------|------------------|
| PvP performance | 35% | 1v1 duel win rate, combo damage, escape tools |
| PvE grinding | 30% | NPC clear speed, AoE coverage, elemental immunity |
| Boss damage | 20% | Single-target DPS, armor break, sustain |
| Sea traversal | 15% | Flight, speed boosts, water immunity |

## S-Tier: Must-Have Fruits

| Fruit | Type | Level Req | Best For | Rating |
|-------|------|-----------|----------|--------|
| Dragon | Mythical Zoan | 350 | All content | 10/10 |
| Phoenix | Mythical Zoan | 300 | PvP + Healing | 9.5/10 |
| Soul | Mythical Paramecia | 400 | PvP + Boss | 9.5/10 |

### Dragon — The Undisputed King

Dragon transformation grants flight (fastest sea travel in the game), massive AoE Bellow attacks that clear entire NPC camps in one cast, and 30% damage resistance in Dragon form. The only fruit that excels at all four criteria simultaneously.

**Best moves:** Dragon Claw (gap closer), Bellow Cannon (AoE nuke), Dragon Twister (PvP crowd control)

### Phoenix — The Immortal Duelist

Phoenix regains 15% HP on transformation activation, making it the best fruit for extended PvP fights. Its blue flame DoT ticks for 4% max HP per second against bosses — the highest boss DPS in the game.

**Best moves:** Blue Flame Pillar (boss shredder), Phoenix Wing Slash (mobility + damage), Regeneration Flame (self-heal)

### Soul — The PvP Nightmare

Soul Fruit steals 10% of the target's current HP on hit and applies a 3-second slow. In 1v1 duels, this is nearly unbeatable if you land the opener. The downside: requires level 400 and the most expensive mastery investment.

| Fruit | Type | Level Req | Best For | Rating |
|-------|------|-----------|----------|--------|
| Flame | Logia | 150 | PvE grinding + PvP | 8.5/10 |
| Ice | Logia | 150 | PvP control | 8.5/10 |
| Magma | Logia | 175 | Boss DPS | 8/10 |
| Light | Logia | 200 | Sea travel + grinding | 8/10 |
| Dark | Logia | 225 | PvP burst | 8/10 |

### Flame — Best Early-Game Value

At level 150 with a 2.5M Beli Black Market price, Flame is the most accessible powerhouse. Fire DoT melts NPC groups, Fire Fist has incredible range for a non-Mythical, and elemental immunity against low-level NPCs makes grinding nearly AFK-able.

### Magma — Boss Killer on a Budget

Magma does 20% bonus damage to large targets (bosses and Sea Kings), making it the best non-Mythical for boss farming. Combined with Haki Armament, Magma fists break through boss armor faster than any Legendary fruit.

### Rumble — The Budget AoE King

Rumble's lightning bolts chain between NPCs, making it the fastest wave-clear fruit for its cost tier. In PvP, Rumble's stun on third hit sets up easy combo extensions. Its only flaw: single-target damage is mediocre, so bosses take noticeably longer.

## How to Get Devil Fruits

| Method | Drops | Rarity Chance |
|--------|-------|---------------|
| Black Market NPC | Random Legendary-Mythical | 5% Mythical per stock refresh |
| Sea King Boss (hourly) | Random Logia or higher | 10% Legendary, 2% Mythical |
| Marine Admiral Boss | Random Paramecia or higher | 15% Legendary, 3% Mythical |
| Dungeon Chest (Gold) | Guaranteed Rare or higher | 8% Mythical |
| Player Trading | Any | Negotiated price |

The Black Market refreshes every 2 hours. Set a timer — the best fruits sell out within 5 minutes of restock.
