## Money Farming Methods

Earning Beli efficiently is the foundation of economic success. The best method shifts as you progress through the game.

### Early Game (Level 0-3,000)

Focus entirely on **island quests**. They provide 3-5x more Beli per hour than grinding regular enemies. Complete every quest on each island before moving forward — skipping quests for exploration is the most common early-game income mistake and slows both your leveling and your wealth building simultaneously. Supplement quest income with code redemption for a one-time boost of 50,000-250,000 Beli, and open chests on every island for 10,000-30,000 Beli in supplemental income.

### Mid-Game (Level 3,000-8,000)

Transition to **boss farming** as your primary income source. Repeatedly defeat mid-tier bosses (Sukuna, Gojo, Jinwoo) for 100,000-500,000 Beli per hour. Use Boss Keys strategically to eliminate travel time between fights — every minute spent running between spawn points is a minute not earning. Start with Sukuna and Gojo (fastest clears), then work up to Jinwoo and Alucard as your combat power increases. High-level quests on Shibuya, Hollow, and Slime islands remain viable at 80,000-150,000 Beli per hour, and dungeon clears on Dungeon Island add 100,000-300,000 Beli per hour as supplemental income.

### Late Game (Level 8,000-12,500)

Run the **endgame boss rush** route for 500,000-1,500,000 Beli per 47-70 minute cycle: Rimuru to Anos to Shadow Cid to Yamato to Atomic Boss or Cid. If you have Sea 2 access, Sea 2 boss farming becomes the highest income in the game at 1,000,000-3,000,000+ Beli per hour. Add luck boosts from codes for increased drop rates. Tower farming on Dungeon Island provides 300,000-800,000 Beli per hour as a secondary option while you're waiting for boss respawns.

## Trading System Deep Dive

The trading system is where the most wealth is created — and lost. Understanding how to trade effectively separates rich players from struggling ones.

### How Trading Works

1. **Approach another player** and select "Trade" from the interaction menu
2. **Add items to trade** — both players select items they want to offer
3. **Review and confirm** — both players must accept the terms
4. **Complete trade** — items are exchanged instantly

### What Can Be Traded

**Tradable:** Devil Fruits, Boss Keys, some Cosmetics, some Titles, and some Exclusive Items.

**Not tradable:** Beli, Gems, Power Shards, Passive Shards, Bloodline Stones, Clan/Race/Trait Rerolls, and consumed or used items.

Always check the item description to confirm tradability before initiating a trade.

### The Golden Rules of Trading

- **Know current market values** — prices change with updates. Check community resources before every trade, even if you traded the same fruit yesterday.
- **Never rush a trade** — scammers create urgency. If someone is pushing you to confirm quickly, that's a red flag. Take your time to verify values.
- **Trade up, never trade down** — only accept trades where you receive equal or greater value. A trade that leaves you with less value is a loss, even if you got what you asked for.
- **Verify before confirming** — double-check every item in the trade window before accepting. Read names, not icons.
- **Keep a trade log** — track what you gave and received. This helps you evaluate your trading performance and identify patterns in your decision-making.

### How to Evaluate Trade Value

When assessing any trade offer, consider these factors in order of importance. **Fruit rarity** carries the most weight: Mythical beats Legendary beats Epic beats Rare down to Common. Next is **fruit utility** — does the fruit fit the current meta? A fruit that's strong in the current patch holds its value better. **Item scarcity** matters significantly; boss drops with 1% rates command premium prices regardless of their practical use. **Current demand** fluctuates with meta shifts and community trends. Finally, **future potential** matters for long-term holds — follow developer notes to anticipate which fruits might be buffed or nerfed in upcoming patches.

### What Is Overvalued in the Current Market

- **Common fruits** — traders overpay for collection completion, but common fruits offer minimal combat value. Skip them unless you're building a collection yourself.
- **Old or nerfed fruits** — nostalgia pricing inflates value long after a fruit's performance has been nerfed. Check recent patch notes before buying.
- **Unawakened Mythical fruits** — the rarity label drives prices up, but an awakened Legendary fruit often outperforms an unawakened Mythical at a fraction of the cost.

### What Is Undervalued in the Current Market

- **Boss Keys** — many players don't realize their farming potential. Collect them from drops and trade for premium fruits with players who value immediate liquidity.
- **Map Pieces** — needed for Sea 2 progression, but casual players may not know their value. Accumulate and trade when Sea 2 demand spikes.
- **Mid-tier fruits with awakening potential** — players obsess over Mythicals, ignoring that awakened mid-tier fruits are combat-viable and affordable. Buy at a discount.
- **Cosmetic titles** — purely cosmetic, but rare titles (0.2-1% drop rates from bosses) appreciate over time as supply stays limited.

## Trading Safety: How to Avoid Scams

The trading system is the most common place for players to lose valuable items. Scammers are creative, but every scam follows one of a few predictable patterns.

### Common Scam Patterns

- **Fake Fruit Trade** — a trader offers a fruit that looks rare but is actually a common reskin or name-alike. Always read the full item name and rarity in the trade window, not just the icon.
- **Quick-Switch Scam** — the trader adds a valuable item, then swaps it for a cheap item at the last second while you're distracted. Review the final trade offer carefully before confirming, and ignore any pressure to rush.
- **Trust Trade Scam** — "Trade me your fruit first, I'll trade you back double." Never trade first with strangers. The built-in trade system requires mutual confirmation — if someone asks you to bypass it, they're trying to steal from you.
- **Middleman Scam** — "Use this trusted middleman player" — the middleman takes both items and disappears. Only use officially recognized middlemen from the community Discord, and verify their identity through the server's verification system.
- **Distraction Scam** — the trader creates chaos by summoning a boss or causing lag while swapping items in the trade window. Cancel the trade if anything feels off and restart in a clean session.
- **Overpay Bait** — "I'll overpay for your fruit!" but the overpay items are worthless consumables or common fruits. Evaluate every item independently and don't let the promise of quantity distract you from quality.

### Safe Trading Checklist

Before accepting any trade, verify:

1. **Item Names** — are the items exactly what was advertised? Read the text, not the icon.
2. **Item Rarity** — is the rarity what you expect? Common through Mythical, check each one.
3. **Item Count** — is the quantity correct? Scammers sometimes remove zeroes.
4. **Trade Terms** — does this trade benefit you? If you're not sure, the answer is no.
5. **Pressure Tactics** — is the other player rushing you, typing in all caps, or threatening to leave? Cancel the trade.
6. **Known Scammer Check** — is this player on any community scammer blacklists? A quick check can save your entire inventory.

### Reporting Scammers

If you are scammed: screenshot the trade window if possible, report to the official Discord where Shadowrise Devs monitor for scam reports, share the scammer's username on community forums, and analyze what went wrong to avoid the same mistake. Every scam is a learning experience — the best players have been scammed at least once and adapted their process.

The gap between players who hit 10 million Beli and players who struggle to buy their first Legendary fruit isn't luck or playtime. It's a specific set of avoidable mistakes that compound over time.

| Symptom | Root Cause | Fix |

The pattern is clear: **spending discipline matters more than grinding speed.** You can earn 1M Beli per hour and still be broke if you spend it on the wrong things. ## Counter-Intuitive Advice

### 1. Grinding More Won't Make You Rich — Spending Less Will

Every player's instinct when they're broke is to grind harder. More hours, more enemies, more bosses. But here's the counter-intuitive truth: **most players above level 5,000 earn enough Beli per hour to afford anything in the game — they're broke because they spend it as fast as they earn it.** The player who earns 500K Beli per hour and keeps 80% of it will outpace the player who earns 1M per hour and spends 90%. Track your spending for one session. You'll likely find that 60-70% of your Beli goes to things that don't improve your income: cosmetic spins, fruit gambling at the dealer, unnecessary rerolls, and buying fruits you outgrow within a week. Cut those, and your wealth compounds naturally without grinding a single extra hour.

### 2. Basic Fruit Spins Are the Most Expensive Item in the Game

The Basic Spin costs 15,000 Beli and has roughly a 0.5% chance of giving a Mythical fruit. The expected value of a Basic Spin is about 3,000 Beli when you factor in weighted probabilities across all fruit tiers. That means you're losing 12,000 Beli of value on average every time you click. Over 100 clicks, that's 1.2 million Beli incinerated for a handful of Common and Uncommon fruits you'll never use. The counter-intuitive truth: **the Basic Fruit Spin button is the most expensive item in the game disguised as the cheapest.** No single purchase drains more wealth from more players. The only spins worth your currency are Premium Gem Spins at 200 Gems — and even those should wait until your fighting style, sword, and essential rerolls are fully purchased.

### 3. Mythical Fruits Are Overrated for Everyone Below Level 8,000

The community obsesses over Dragon, Fiend, and Kitsune. Trading channels overflow with "WTS Dragon 400K." But an unawakened Mythical fruit in the hands of a level 5,000 player performs measurably worse than an awakened Legendary fruit. Awakening a Mythical costs tens of thousands of Beli and requires boss drops that are inefficient to farm at mid-game levels. Meanwhile, an awakened Quake or Light fruit costs 100K-120K Beli and delivers roughly 85% of the combat performance of a Mythical at 35% of the cost. The counter-intuitive truth: **the best fruit for your money at mid-game is an awakened Legendary, not a bare Mythical.** Save the Mythical chase for endgame when you have the resources to awaken it properly. You'll progress faster, earn more Beli along the way, and reach the point where you can afford that Dragon fruit with interest to spare.

### 4. Trading Less Makes You More Money

Active traders who execute 20+ trades per day often end up poorer than patient traders who execute 2-3 high-quality trades per week. Each trade carries risk: mispricing the market, falling for a scam, accepting a deal that looks good but isn't, or simply losing track of your inventory value. More trades means more exposure to those risks. The patient trader waits for clear asymmetric opportunities: a fruit that's temporarily undervalued after a patch note, a seller who doesn't research prices, or a buyer who desperately needs a specific fruit for their build and is willing to overpay. The counter-intuitive truth: **your best trading tool is the Decline button.** Say no to 90% of trade offers, and the 10% you accept will be disproportionately profitable. Speed in trading is the enemy of accuracy — the fastest way to lose wealth is to trade impulsively.

- **Priority 3 — Quake, Light, Spirit.** Solid all-around fruits, good value for their price point. Target at level 3,000+ as your first serious fruit investment.

### Fruit Trading Tips

- **Buy during updates** — new patches cause price fluctuations as players rush to test changes. Good fruits may be temporarily cheaper during the chaos.
- **Sell hype fruits** — when a fruit gets buffed, its price spikes for 24-48 hours. Sell during the hype window and buy back when prices normalize.
- **Awakened fruits command premium** — if you can awaken a fruit before trading it, your profit margin jumps significantly. The awakening cost is almost always lower than the value increase.
- **Trade common fruits in bulk** — some collectors want full sets for completion. Bundle 5-10 common fruits together for a better deal than selling individually.
- **Watch the meta** — fruits that are strong in the current patch hold and increase in value. Fruits that were recently nerfed drop fast. Stay ahead of the curve.

## Shop Prices and What to Buy First

Knowing what to buy and in what order prevents wasteful spending that sets your progression back by hours.

### Fruit Dealer Shop (Sailor Island)

The Fruit Dealer is designed to drain your Beli. Here's what's actually worth buying:

- **Basic Fruit Spin (15,000 Beli)** — Low priority. Mostly gives Common and Uncommon fruits with terrible expected value. Avoid after your first day of play.
- **Premium Beli Spin (50,000 Beli)** — Medium priority. Better rates than Basic, but 50,000 Beli buys you a guaranteed Rare or Epic fruit on the trading market. Only spin if you have excess Beli after all essential purchases.
- **Gem Spin (50 Gems)** — Medium priority. Decent chance at rare fruits, but Gems are better saved.
- **Premium Gem Spin (200 Gems)** — High priority. Best rates for rare and Mythical fruits. This is what you save your Gems for.

### Fighting Styles

Fighting styles are your best Beli investment because they provide permanent, compounding combat power that speeds up every future Beli-earning activity.

- **Basic Styles (early islands)** — 5,000-20,000 Beli. High priority early game. Essential upgrade from basic Combat.
- **Mid-Tier Styles** — 50,000-150,000 Beli. High priority mid-game. Significant power increase that directly speeds up boss clears.
- **Top-Tier (Sharkman Karate, Water Kung Fu)** — 150,000-300,000 Beli. Very high priority for melee mains. These styles define your combat ceiling.
- **Endgame (Strongest in History)** — 300,000-500,000+ Beli. Highest priority for the ultimate melee power. Save specifically for this.

For melee mains, upgrade your fighting style as soon as you can afford the next tier. Each upgrade provides noticeably better hitboxes, damage scaling, and i-frames that translate directly into faster boss kills and more Beli per hour.

### Sword Shop

- **Basic Swords** (10,000-30,000 Beli) — Low priority. Replace quickly as soon as you find a better drop.
- **Mid-Tier Swords** (50,000-150,000 Beli) — Medium priority. Good for sword-focused builds.
- **High-Tier Swords** (200,000-500,000+ Beli) — High priority if you main swords. The damage difference is dramatic.

### Reroll Costs

- **Race Reroll** — NPC on Sailor Island (Beli) or codes. High priority early game — get Galevorn or Fishman ASAP. Your race affects your income rate directly through EXP and damage bonuses.
- **Clan Reroll** — Codes or drops. High priority once you have a stable income. Devil clan's 30% bonus drop rate increases the value of every boss kill.
- **Trait Reroll** — Codes or drops. Medium priority. Optimize your traits only after race and clan are finalized, since those have a larger impact on your farming efficiency.

### Purchase Priority by Stage

- **Early (0-3,000):** Basic fighting style first, then race reroll (Fishman for bonus EXP, which means faster leveling and faster access to better farming), then Armament Haki unlock. Fruit spins come last.
- **Mid (3,000-8,000):** Mid-tier fighting style and clan reroll (Devil clan for the damage and drop rate bonuses). Then Premium fruit spins if you have Gems saved. Swords are lower priority.
- **Late (8,000+):** Top-tier fighting style and confirm Devil clan is active. Then Premium Gem Spins. Luxury cosmetics are the absolute last priority — they provide zero income benefit.

## Economy Tips by Progression Stage

### Stage 1: Starter Phase (Level 0-1,000)

Build your initial wealth by completing every Starter Island and Jungle quest for 50,000-100,000 Beli. Redeem all active codes for 200,000+ Beli, 100+ Gems, and materials — this single action can double your starting capital. Reroll to Fishman race for the +EXP grinding bonus that speeds up everything you do. Buy your first fighting style from the NPC on Starter Island — better combat means faster clears, which means more Beli per hour from the very beginning.

### Stage 2: Progression Phase (Level 1,000-5,000)

Establish consistent income by farming Sukuna, Gojo, and Jinwoo with Boss Keys for 200,000-500,000 Beli per hour. Save 45,000-120,000 Beli for an Epic or Legendary fruit — this is the single purchase that most dramatically accelerates your progression speed. Reroll to Devil clan for the +42% damage and 30% bonus drop rate. Start trading surplus items for Beli or better fruits to diversify your income beyond pure grinding.

### Stage 3: Late Game (Level 5,000-10,000)

Maximize boss income with the full boss rush route for 500,000-1,500,000 Beli per cycle. Trade actively for undervalued Boss Keys and Map Pieces — these are the easiest arbitrage opportunities in the game. Accumulate 350,000-400,000+ Beli for your first Mythical fruit (Dragon, Fiend, or Kitsune). Collect Ancient Fragments from high-rate bosses for Sea 2 unlock — this gates your access to the highest-reward content in the game.

### Stage 4: Endgame (Level 10,000+ / Sea 2)

Farm Sea 2 bosses for 1,000,000-3,000,000+ Beli per hour — the highest consistent income in the game by a wide margin. Engage in premium trading of rare titles, awakened fruits, and exclusive items. Participate in guild content for exclusive rewards and bonuses that aren't available through any other method. Save for "The World" weapon or power acquisition — the ultimate endgame goal that most players never reach.

## Advanced Trading Techniques

### Arbitrage Trading

- **Update Arbitrage** — buy fruits before an update; sell if they get buffed when patch notes drop. Medium risk, but the payoff can be 2-3x your investment within hours.
- **Seasonal Arbitrage** — buy during low-activity periods when fewer players are online and prices dip (early morning, weekdays). Sell during peak hours (evenings, weekends) when demand is highest. Low risk, consistent returns.
- **Meta Arbitrage** — track community tier lists and YouTuber recommendations. When a fruit starts climbing the rankings, buy before the wider market adjusts. Medium risk with strong upside if you catch the trend early.

### Bulk Trading

- **Boss Key Bundling** — collect 5-10 Boss Keys of different types and sell them as a bundle. Boss farmers pay a premium for convenience.
- **Fruit Collection Trades** — trade complete sets of lower-tier fruits (all Common, all Uncommon) for one higher-tier fruit. Collectors pay the markup for completion.
- **Reroll Trading** — if you've accumulated more clan, race, or trait rerolls than you need from codes, trade the excess for Beli or fruits. These are limited-supply items with steady demand.
