You spawn on Starter Island with a Rowboat. It is slow. Painfully slow. Six real minutes to sail from Starter to Shell Island — a distance that takes a Caravel about 2 minutes. The Rowboat can only reach three islands. You cannot access Jungle Island, Desert Island, or any mid-to-late-game content. The Rowboat is designed to be outgrown within your first 3 hours of gameplay. Most players keep it for 15.

This guide will teach you how to skip the Rowboat and get a Caravel fast in Sailor Piece. We will show you the fastest way to earn Beli and upgrade your ship within your first 3 hours of gameplay.

## The Economics of Sailing

Every minute you spend sailing is a minute you are not earning Beli, not gaining XP, and not progressing. If you spend 40 percent of your playtime sailing with the Rowboat and 60 percent actually playing, upgrading to a Caravel (15 percent sailing, 85 percent playing) effectively increases your productive playtime by 25 percent.

Over a 50-hour playthrough, that is 12.5 extra hours of actual progression — the equivalent of gaining 12.5 hours of game time for the price of a 50,000-Beli boat.

## How to Skip the Rowboat

### Step 1: Level 1-15
1. Complete all Starter Island quests
2. Kill low-level enemies on Starter Island and Shell Island
3. Collect all chests and treasure maps
4. Do not buy a Sailboat

### Step 2: Level 16-25
1. Complete all Shell Island quests
2. Kill medium-level enemies on Shell Island and Beach Island
3. Collect all chests and treasure maps
4. Start saving for the Caravel

### Step 3: Level 26-30
1. Complete all Beach Island quests
2. Kill high-level enemies on Beach Island and Jungle Island
3. Collect all chests and treasure maps
4. Buy the Caravel for 50,000 Beli

## The Sailboat Trap

The Sailboat problem in Sailor Piece is similar to the Steady Rod enchantment problem. The Sailboat is a trap that catches many players. You have 10,000 Beli at level 15. The Rowboat is miserable. The shop offers a Sailboat for 10,000. It is faster. It feels like an upgrade. You buy it. Two hours later, you have outgrown it — it cannot reach Desert Island or beyond. You need a Caravel. Those 10,000 Beli are gone. If you had saved them toward the Caravel, you would be 10,000 Beli closer. The Sailboat is a comfort purchase disguised as an upgrade. Skip it. Save for the Caravel.

## How to Earn Beli Fast

### Method 1: Questing
Complete all quests as soon as possible. Quests are the fastest way to earn Beli and gain XP.

### Method 2: Treasure Maps
Collect all treasure maps and complete them. Treasure maps are a great source of Beli and rare items.

### Method 3: Chest Collecting
Collect all chests on every island. Chests are a great source of Beli and rare items.

### Method 4: Enemy Farming
Kill enemies for Beli and XP. Focus on high-level enemies for maximum Beli per hour.

## Conclusion

Upgrading your ship is one of the most important decisions you will make in Sailor Piece. By skipping the Rowboat and getting a Caravel fast, you can effectively increase your productive playtime by 25 percent. This will allow you to progress through the game faster and reach mid-to-late-game content sooner.

Remember: **Skip the Sailboat. Save for the Caravel.**
