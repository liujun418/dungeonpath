## How XP Works in Sailor Piece

XP gain in Sailor Piece scales with enemy level relative to yours. Fighting at-level enemies is a trap — you want to punch up.

| Enemy Level vs Yours | XP Multiplier | Recommended? |
|---------------------|--------------|-------------|
| 10+ levels below | 0.5x | Never — waste of time |
| 1-9 levels below | 0.8x | Only if no better camp available |
| Your exact level | 1.0x | Suboptimal — you can handle harder |
| 1-5 levels above | 1.2x | Good |
| 5-10 levels above | **1.5x** | Best — ideal grinding range |
| 11-15 levels above | 1.3x | Higher risk, less efficient |
| 15+ levels above | 1.0x | You'll die too often for this to be worth it |

## Quest XP vs Grinding XP

Many players think quests are the best XP source. The numbers say otherwise.

| Method | XP/Hour (Level 200) | XP/Hour (Level 400) |
|--------|--------------------|--------------------|
| Pure NPC grinding (optimal camp) | 35,000-45,000 | 55,000-70,000 |
| Quest-focused (travel time included) | 20,000-30,000 | 30,000-45,000 |
| Mix (grab quest, grind NPCs, turn in) | 40,000-50,000 | 60,000-75,000 |

**The mix approach wins.** Grab quests that target your current grinding camp, grind NPCs while quest tracking, turn in when full. The travel time for quest-only leveling kills efficiency.
