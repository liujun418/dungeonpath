## Your Loadout Works on One Map. It's Wrong on the Other 6.

Bridge: your Heavy Sniper dominates. You feel like a god.

Warehouse: you equip the same Heavy Sniper. An SMG player rounds the corner at 5 meters. You can't scope fast enough. You die. You die again. You die 15 times. You think you had a bad game. You had the wrong weapon for the map.

RIVALS has 7 maps. Each demands a different primary weapon, different positioning, and different engagement distances. Here's what works on each.

---

## Bridge (Long-Range, Open)

**Best weapon:** Heavy Sniper. Long, straight sightlines with obvious cover — the Heavy Sniper's ideal map.
**Power positions:** The bridge pillars. The under-bridge path. The control room at either end.
**Avoid:** The open bridge center. It's a kill zone — exposed from both ends and both sides.
**Strategy:** Hold a pillar. Peek. Shoot. Reposition. Don't hold the same pillar for more than 15 seconds — the enemy pre-aims where you last appeared.

---

## Warehouse (Close-Range, CQB)

**Best weapon:** Tactical SMG or Shotgun. Engagement distances are 5-20 meters — sniper scopes are useless here.
**Power positions:** The catwalk (high ground). The container stacks (cover + flank routes). The back warehouse corridor (longest sightline on the map, good for AR).
**Avoid:** The open warehouse floor. No cover, multiple angles. You'll be shot from 3 directions.
**Strategy:** Wall Run between containers. Never walk the floor. The catwalk controls the map — whoever holds it wins.

---

## Rooftops (Vertical, Sniper-Friendly)

**Best weapon:** Heavy Sniper or Semi-Auto Sniper. Vertical angles favor scoped weapons.
**Power positions:** The AC unit corner. The central rooftop. The lower balconies (flank routes).
**Avoid:** The open rooftop center during daytime — silhouetted against the sky, easiest headshot on the map.
**Strategy:** Grapple between rooftops. Never take the stairs — they're camped. Hold the AC unit for 30 seconds, then rotate. Static snipers on Rooftops are dead snipers.

---

## Construction (Mixed Ranges, All Weapons Viable)

**Best weapon:** AR or Semi-Auto Sniper. Mixed ranges mean you need versatility.
**Power positions:** Top floor. The scaffolding on the east side. The ground-floor garage.
**Avoid:** Standing near windows — silhouetted. Standing on exposed beams — no cover.
**Strategy:** Control the top floor. Vertical audio is buggy in RIVALS — players below can't pinpoint your footsteps. Use this. Rotate between floors after every kill. Never stay on one floor for more than 20 seconds.

---

## Downtown (Mid-Range, Street Combat)

**Best weapon:** AR or Tactical SMG. Street-level fighting with alley flank routes.
**Power positions:** The alley dumpster (cover + escape). The rooftop access (overwatch). The street corner (hold angle on main road).
**Avoid:** The open plaza center. Crossfire from 4 angles.
**Strategy:** Fight in the alleys, not the streets. The alleys have single approaches — you control who enters. The streets have multiple approaches — you don't.

---

## Arena (Open Center, Cover-Based)

**Best weapon:** AR or Semi-Auto Sniper. Open center demands range. Cover pillars demand peek discipline.
**Power positions:** The center platform (controls the map). The perimeter pillars (safe peeks). The elevated ramp (overwatch).
**Avoid:** The open arena floor — no cover, 360° exposure.
**Strategy:** Control the center platform. It's the highest-value position on any map — sightlines to the entire arena. But it's also the most contested. If you can't hold it, rotate between perimeter pillars and pick off the platform holder.

---

## Hangar (Long-Range, Aircraft Cover)

**Best weapon:** Heavy Sniper. Long sightlines down the hangar bay.
**Power positions:** The back hangar (longest sightlines). The plane wreckage (cover + peek angles). The side catwalks (elevated, good for AR).
**Avoid:** The open hangar bay center — no cover, entire map can see you.
**Strategy:** Hold the back hangar. Jiggle-peek the corner by the exit doors. The plane wreckage breaks sightlines — use it to reposition without being seen.

## The Map-to-Loadout Quick Reference

Don't pick your loadout until you see the map. Here's the cheat sheet:

| Map | Primary | Ability | Why |
|-----|---------|---------|-----|
| Bridge | Heavy Sniper | Grapple Hook | Long sightlines. Grapple reaches bridge pillars. |
| Warehouse | Tactical SMG | Wall Run | Close range. Wall Run navigates containers. |
| Rooftops | Heavy Sniper | Grapple Hook | Vertical. Grapple is mandatory for rooftop-to-rooftop. |
| Construction | AR | Grapple Hook or Wall Run | Mixed ranges. Both movement abilities work. |
| Downtown | Tactical SMG | Wall Run | Close-mid. Wall Run in alleys. |
| Arena | AR | Adrenaline | Open center. Adrenaline for crossing exposed areas. |
| Hangar | Heavy Sniper | Recon Pulse | Long sightlines. Pulse checks plane wreckage corners. |

**The one-weapon trap:** You're a sniper main. You love the Heavy Sniper. You use it on every map. On Warehouse and Downtown, you're playing at a 30% disadvantage because your weapon doesn't match the engagement distances. You don't need to master every weapon. You need ONE close-range alternative (SMG or Shotgun) for the 3 maps where snipers underperform. Two weapons. Seven maps. That's the minimum.
