## Season 3 Battle Pass Overview

Season 3 runs from April 15 to June 10, 2026 (8 weeks). The theme is "Hollywood Action" — weapon skins are movie-poster themed, abilities have cinema VFX.

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Pass cost | 799 Robux |
| Free tier rewards | 5 (Credits only, 25 per free tier) |
| Premium tiers | 50 |
| Season duration | 8 weeks (April 15 - June 10) |
| Total XP required | 500,000 |
| XP per tier | 10,000 |
