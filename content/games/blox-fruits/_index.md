### Blox Fruits Beginner Guide — Your First Fruit, First Sea & When to Change Seas (2026)

**Should I eat the first fruit I find in Blox Fruits?**

Only if it's Light, Ice, Flame, or Magma (all Legendary tier Logia fruits). If it's a Common, Rare, or even some Epic fruits, sell it or store it. Eating a bad fruit means you can't eat another one without replacing it — and finding a second fruit could take hours. A bad fruit actively slows your progression because you're stuck with weak abilities. Wait for a good one.

**When should I leave First Sea and go to Second Sea?**

At level 700. Not 650, not 680 — 700 exactly. That's when Second Sea unlocks. Staying in First Sea past 700 wastes XP because Second Sea enemies give 2-3x more XP per kill. The most common mistake: reaching level 680 and thinking 'just 20 more levels.' Those 20 levels in First Sea take longer than 20 levels in Second Sea would take. Leave the moment you hit 700.

### Blox Fruits Second Sea Complete Guide: Islands, Bosses & Progression

**What level do I need to enter the Second Sea in Blox Fruits?**

You need to be level 700+ to enter the Second Sea. Talk to the Military Detective at the Prison in the First Sea to start the quest that unlocks access.

**Is Second Sea harder than First Sea?**

Yes, enemies scale from level 700 to 1,500. The difficulty jump is significant if you rush in at exactly level 700 with poor gear. Farm the first few islands carefully before pushing deeper.

**What's the best fruit for Second Sea progression?**

Buddha is the consensus best for leveling through Second Sea due to its massive AoE and damage reduction. Magma and Light are also strong alternatives if you prefer ranged combat.

### Still Using Flame at Level 300? Exactly When to Ditch Your Starter Fruit in Blox Fruits (2026)

**I'm level 250 with Flame fruit. Should I switch?**

Run this test: go to your current grinding spot. Time how long it takes to clear one full NPC camp (gather enemies + AoE attack + cleanup). Now remember how long it took at level 80 when you first got Flame. If your current clear time is more than 2x your old clear time, your fruit has fallen behind and you should switch. If it's still under 2x, you can wait until level 300 — the Second Sea pre-switch window is the most efficient time to upgrade.

### Blox Fruits Trading Guide: Fruit Values, Economy & Safe Trading (2026)

**What is the most valuable fruit in Blox Fruits?**

West Dragon is the most valuable fruit at approximately 1 billion Beli trading value, followed by East Dragon at ~860 million Beli. Kitsune (~165M), Yeti (~115M), and Gas (~75-80M) round out the top five. Dragon fruits dominate the market due to their PvP power and extremely low supply.

**How do I avoid getting scammed when trading in Blox Fruits?**

Always use the official in-game trading system (never drop-trade), verify values on at least two independent platforms (BloxFruitsValues, FruityBlox), never trust 'trust trades' or 'test trades,' use Discord trading servers with reputation systems and middlemen for high-value trades, and watch for common scams like quick-swap, fake premium, and overpay-then-cancel tricks.

**Do Blox Fruits values change over time?**

Yes, values fluctuate significantly with game updates, reworks, and new fruit releases. A fruit that gets buffed in an update can double or triple in value within days (e.g., Dough after its rework). Conversely, nerfed fruits can lose half their value. Check current community prices weekly if you trade actively.
