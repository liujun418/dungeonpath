### TDS Beginner Guide — Your First Win, Farm Economy & When to Save vs Spend (2026)

**Why do I keep dying on wave 15-20 in TDS?**

Because you're playing TDS like it's a tower placement game, when it's actually an economy game. If you placed 5 Militants by wave 10, you spent all your coins on towers that will be obsolete by wave 20 — and you have no Farm income to afford the mid-game upgrades. The fix: 2-3 Farms at level 2 by wave 12. They generate the passive income that buys Minigunners, Commanders, and DJs later. No Farms = you'll always die around wave 25 regardless of how well you place your towers.

**What's the simplest loadout for my first win?**

Militant, Farm, Commander, DJ, Minigunner. In that unlock order. This loadout works on every Normal-mode map. Place Farm first, upgrade to level 2. Place Militant for early defense. Place Commander by wave 15, DJ by wave 20. Upgrade Militant to level 3. Save for Minigunner around wave 25. Max Minigunner by wave 30. This isn't optimal — but it's simple, forgiving, and will get you your first Normal-mode win.

### Best Ways to Farm Coins & Gems in TDS — Economy Guide for Every Level (2026)

**What's the fastest way to farm coins in TDS?**

Fallen Mode on Crossroads with a fast loadout (Accelerator, Engineer, DJ, Commander, Farm) earns 1,000-1,200 coins per clear in 18-22 minutes — roughly 3,000-3,500 coins per hour. Hardcore Mode gives more per clear (1,500-2,000) but takes 35-40 minutes, making its hourly rate lower. For pure coin efficiency, Fallen Mode on the fastest map you can clear consistently is the meta.

**How do you farm gems fast in TDS?**

Gems are primarily earned from Hardcore Mode (50-80 gems per win, depending on completion speed) and weekly quests (100-200 gems per week). There is no fast gem farm — they're intentionally scarce. The most efficient approach: do daily quests for 15-25 gems/day, complete the weekly quest for 100-200 gems, and run Hardcore 2-3 times daily. Expect ~300-500 gems per week with consistent play.

**Is the Farm tower worth using in TDS?**

Yes, Farm is essential for coin farming runs. A level 3 Farm generates 750 coins per wave by wave 20+, paying for itself in 6-7 waves. In a typical 35-wave run, a single Farm generates 8,000-12,000 extra coins — enough to max out 2-3 additional DPS towers. In multiplayer, one player running 3-4 Farms can fund the entire team. Skip Farm only in speedrun or specific challenge modes.

### TDS Event Towers Guide — The Ones You Missed, The Ones You Regret, and Whether Any of Them Actually Matter

**Which TDS event towers are still obtainable?**

As of mid-2026, none of the past seasonal event towers are available. Hardcore towers like Accelerator, Engineer, Brawler, and Necromancer are always available through Hardcore mode gems. The Gladiator sometimes returns during anniversary events. Everything else — Swarmer, Toxic Gunner, Executioner, Archer, Sledger, Frost Blaster, Jester, Elf Camp, Gardener — is locked away with no announced return dates.

**Was the Gladiator the best event tower in TDS?**

Gladiator dominated from 2019 to 2021 because nothing else matched its early-game DPS for the price. But power creep hit hard. Golden Scout and Golden Soldier do the same job cheaper and faster now. Gladiator is basically a trophy at this point. If you want an event tower that still pulls weight, Executioner is your best bet for cost-efficient AoE in Fallen mode.

**How do I prepare for the next TDS event?**

Bank 50,000 to 100,000 coins and 2,000 to 3,000 gems before anything gets announced. Most event towers cost 20,000 to 50,000 coins or 1,000 to 2,000 gems. Follow the TDS Discord with notifications on for the announcements channel. Events usually drop on Fridays. The pattern never changes: teaser three to five days out, full announcement one to two days before, event goes live around noon EST on Friday. Even if the battle pass tower looks mediocre, finish the pass. Buffs happen.

### TDS Event Towers Strategy — Why You Keep Missing S-Tier Towers That Never Come Back

Skipping TDS events is the most expensive mistake in the game: limited-time event towers (like the Pumpkin Launcher from Halloween 2023) never return and can stay meta-defining permanently — the Pumpkin Launcher deletes 40% of a boss's HP per cycle while the standard Accelerator does ~2%. **Farm every event while it's live**, because the towers you miss are gone for good.

### Best Golden Towers in TDS (2026) — Every Golden Skin Ranked, DPS & Crate Strategy

**What is the best golden tower in TDS?**

Golden Minigunner is the undisputed #1 golden tower for late-game DPS. It has high damage output with a relatively cheap upgrade cost, no placement limit, and scales extremely well with Commander + DJ Booth buffs. Golden Crook Boss is the best all-purpose golden tower, effective from Wave 1 through the final wave with both tower damage and goon support.

**Should I buy golden crates or wait for the daily shop?**

Wait for the daily shop. Golden crates (50,000 coins) give a random golden skin, meaning you can get Golden Pyromancer or Golden Soldier repeatedly and waste coins. The daily shop lets you buy the exact golden tower you want. Most veteran players recommend saving coins and buying specific golden towers from the shop rather than gambling on crates.

**Which golden tower should I get first?**

Get Golden Minigunner first if you want the best late-game DPS tower. Get Golden Crook Boss first if you want an all-round tower that's effective from start to finish. Get Golden Scout first if your primary goal is speedrunning Fallen Mode. Avoid Golden Pyromancer as a first purchase — get it last for collection purposes only.

### Golden vs Fallen vs Hardcore in TDS — Which Mode to Farm First? (2026)

**Which TDS special mode should I play first?**

Start with Fallen Mode first. It requires the lowest entry investment (level 15, any loadout that can beat Normal mode), gives solid coin rewards (~800-1,200 coins per win), and the Fallen skins you earn are tradeable. Move to Golden Mode once you have a level 30+ account and at least 3 level-3 towers. Save Hardcore for last — it requires a coordinated loadout with specific towers at level 4+.

**Is Golden Mode worth the grind in TDS?**

Yes, but it's a long grind. Each Golden Mode win gives you one Golden Crate, and you need 3 wins to unlock a single Golden Tower (3 crates = 1 Golden Tower of your choice). Golden Towers are significantly stronger than their regular versions — Golden Minigunner does 2x the DPS of regular Minigunner. A full set of the 5 best Golden Towers takes roughly 15-20 wins.

**What's the hardest mode in TDS right now?**

Hardcore mode is the hardest by a wide margin. Enemies have 3x the HP of Fallen mode equivalents, and the final boss (Void Reaver) has a unique ability that disables towers in a radius. You need a very specific loadout — typically Ace Pilot, Military Base, DJ, Commander, and either Turret or Pursuit. Casual players should expect 5-10 failed attempts before their first Hardcore clear.

### Tower Defense Simulator Hardcore Mode Guide: Tips, Loadouts, and Strategies (2026)

Hardcore is TDS's toughest standard mode: enemies have **2-3x HP**, waves come faster, boss waves stack multiple bosses, and the early economy is tight. Prerequisites: complete all Normal maps and unlock the **Accelerator** (essential DPS). Expect burn-immune, stun-resistant, and speed-boosted enemy variants, and build around a strong early economy plus one clear DPS carry.

### When to Place, Upgrade & Sell in TDS — The Timing Decisions That Win or Lose Games (2026)

**Should I upgrade my Farm or buy a DPS tower first in TDS?**

Farm to level 2 first — always. A level 2 Farm generates 350 coins per wave and pays for itself in 3 waves. Only after you have 2-3 level 2 Farms should you buy DPS towers. The math: a level 2 Farm costs 700 coins and generates ~12,000+ coins over a 35-wave game. Buying a Minigunner first costs 1,500 coins and generates zero passive income. The Farm starts paying you back immediately. The Minigunner only pays you back if you survive — and with no Farm income, you might not afford the upgrades to survive.

**When should I sell a tower in TDS?**

Sell a tower when: (1) it's in the wrong position and enemies have already passed it, (2) you need emergency cash to place a critical tower (like a Commander or DJ) before a boss wave, or (3) the tower has outlived its usefulness (early-game towers on wave 30+). Never sell towers just to afford upgrades on other towers — the 30% sell penalty means you're losing value. Selling a level 3 Minigunner (6,050 coins spent) returns 4,235 coins — you just burned 1,815 coins.
