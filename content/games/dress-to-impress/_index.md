### DTI All Themes List — Every Theme, Its Category & the Quick-Win Formula (2026)

**How many DTI themes are there?**

DTI has 80+ rotating themes. The exact number changes with updates, but all themes fall into exactly 5 categories: Fashion Style, Era/Time, Environment, Character/Profession, and Mood/Concept. You don't need to memorize 80 themes. You need to recognize the category in 5 seconds and apply its universal rules.

**What is the easiest DTI theme to win?**

Gothic, Beach, Y2K, Formal, Princess, and 1980s have the highest win rates because voters instantly recognize a correct interpretation. These themes have strong, universal visual signatures that leave little room for disagreement.

**What is the hardest DTI theme to win?**

Avant-Garde, Editorial, Futuristic, Minimalist, and Abstract have the lowest win rates because voters disagree on what good looks like. Boldness beats safety here — memorable and polarizing outfits outperform safe, forgettable ones.

### Dress To Impress Beginner Guide — Your First 5 Themes, What to Buy & How to Score 3+ Stars (2026)

**Why do I keep getting 2 stars even when I match the theme?**

Because DTI voters judge visual impact, not theme accuracy. A perfectly themed but visually flat outfit (all one color, no accessories, default hair) looks 'low effort' on the runway — even if it's exactly what the theme asked for. A loosely themed but striking outfit (bold contrast, clear silhouette, memorable accessory) gets more stars. The voter's eye processes color contrast and silhouette in the first 1-2 seconds. Theme accuracy registers at second 3. If you lost the voter in the first 2 seconds, accuracy doesn't save you.

**What items should I buy first with in-game currency?**

Buy items that work across multiple themes — not theme-specific pieces. The best early purchases: a black corset top (works for Gothic, Formal, Dark, Royalty), a floor-length skirt (Formal, Bridal, Gothic, Royalty), and silver jewelry (matches almost everything). Three versatile items that cover 8+ themes are worth more than 10 items that each work for one theme.

**Should I spend all my currency on one expensive dress?**

No. One 400-currency gown wins you one theme and leaves you broke for the rest. The three best early purchases are a black corset top, a floor-length skirt, and silver jewelry. Together they cost roughly 500 currency and cover 8+ themes. One dress covers one. Buy versatility first, specialization second.

### DTI Custom Makeup Master Guide — Eyes, Lips, Face & Color Theory (2026)

**Is DTI Custom Makeup worth the Robux?**

Yes, Custom Makeup is the single most impactful gamepass in Dress To Impress. It costs 299 Robux and lets you customize eyeshadow, eyeliner, lipstick, blush, highlighter, and face details individually — instead of being limited to preset faces. This means your makeup can perfectly match your outfit colors every round, which consistently earns more stars than mismatched preset faces. Players with Custom Makeup average 0.5-1 more stars per round.

**How do I match my makeup to my outfit in DTI?**

Pick your outfit's dominant color. Apply the rule of analogous harmony: eyeshadow should be within 2 shades of the outfit's main color on the color wheel. Lipstick can either match exactly (bold) or be a neutral (nude/pale pink). If the outfit has multiple bold colors, go neutral on makeup so your face doesn't compete with your clothes. One pop of color (bold lip OR bold eye, not both) is the safest winning formula.

**Can I save custom makeup faces in DTI?**

No, DTI does not currently have a built-in face saving system. You must recreate your custom face each round. However, you can screenshot your best faces and reference them during the dress-up phase. Top players build a mental library of 3-5 go-to face recipes for common themes (formal, gothic, fairy, casual, Y2K) and can recreate them in under 15 seconds with practice.

### Dress To Impress Free vs VIP Guide: Is VIP Worth It? Full Item Comparison (2026)

**Is VIP worth buying in Dress To Impress?**

For most players, VIP is a 'nice-to-have' rather than essential. While the VIP room contains unique items like wings, the jellyfish skirt, and a Victorian gown, many of these can be approximated using free items and DTI codes. The Custom Makeup pass (349 Robux) is generally considered more impactful than VIP for winning rounds, as makeup is more visible and transforms your look more noticeably than a single exclusive item.

**What does VIP get you that free players don't have?**

VIP players get: access to the exclusive VIP room with unique items (jellyfish skirt, column dress, Victorian gown, multiple wing sets, a sword, exclusive jewelry), private dressing rooms and hair salon seats, a golden VIP title badge, and exclusive poses and animation sets. VIP also grants access to VIP-only servers in some cases.

**How much does VIP cost and what are the options?**

VIP costs 299 Robux per month (~$3.99) or 799 Robux for permanent access (~$10.00). The permanent option is significantly better value — it pays for itself in under 3 months compared to monthly. If you plan to play DTI for more than a few months, go permanent.

### Best Hair Combos & Makeup Pairings for Every DTI Theme — Quick Reference (2026)

**How do you layer multiple hairs in Dress To Impress?**

DTI allows you to equip up to 5 hair items simultaneously by using different hair slots: one full hairstyle, one bang/fringe, one back hair extension, and two accessory hairs (like ponytails or braids). Open the Hair section in the dressing room, equip a base hairstyle, then click other hair items — they'll layer automatically if they occupy different slots. Items in the same slot replace each other.

**Is Custom Makeup worth buying in DTI?**

Yes, Custom Makeup is the single most impactful gamepass in DTI. It lets you choose individual eyeshadow, eyeliner, lipstick, blush, and face details separately instead of using preset faces. This means your makeup can perfectly match your outfit colors — purple eyeshadow for a gothic look, coral lips for beach themes, etc. Without Custom Makeup, you're stuck with the preset faces which often clash with creative outfit color schemes.

**What's the best hair combo for the Fairy/Magic theme in DTI?**

Use the Long Flowing Hair as your base, layer the Elven Bangs in front, add the Floral Crown hair accessory, and top it with a subtle glitter hair overlay if available. For makeup: light purple eyeshadow, pale pink lips, and the sparkle cheek highlight. This combo consistently places top-3 on Fantasy and Fairy themes because it reads as clearly magical without looking like a costume.

### Dress To Impress Makeup & Pose Guide: Custom Makeup, Animations & Runway Poses (2026)

**How does custom makeup work in Dress To Impress?**

Custom Makeup is unlocked by purchasing the Custom Makeup pass (349 Robux). Once purchased, sit in a Salon chair and press E to access the makeup editor. You can customize eyes, brows, lips, contour, blush, eyeshadow, nose, and more using hex color codes for precise color matching. The Trip To Tokyo update added 70 new custom makeup items.

**What are the best poses to use on the runway in Dress To Impress?**

The best poses depend on your outfit theme. For elegant/gala themes, use poses from the Idol, Petal, or Valentine's packs. For edgy/grunge looks, poses from the Brat or Cosplay packs work best. Always favorite your most-used poses (star icon) for quick access during timed runway segments. Walk animations like the Superstar Walk Pack also influence how your avatar moves down the runway.

### DTI Voting Psychology — Why Good Outfits Lose & How to Win Over Voters (2026)

**Why do I keep getting 2-3 stars when my outfit matches the theme perfectly?**

Because DTI voters don't judge 'accuracy to theme' — they judge 'visual impact in the 4 seconds you're on the runway.' A perfectly accurate but visually muted outfit (all beige, no contrast, no focal point) scores lower than a loosely-themed but visually striking outfit (bold colors, clear silhouette, one memorable piece). The voter's eye goes to contrast first, silhouette second, and theme accuracy third. You're optimizing for accuracy. You should be optimizing for impact.

**What's the single most effective thing I can do to get more stars?**

Add one bold focal point. A dramatic hat, an oversized accessory, a striking color contrast between top and bottom — something that makes the voter think 'oh, that's interesting' in the first second they see your model. Most 2-star outfits are 'nice but forgettable.' Most 4-5 star outfits have one thing you remember 10 seconds after the runway ends. You only get 4 seconds on stage. Make one of them count.

**Does my position in the voting order affect my score?**

Yes. Voters anchor to their first few ratings. If the first models get 4-5 stars, voters tend to stay generous. If the first models get 1-2 stars, voters stay harsh. You can't control your position, but you can read the lobby. After the first 2-3 votes, check the star pattern. If it's generous, submit your boldest look next round. If it's harsh, play safe with a clean, high-contrast outfit that won't polarize.
