### DOORS Ambush Protocol — How to Tell It From Rush by Sound, Multi-Pass Timing & Why Exiting Between Passes Kills You (2026)

**How do I tell Rush and Ambush apart?**

Rush: clean high-pitched screech rising smoothly, one pass. Ambush: distorted glitchy lower-pitched screech wavering up and down, 2-6 passes. In 30 seconds of listening side by side, the difference becomes unmistakable. The lights also differ: Rush flickers once before passing. Ambush flickers repeatedly between passes. If the lights are still flickering after the first pass, stay in the closet.

### DOORS Ambush vs Rush Audio Differences — How to Tell Them Apart in Under 1 Second (2026)

**What are the key audio differences between Ambush and Rush?**

Rush produces a clean, high-pitched screech that rises smoothly over 3.5 seconds. Ambush makes a distorted, glitchy lower-pitched screech that wavers up and down. These differences are teachable in 30 seconds of side-by-side listening.

**How many times can Ambush pass in a single encounter?**

Ambush can pass 2 to 6 times per encounter, with 2 to 5 second gaps between passes. The number of passes and gap duration are randomized — there is no fixed pattern to exploit.

**Can you learn to distinguish Ambush from Rush quickly?**

Yes, after 30 seconds of side-by-side listening, most players can identify Ambush vs Rush with 90% accuracy. The key is knowing what to listen for: pitch stability (Rush) versus wavering distortion (Ambush).

### Figure Keeps Killing You on Room 50? Here's the Rhythm That Beats Him Every Time (2026)

**How many seconds do I have before Figure detects me if I sprint?**

Zero seconds if Figure is in its listening phase. During the walk phase, you have about 1.5 seconds of sustained noise before detection triggers — Figure's own footsteps partially mask your sound. During the listen phase (every ~11 seconds), detection is instant. This is why most players die: they sprint during a listen phase when Figure just stopped moving, and they're caught before they can react. The rule: only move during Figure's walk phase. When it stops, you stop. No exceptions.

### DOORS Hotel Walkthrough — Room-by-Room Guide From Door 1 to Your First Clear (2026)

**How long does it take to beat the DOORS Hotel?**

A full Hotel clear (Doors 1-100) takes 20-35 minutes for experienced players and 45-90 minutes for beginners who are still learning entity patterns. The biggest time variance is Room 50 (The Library/Figure) — it can take anywhere from 3 minutes (experienced, clean run) to 15 minutes (beginner, multiple close calls). The rest of the Hotel is relatively consistent at 15-20 minutes once you know the entity sounds.

**What's the hardest room range for beginners?**

Doors 9-20 are where most beginners quit. Before Door 9, the Hotel is peaceful. At Door 9, Rush activates suddenly, and players who haven't built the 'enter-room, find-closet' habit die repeatedly. The difficulty spike is psychological, not mechanical — the game goes from zero threat to lethal threat in one door.

**Can you beat the Hotel without buying items from Jeff's Shop?**

Yes. Jeff's Shop at Door 52 sells convenience, not progression. Every item in the Hotel can be found as floor loot. A no-shop run is harder because you rely on RNG for light sources and healing, but it is absolutely possible. Many experienced players skip Jeff's entirely to maintain movement speed.

### DOORS Room 50 Library Walkthrough — Surviving Figure, Step by Step (2026)

**How many books do you need to find in DOORS Room 50?**

You need to find and place 8 books to unlock the exit door in the Library. Each book has a shape symbol on its cover that corresponds to a number on the decoding chart on the wall. The code to the exit door is generated by the order of the books — book 1's number, book 2's number, etc. This means every run has a different code, and you can't memorize a single answer.

### DOORS The Mines Survival Guide — What Changes From the Hotel & How to Adapt (2026)

**What's the biggest difference between the Hotel and The Mines?**

The Mines removes the locker system in roughly 30% of rooms. Unlike the Hotel where every room has at least one closet, The Mines has open cavern rooms with zero hiding spots. You must either outrun Rush (not possible after room 20 without a Sprint Potion) or reach the previous room's cover before Rush arrives. This means you need to track where the last hiding spot was at all times — a skill the Hotel never required.

### DOORS The Mines Walkthrough — Room-by-Room From Your First Death to a Full Clear (2026)

**How many rooms are in The Mines?**

The Mines has 100 rooms, same as the Hotel. But the room types are completely different — open caverns with zero hiding spots, flooded chambers that slow movement 30%, minecart tracks, and Grumble nest rooms with multiple patrolling threats replace the Hotel's predictable corridors and closet-lined hallways. The difficulty curve is steeper: you'll encounter Grumble by Room 3 and open-cavern Rush by Room 6, compared to the Hotel where Rush doesn't appear until Room 9.

### The Mines Survival Protocol — What Kills Hotel Veterans & How to Adapt in Your First 10 Runs (2026)

**Why do I keep dying in the first 10 rooms of The Mines?**

Because you're still playing like you're in the Hotel. Three Hotel habits will kill you in The Mines: (1) assuming every room has a closet — 30% of Mine rooms are open caverns with zero hiding spots; (2) looking around every new room — Grumble tracks movement and hanging ceiling variants spot you before you spot them; (3) relying on Guiding Light for navigation — The Mines has fewer sparkles and expects you to navigate by environment. Break these three habits and your survival rate past room 10 jumps from ~20% to ~70%.

### DOORS The Mines Walkthrough — Every New Entity, Puzzle & Boss Encounter Organized by Threat (2026)

**How do I survive open-cavern rooms?**

Open caverns have zero hiding spots — no closets, no beds, no side rooms. When Rush screeches in one, you must sprint back to the previous room's closet. You have 3.5 seconds. This means tracking the last closet location at all times. As you leave every room with closets, mentally note its position. When you enter an open cavern, immediately ask: if Rush screeches now, do I know where to run? If the answer is no, you haven't been tracking. Sprint Potions guarantee survival — pop one the moment you hear Rush in an open cavern.
